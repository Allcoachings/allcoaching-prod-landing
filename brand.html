01 · Brand essence

Build your own LMS. In just a few clicks.

AllCoaching is an LMS for educators. Drag and drop your academy, make it your brand, and start selling, with a 7-day free trial. Every page should leave an educator feeling that this is simple, theirs, and serious.

Promise

Launch it yourself

Self-serve from sign-up to first sale. No developer, no sales call, no demo dependency.

Proof

Your brand, your money

Own domain on every plan, 0% commission on every monthly plan so you keep 100% of every sale, paid to your bank automatically.

Feeling

Light, calm, trustworthy

Lots of white space, one blue accent, real product screenshots. Never loud, never cheap.

02 · Logo & wordmark

The mark and the italic wordmark.

The round logo mark always sits left of "AllCoaching", set in Instrument Serif italic. The wordmark is the only place the serif appears in navigation.

05 · Voice

Plain, direct, on the educator's side.

Write like a founder talking to one educator: short sentences, real numbers, no hype. Lead with what they get, then how.

Do

  • Say educator and studio
  • Use real facts: 7 days free, 0% commission on monthly plans, 500+ educators
  • Name the pain, then the fix
  • Mark illustrative numbers as illustrative
  • End sections with one clear action

Don't

  • No exclamation marks or "#1 platform"
  • No "cheapest LMS", "free forever" or "7 days free to start"
  • No made-up stats or testimonials
  • No "creator", "user" or "dashboard"
  • No competitor prices without a source

07 · Graphics & imagery

Light rays and real product.

The signature graphic is a band of blue-to-cyan light rays under a soft white arc, with the real studio screenshot on top. Use real screenshots from the demo studio, never mock-ups that show features we don't have.

Real screenshots

Studio, website and student app from the demo account, at 2x. Light mode on light pages.

Gentle motion

Drifting blobs, floating cards, slow ray shimmer, fade-up on scroll. Always respect reduced motion.

Quotes in serif

Pull quotes sit on a soft blue band in Instrument Serif italic.

"A teacher who has to think about payment gateways has already stopped being a teacher."
08 · Before you publish

The ten-second brand check.

  • White or pastel background, one blue accent, navy footer
  • Headline mixes weight 200 and 700, with the payoff in bold
  • Wordmark in Instrument Serif italic next to the round mark
  • "Build your own LMS" message and the 7-day free trial are visible
  • One solid blue button per section, pointing to the free trial
  • Real product screenshot or real product moment, no invented features
  • Facts match pricing: monthly Starter ₹3,999, Growth ₹5,999 and Pro ₹7,999 at 0% commission; yearly Starter ₹6,999 (10% fee), Growth ₹12,999 (7.5%) and Pro ₹24,999 (5%), + GST; never claim 0% for the yearly plans
  • No exclamation marks, no "cheapest", no fabricated numbers
  • Works at 320px wide with no sideways scroll
