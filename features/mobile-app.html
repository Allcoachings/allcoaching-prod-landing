Your own app

Your students open your app, not ours.

Every AllCoaching plan includes your own branded Android app, and Growth and Pro add an iOS app after 30 days on the plan. Both are published on your own Google Play Console and App Store Connect accounts, so the app carries your name.

Plans

Android on every plan. iOS on Growth and Pro.

Every plan is the full studio, including your own website and Android app. Growth and Pro add your own iOS app after 30 days on the plan, along with more storage and live-room hours.

  • Monthly: Starter ₹3,999, Growth ₹5,999, Pro ₹7,999 a month + GST, 0% commission
  • Yearly: ₹6,999, ₹12,999 or ₹24,999 a year + GST, with a 10%, 7.5% or 5% fee per sale + GST
  • iOS app on Growth and Pro, after 30 days on the plan
  • Storage can be extended on any plan
Publishing

Your app, on your own console.

Apps are published only on your own accounts: Google Play Console for Android and App Store Connect for iOS. Google and Apple review each app, and the listing sits under your name.

  • Own Android app on Starter, Growth and Pro
  • Own iOS app on Growth and Pro, after 30 days
  • You give your Google Play Console and App Store Connect account
  • Free web address or your own domain alongside the app
Students

Your academy, in your students’ pocket.

On a phone, students browse your courses, open a course page and enrol, and take your test series, all under your logo and name. Courses, tests, library and profile sit in one bar at the bottom.

  • Courses with category filters
  • Course pages with price and Enroll now
  • Test series with free tests marked
  • Your logo and academy name at the top of every screen
How it works

Your own app, step by step.

The same steps as the studio tutorial at the top of this page.

  1. Start your free trialEvery plan starts with a 7-day free trial, no card needed.
  2. Pick monthly or yearlyIn Settings › Plan, press Change plan and compare Starter, Growth and Pro.

  4. Add iOS on Growth or ProAfter 30 days on Growth or Pro, your iOS app joins your Android app.
Questions

What educators ask about your own app.

Do I get my own Android app on every plan?

Yes. Every plan, monthly or yearly, includes your own branded Android app, published on your own Google Play Console account.

Which plans include an iOS app?

Growth and Pro, monthly or yearly, after 30 days on the plan. Starter does not include an iOS app.

Whose account is my app published on?

Always your own: Google Play Console for Android and App Store Connect for iOS. Google and Apple review every app before it goes live.

How much does an online coaching platform with my own app cost?

Monthly: Starter ₹3,999, Growth ₹5,999, Pro ₹7,999 a month + GST, 0% commission. Yearly: ₹6,999, ₹12,999, ₹24,999 a year + GST, with a 10%, 7.5% or 5% fee per sale + GST.

Monthly or yearly plan, which is better?

If you sell a lot, monthly (0% commission) works out better; with fewer sales, yearly can cost less. Start with the 7-day free trial, no card needed.
