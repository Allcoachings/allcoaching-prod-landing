Pricing & Plans

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan comes with a 7-day free trial, so you can set up your academy, add a course and see your website before you pay, and no card is needed to start. When the trial ends, pick Starter, Growth or Pro to continue.

Full details · Pricing page

How much does AllCoaching cost for an Indian educator in 2026?

AllCoaching has three plans (Starter, Growth, Pro), and you can pay for them two ways. Paid monthly, they cost ₹3,999, ₹5,999 and ₹7,999 a month with 0% commission, so you keep 100% of every sale. Paid yearly, they cost ₹6,999, ₹12,999 and ₹24,999 a year with a small fee only on what you sell (10%, 7.5% or 5%). Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, no card needed. There is no sales limit and no setup fee on any plan. Payouts reach your bank automatically. Prices exclude GST.

Full details · Pricing page

What does the platform fee include?

Monthly plans take 0% commission; only the yearly plans have a platform fee per sale (10% on Starter, 7.5% on Growth, 5% on Pro). Every plan covers the UPI and card checkout, secure video streaming with watermarking, DRM and piracy protection, refund processing, data storage, payout settlement, your marketplace listing and ongoing platform engineering. Payment-gateway charges on student payments are separate, at the gateway's rate; AllCoaching adds no markup on top.

Is there a setup fee or onboarding cost?

No setup fee. Unlike white-label app builders, which commonly charge a separate setup fee plus monthly software, AllCoaching has no setup fee on any plan — monthly plans with 0% commission from ₹3,999 a month, or yearly plans from ₹6,999 a year. The branded app, academy website, marketplace listing, payment infrastructure and onboarding are included in every plan; only the yearly plans add a small platform fee (10%, 7.5% or 5%) when you make a sale.

Are there any hidden fees?

No hidden fees. You pay the plan price: a monthly plan (₹3,999, ₹5,999 or ₹7,999 a month) with 0% commission, or a yearly plan (₹6,999, ₹12,999 or ₹24,999 a year) plus 10%, 7.5% or 5% only on what you sell. There is no setup fee, no per-student charge and no sales limit. Payment-gateway charges on student payments are separate, at the gateway's rate.

Is AllCoaching pricing inclusive of GST?

No. Plan prices and the platform fee are exclusive of GST, which is added at checkout and shown as a separate line on your invoice, so GST-registered educators can usually claim it as input tax credit.

What is AllCoaching's refund policy?

On a yearly plan, when a student is refunded, AllCoaching reverses its platform fee (10%, 7.5% or 5%) proportionally — a full refund reverses the full fee, a partial one reverses in proportion. Monthly plans take 0% commission, so there is no fee to reverse. Refunds follow the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020, within 30 days. Your earned payouts on completed sales settle automatically and are not affected by an unrelated refund.

Compliance & Tax

Is AllCoaching DPDP Act 2023 compliant?

AllCoaching is built with the DPDP Act 2023 in mind: consent for student data, data-retention controls, deletion on request and breach-notification procedures. As the educator you remain responsible for how you collect and use your own students' data. Our privacy policy has the details.

Does AllCoaching handle GST registration and filing for educators?

No. AllCoaching handles GST on its own revenue (plan fees + platform fee). Your own GST is yours to handle: you must register for your own GSTIN if your turnover crosses ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in NE states) and file your own returns. Your sales and payment records in the studio help your accountant.

What is SAC 999293 and why does it matter?

SAC 999293 is the Services Accounting Code for "commercial training and coaching services" under Indian GST law. Carries an 18% GST rate. School-level recognised education and degree institution coursework is exempt. Competitive exam coaching (NEET, JEE, UPSC, SSC, banking) typically falls under SAC 999293 and requires 18% GST collection above the turnover threshold.

Deep dive · Indian EdTech laws & regulations

Is AllCoaching IT Rules 2021 compliant?

Yes. AllCoaching has appointed a grievance officer, publishes contact details, acknowledges complaints within 48 hours, resolves them within the timelines the law prescribes, removes infringing content on valid notice, and retains records per the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules 2021. Educators on the marketplace inherit platform-level compliance.

Does AllCoaching comply with POCSO Act for minor students?

AllCoaching acts on reported misconduct, including removing content and suspending accounts, and cooperates with the authorities as the law requires. Educators teaching students under 18 remain responsible for their own conduct under the POCSO Act; good practice is to teach in group or recorded sessions and keep parents informed.

Are AllCoaching's tax invoices ITC-eligible for educators?

Usually, yes. AllCoaching issues GST tax invoices for plan fees and the platform fee, with the platform's GSTIN, the taxable value and the GST shown separately. GST-registered educators can usually claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) on that 18% GST; check with your accountant.

Payouts & Money

When do educator payouts happen?

Automatically. Net educator earnings are settled to the registered bank account through Razorpay, our payment partner. No monthly cycle, no minimum payout threshold, no manual withdrawal request. Settlement via NEFT/RTGS/IMPS depending on amount.

What is the minimum payout amount?

No minimum threshold. AllCoaching settles whatever the educator has earned — even if it is ₹50. Automatic settlement removes the lock-in pressure of monthly payout cycles and keeps cash flow predictable.

What happens if a student requests a refund? Does it affect my payout?

Refund handling follows Consumer Protection E-Commerce Rules 2020. If approved within the published refund window, the corresponding educator earning is reversed in the next settlement. Educators can set their own refund window within legal limits (typically 7–30 days).

Can I change my pricing after I've enrolled students?

Yes for new enrolments; existing enrolments keep the price they paid. You can adjust course pricing, add bundles, run discounts or restructure batches any time from the studio.

Security & Anti-piracy

What anti-piracy protection does AllCoaching include?

Watermarking, DRM and piracy protection come with every plan, monthly or yearly. Uploaded videos are streamed in your own branded player rather than handed out as files, and a watermark on the video discourages sharing. No system can stop every leak, so treat this as strong protection, not a guarantee.

Deep dive · 7-layer video security stack

Can students download my videos and re-sell them?

Not easily. Uploaded videos are streamed, not offered as downloadable files, and DRM makes direct downloading hard. No platform can fully stop a student filming the screen with a second phone; the watermark on the video discourages that and helps you act on a leak.

What about screen recording or screenshots?

Where the device supports DRM, protected video usually shows up black in screen recordings, especially in the app. Support differs between phones, browsers and operating systems, so treat DRM and watermarking as strong deterrents, not a guarantee.

Account & Data

Can I stop or leave AllCoaching anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans have no lock-in: stop any month, and your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for. A yearly plan runs to the end of its year. If you don't renew, the academy goes offline to students until you renew, and you have 30 days to export your student lists, course content and payment history; after that, uploaded videos are deleted.

Can I export my student list and data?

Yes. Educator data — student contacts, payment history, course content, engagement analytics — is exportable anytime in standard formats. AllCoaching follows data portability requirements under the DPDP Act 2023. You retain ownership of your educator brand, student relationships, and uploaded content.

Can multiple teachers share one AllCoaching account?

Yes — multi-teacher institute support is included on every plan. Coaching institutes with multiple faculty can run a unified educator profile with separate teacher logins, individual content management, batch ownership, and revenue handled by the institute. Useful for chains, partnerships, and institutes with multiple teachers. Your plan applies as it is (0% commission on monthly plans, 5–10% platform fee on yearly plans), with 3, 5 or 7 team seats on Starter, Growth or Pro — there is no separate per-teacher fee.
