Pricing & Plans

How much does AllCoaching cost for an Indian educator in 2026?

AllCoaching offers three tiers: (1) Free 30-day trial — full platform, no card required; (2) Early Launch Offer — ₹11,999/year + 10% per transaction, locked for 2 years, limited to first 50 educators; (3) Standard Plan — ₹24,999/year + 10% per transaction. Educators keep 90% of revenue with daily payouts. No setup fee, no SMS credits, no annual escalation.

Full details · Pricing page

What does the 10% transaction commission include?

All-inclusive: UPI/card/EMI/net-banking payment gateway, GST-compliant invoicing under SAC 999293, HLS+AES-128 secure video streaming with DRM, anti-piracy watermarking, refund processing under Consumer Protection Rules 2020, DPDP-compliant data storage, grievance officer infrastructure, daily payout settlement, marketplace AI recommendation, and ongoing platform engineering. No payment gateway markup is added on top.

Is there a setup fee or onboarding cost?

No. Unlike Classplus, Graphy, and Teachmint (which charge ₹25,000–₹2,00,000 setup plus monthly software), AllCoaching has zero setup cost. The annual subscription is the only fixed cost — branded app, marketplace listing, payment infrastructure, and onboarding support are all included.

What is the Early Launch Offer and how does it work?

Early Launch is ₹11,999/year locked for 2 years (₹23,998 total vs ₹49,998 on Standard tier — a 52% discount). Strictly limited to the first 50 educators. After 50 slots fill, future educators move to Standard. 10% transaction fee is the same across paid tiers.

Is AllCoaching pricing inclusive of GST?

Pricing is shown exclusive of GST. 18% GST is added at invoice (₹11,999 + 18% = ₹14,159 for Early Launch). The 10% transaction commission is also exclusive of GST. Invoices show GST as a separate line — ITC-eligible for GST-registered educators. AllCoaching handles full GST compliance under SAC 999293.

What is AllCoaching's refund policy?

Follows the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 — refunds within 30 days. For annual subscriptions, pro-rated refunds available within first 7 days. After 7 days, subscription continues for the year. Educator payouts are unaffected — earnings already accrued are paid out on the normal daily cycle.

Compliance & Tax

Is AllCoaching DPDP Act 2023 compliant?

Yes. Built DPDP-compliant from the ground up — explicit consent flows for student data, verifiable parental consent for minors under 18, data retention controls, deletion-on-request, and breach notification protocols. AllCoaching acts as data fiduciary for platform-level processing, reducing the educator's individual DPDP burden significantly.

Does AllCoaching handle GST registration and filing for educators?

AllCoaching handles GST on platform-level revenue (subscriptions + commission). For educators, AllCoaching auto-generates GST-compliant invoices to students under SAC 999293 and provides monthly statements + GSTR-1/GSTR-3B export-ready reports. Educator must register for own GSTIN if turnover crosses ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in NE states).

What is SAC 999293 and why does it matter?

SAC 999293 is the Services Accounting Code for "commercial training and coaching services" under Indian GST law. Carries an 18% GST rate. School-level recognised education and degree institution coursework is exempt. Competitive exam coaching (NEET, JEE, UPSC, SSC, banking) typically falls under SAC 999293 and requires 18% GST collection above the turnover threshold.

Deep dive · Indian EdTech laws & regulations

Is AllCoaching IT Rules 2021 compliant?

Yes. AllCoaching has appointed a grievance officer, publishes contact details, acknowledges complaints within 24 hours, resolves within 15 days, removes infringing content on valid notice, and retains records per the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules 2021. Educators on the marketplace inherit platform-level compliance.

Does AllCoaching comply with POCSO Act for minor students?

Yes. For educators teaching students under 18, AllCoaching enforces audited communication channels, parental visibility into 1-on-1 interactions, mandatory recording of teacher-student sessions, content moderation, and POCSO-defined incident protocols. Platform provides POCSO-compliant infrastructure; individual educators remain responsible for their own conduct.

Are AllCoaching's tax invoices ITC-eligible for educators?

Yes. AllCoaching issues GST-compliant tax invoices under SAC 999293 with the platform's GSTIN, taxable value, and GST line item separated. GST-registered educators can claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) on the 18% GST charged on subscription + commission.

Payouts & Money

When do educator payouts happen?

Daily. Net educator earnings (90% of student payment) are settled to the registered bank account every day on a T+1 basis. No monthly cycle, no minimum payout threshold, no manual withdrawal request. Settlement via NEFT/RTGS/IMPS depending on amount.

What is the minimum payout amount?

No minimum threshold. AllCoaching settles whatever the educator has earned that day — even if it is ₹50. Daily settlement removes lock-in pressure of monthly payout cycles and gives educators real-time cash flow visibility.

What happens if a student requests a refund? Does it affect my payout?

Refund handling follows Consumer Protection E-Commerce Rules 2020. If approved within the published refund window, the corresponding educator earning is reversed in the next daily settlement. Educators can set their own refund window within legal limits (typically 7–30 days). Most educators see refund rates under 3%.

Can I change my pricing after I've enrolled students?

Yes for new enrollments; existing enrollments retain the price they paid. Educators can adjust course pricing, add bundles, run discounts, or restructure batches anytime from the studio. Platform automatically grandfather-protects existing students at their enrollment price, required under Consumer Protection rules.

Security & Anti-piracy

What anti-piracy protection does AllCoaching include?

All 7 modern layers: HLS+AES-128 encryption with sub-10-second token URLs, hardware-backed DRM (Widevine L1 on Android, FairPlay on iOS, PlayReady on Windows), dynamic visible watermarking + forensic invisible watermarking per student session, device-binding with concurrent stream enforcement, geo-fencing with VPN detection, marketplace-scale Telegram/YouTube/Drive monitoring, and 24–48 hour DMCA takedown SLA.

Deep dive · 7-layer video security stack

Can students download my videos and re-sell them?

Technically very difficult. AllCoaching uses HLS+AES-128 with sub-10-second token rotation — direct download tools fail. Hardware-backed DRM prevents browser-level downloads. The only realistic vector is the "analog hole" — a student pointing a second phone camera at the screen. Forensic watermarking identifies which student session was captured, enabling targeted account suspension and legal action.

What about screen recording or screenshots?

On Android with Widevine L1, screen recording of DRM-protected video is blocked at the OS level. On iOS, FairPlay applies the same protection. Screen recording produces a black screen on protected content. Screenshots are blocked similarly. Web playback is restricted to short preview clips; full course content requires the native app.
