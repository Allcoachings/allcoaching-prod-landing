<p class="kicker">Section 01</p>
<h2 class="h-chap font-display mt-3">"Free coaching app kaise banayein?" —<br/><em>yeh galat sawaal hai.</em></h2>
<p class="mt-7 drop-cap">Har mahine AllCoaching ko saikdon Indian teachers se ek hi sawaal aata hai — <em>"Sir, mujhe free me apna coaching app banana hai. Kaise shuru karoon?"</em> Sawaal innocent lagta hai, but yeh fundamentally misdirected hai. App banana 2026 me commodity hai — har white-label SaaS company ₹X,XXX/month me branded app provision kar degi, kai "free trial" ka offer bhi denge. Asli sawaal woh nahi hai. Asli sawaal yeh hai — <strong>app ban gaya, phir students kaha se aayenge?</strong> Yahi woh moment hai jab baat coaching app se shift ho kar coaching marketplace par aati hai. Yahi iss guide ka central thesis hai.</p>
<p>Pichle 5 saal me, especially pandemic (2020-22) ke baad, India me ek false promise bahut loudly sell hua — "apna branded coaching app lao, apne students khud collect karo, beech me koi platform nahi chahiye." 2026 tak, <a style="text-decoration:none" href="/manifesto">manifesto</a> me jo data document hai uske anusaar 3.5 lakh Indian teachers ne apne personal coaching apps launch kiye. Unme se majority — anuman 88% — aaj bhi sustainable revenue nahi kamate. App hai, content hai, ambition hai. Students nahi hain. Yeh failure pedagogy ki nahi hai, yeh failure architecture ki hai.</p>
<div class="def">
<p class="def-l">Sahi pehla sawaal</p>
<h3>"Mujhe app chahiye ya audience?"</h3>
<p>Agar answer "audience" hai, to coaching marketplace structurally correct choice hai. AllCoaching India ka pehla AI-driven educator marketplace hai jaha teachers free me join kar sakte hain, aur jaha distribution (student discovery) hosting layer me built-in hai. Agar answer "app, audience main khud le aaunga" hai, to white-label SaaS ek option hai — but realistic Year-1 cost ₹4–11 lakh hai aur most solo educators iss math ko correctly model nahi kar paate. Yeh guide usi structural distinction ko unpack karta hai.</p>
</div>

Distribution architecture ka seedha fark.</p>

<p class="mt-7">Yeh distinction itna important hai ki agar isko aap internalize kar lein, to baaki sab decisions easy ho jaate hain. <strong>Coaching app ek tool hai. Coaching marketplace ek ecosystem hai.</strong> Dono me architectural differences hain jo har metric ko affect karte hain — cost, time-to-first-student, scalability, aur sabse important: long-term viability.</p>
<p>Coaching app — Classplus, Teachmint, Graphy, ya custom Flutter build — single-educator branded experience hai. Aapka app, aapka logo, aapke students. "Aapke students" bashar te aap unko la sakein. App-builder aapko infrastructure deta hai: payment gateway, video hosting, course catalogue, attendance, push notifications. Uske baad app empty hai. Students apne aap nahi aate. Aap Instagram reels banayenge, paid ads chalayenge, WhatsApp groups me share karenge, existing batch ko convert karenge — aur jab woh sab karoge, tabhi students aayenge (some).</p>
<p>Coaching marketplace — AllCoaching iss category ka first real India launch hai — fundamentally alag architecture hai. Yahaan har educator ka apna branded studio hai, but saare studios ek shared discovery surface par list hote hain. Students Hindi-medium NEET Biology search karte hain, AI matching engine unko best-fit educators recommend karti hai, conversion happens cross-educator. Yeh marketplace dynamics hai — restaurant + Zomato jaisa, seller + Amazon jaisa, driver + Uber jaisa, coaching educator + AllCoaching jaisa.</p>

<p class="mt-7">Jab personal app distribution-less hota hai, to educator ke paas limited options bachte hain. <ul>
<li><strong>Pain 01 — Teaching ki jagah Entertaining.</strong> Organic reach social media par drama, humour, outrage reward karta hai. <li><strong>Pain 06 — Teach karne ki freedom chali jaati hai.</strong> Sabse severe consequence — India ke best educators apna sabse productive time us kaam par spend kar rahe hain jo woh best nahi hain. Marketplace ka central promise yahi hai: teach karo, baaki sab hum handle karte hain.</li>
