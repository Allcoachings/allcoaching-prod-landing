<h2 class="h-chap font-display mt-3">What "student traffic" actually means —<br/><em>and where it comes from.</em></h2>
<p class="mt-7">Student traffic is people who could buy your course arriving at your academy. It is not the same as page visits: a visit only matters if the person is looking for what you teach, in the language you teach it, at a price they can pay.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Your existing audience</strong> — past students, parents, WhatsApp groups, your offline batch. Usually the first paying students.</li>
<li><strong>YouTube and Instagram</strong> — free lessons build trust; the link in the description sends viewers to your academy.</li>
<li><strong>Google search</strong> — pages on your own domain that answer a specific search can rank over time; it is slow work.</li>
<li><strong>Marketplace listings</strong> — places where students already browse, such as the AllCoaching marketplace app. An extra source, rarely the main one.</li>
<li><strong>Paid ads</strong> — optional; test them once you know which course converts.</li>
</ul>
