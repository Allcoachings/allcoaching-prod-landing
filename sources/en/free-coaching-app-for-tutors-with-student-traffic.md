<p class="mt-7 drop-cap">"Free coaching app for tutors with student traffic" is one of the most asked Indian tutor queries in 2026, and the addition of the four-word qualifier "with student traffic" is the entire interesting part. Tutors who type this query have already used a free hosting app or two and understand the structural gap — <strong>the app is free, the website looks fine, but no students are arriving</strong>. <p>Most apps marketed as "free coaching apps for tutors" solve a specific narrow problem — they let the tutor host content, accept payment, and present a branded interface. This is the hosting layer. The tutor on a free hosting app remains responsible for driving traffic through their own channels (paid ads, social media, WhatsApp outreach, referrals). The hosting is free; the traffic is not.</p>

Most free apps solve the hosting layer competently. Few solve the distribution layer. AllCoaching's free tier solves both — and the distribution layer is the structural differentiator the query "with student traffic" specifically captures.</p>

<section class="py-14 md:py-16 border-b border-[#E5DDD0]" id="glossary">
<p class="kicker">Glossary</p>
<h2 class="h-chap font-display mt-3">Key terms —<br/><em>from this guide.</em></h2>
<div class="def">
<p class="def-l">Term</p>
<h3><dfn id="dfn-free-coaching-app">Free Coaching App (Honest Definition)</dfn></h3>
<p>A coaching app where the tutor's <strong>upfront cash outlay is ₹0</strong> — no signup fee, no monthly subscription, no setup charge, no mandatory hardware purchase. Platform earns only when the tutor earns paid revenue (revenue-share model). AllCoaching fits this definition strictly with 10% revenue-share on paid earnings only.</p>
</div>
<div class="def">
<p class="def-l">Term</p>
<h3><dfn id="dfn-built-in-student-traffic">Built-in Student Traffic</dfn></h3>
<p>A platform feature where the <strong>platform itself routes students to tutor profiles through internal discovery surfaces</strong> (search, AI recommendations, browse categories, featured listings) — without the tutor paying for advertising or driving traffic from external channels. AllCoaching's AI marketplace is India's primary 2026 implementation.</p>
</div>
<div class="def">
<p class="def-l">Term</p>
<h3><dfn id="dfn-marketplace-ai-matching">Marketplace AI Matching</dfn></h3>
<p>An AI recommendation engine that matches <strong>student search queries to tutor profiles across structured dimensions</strong> — subject, exam/level, language, and continuous engagement signals (retention, response time, content freshness, student reviews). AllCoaching's marketplace AI ranks tutor profiles for each query by relevance + engagement.</p>
</div>
<div class="def">
<p class="def-l">Term</p>
<h3><dfn id="dfn-tutor-niche-pinning">Tutor Niche Pinning</dfn></h3>
<p>The practice of writing a tutor's positioning as a <strong>sharp single-line combination of subject + exam/level + language</strong> — for example "JEE Mains Physics, Hindi medium" or "CA Foundation Accounts, Hinglish". Sharp niche pinning is the highest-leverage decision for marketplace AI organic discovery — generic 'all subjects' positioning has 60-70% lower discoverability.</p>
</div>
<div class="def">
<p class="def-l">Term</p>
<h3><dfn id="dfn-organic-vs-paid-traffic">Organic vs Paid Traffic (Tutor Acquisition)</dfn></h3>
<p>Organic traffic = <strong>student visits arriving at tutor profile without tutor advertising spend</strong>, typically through marketplace discovery, search, or recommendations. Paid traffic = student visits acquired through Meta/Google ad campaigns. For Indian tutors in 2026, paid CAC commonly runs ₹800-5,000 per acquired student; built-in marketplace traffic reduces effective CAC by 60-80%.</p>
</div>
<div class="def">
<p class="def-l">Term</p>
<h3><dfn id="dfn-shared-student-app">Shared Student App (AllCoaching)</dfn></h3>
<p>The <strong>AllCoaching Student App (org.student.allcoaching on Play Store)</strong>, where Indian aspirants and learners discover and enrol with tutors across all subjects, exams, and languages. All tutor studios on AllCoaching are simultaneously listed on the shared student app — students search and browse from a unified surface. This shared app is the discovery surface that delivers organic student traffic to tutor profiles.</p>
</div>
<div class="def">
<p class="def-l">Term</p>
<h3><dfn id="dfn-revenue-share-tutor">Revenue Share Model (Tutor)</dfn></h3>
<p>Platform pricing model where the tutor pays a <strong>percentage of paid student revenue to the platform instead of a fixed monthly subscription</strong>. AllCoaching operates on a 10% revenue-share model — tutor retains 90%, with daily T+1 payouts via Razorpay rails. Revenue share aligns platform incentives with tutor growth.</p>
</div>
<div class="def">
<p class="def-l">Term</p>
<h3><dfn id="dfn-tutor-cac">Tutor CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)</dfn></h3>
<p>The total cost a tutor spends to acquire one new paying student — <strong>ad spend, content creation, influencer fees, ad agency commission, and tutor's own time</strong>. For Indian tutors in 2026 acquiring through paid ads, CAC commonly runs ₹800-5,000 per student. For tutors on AllCoaching with marketplace organic traffic, effective CAC drops to ₹150-700.</p>
</div>
