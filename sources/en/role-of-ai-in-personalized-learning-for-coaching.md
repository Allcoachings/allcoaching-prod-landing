<p class="kicker">Section 01</p>
<h2 class="h-chap font-display mt-3">Personalised learning is a loop —<br/><em>not a content library.</em></h2>
<p class="mt-7 drop-cap">A bigger content library does not personalise anything. Personalisation happens when a teacher knows what each student does not understand and changes what that student does next. In a batch of 50 that is hard by hand, which is where AI tools can help.</p><p>The loop has four steps: diagnose, assign practice, re-check, intervene. Every AI feature worth paying for should make one of these steps faster or more accurate.</p>
</section>
<div class="orn">· · ·</div>

<!-- ============ SECTION 02 — The six layers ============ -->
<section class="py-14 md:py-16 border-b border-[#E6E8F0]" id="ch2">
<p class="kicker">Section 02</p>
<h2 class="h-chap font-display mt-3">Six places AI can help —<br/><em>mapped to the loop.</em></h2>
<ol class="mt-7"><li><strong>Diagnostic tests</strong> — short chapter tests that show weak topics.</li><li><strong>Practice selection</strong> — choosing the next set of questions for each student.</li><li><strong>Question drafting</strong> — drafting MCQs and explanations from your notes, with your review.</li><li><strong>Doubt help</strong> — first-pass explanations, checked by a teacher.</li><li><strong>Progress signals</strong> — flags from attendance and test scores for students who are slipping.</li><li><strong>Nudges</strong> — reminders to students or parents at the right moment.</li></ol>
</section>
<div class="orn">· · ·</div>

<!-- ============ SECTION 03 — Comparison table ============ -->
<section class="py-14 md:py-16 border-b border-[#E6E8F0]" id="ch3">
<p class="kicker">Section 03</p>
<h2 class="h-chap font-display mt-3">What works today —<br/><em>and what is still early.</em></h2>
<p class="mt-7"><strong>Works today (with a human check):</strong> AI drafting of questions and explanations in tools such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini; chapter-wise test analysis; simple at-risk flags from attendance and scores.</p><p><strong>Early:</strong> fully adaptive learning paths, AI tutoring in regional languages, and predictions of exam rank. Ask any vendor for a live demo on your own chapter before paying for these.</p>
</section>
<div class="orn">· · ·</div>

<!-- ============ SECTION 04 — Diagnostic deep dive ============ -->
<section class="py-14 md:py-16 border-b border-[#E6E8F0]" id="ch4">
<p class="kicker">Section 04</p>
<h2 class="h-chap font-display mt-3">Diagnosis —<br/><em>the layer everything else depends on.</em></h2>
<p class="mt-7">Without a good diagnosis, personalisation guesses. Start with one short test per chapter, tagged by topic. Look at which topics most students miss, and which students miss many. That alone tells you who needs what next. AI can help draft the test and summarise results; the tagging and the judgement are yours.</p>
</section>
<div class="orn">· · ·</div>

<!-- ============ SECTION 05 — Intervention loop economics ============ -->
<section class="py-14 md:py-16 border-b border-[#E6E8F0]" id="ch5">
<p class="kicker">Section 05</p>
<h2 class="h-chap font-display mt-3">Interventions —<br/><em>where personalisation pays back.</em></h2>
<p class="mt-7">A flag that nobody acts on changes nothing. Decide in advance what each flag triggers: a short revision video, a re-test, a message to the student, a call to the parent. Keep courses, tests and live classes in one place so you can see the signal and act on it quickly.</p>
</section>
<div class="orn">· · ·</div>

<!-- ============ SECTION 06 — What does NOT work ============ -->
<section class="py-14 md:py-16 border-b border-[#E6E8F0]" id="ch6">
<p class="kicker">Section 06</p>
<h2 class="h-chap font-display mt-3">What AI personalisation is not —<br/><em>three honest limits.</em></h2>
<ul class="mt-7"><li><strong>Not a substitute for teaching.</strong> Students still need clear explanations and someone who notices.</li><li><strong>Not reliable without review.</strong> AI drafts can be wrong; every question and answer needs a check.</li><li><strong>Not a student-acquisition tool.</strong> It improves results for students you have; it does not find new ones.</li></ul>
</section>
<div class="orn">· · ·</div>

<section class="py-14 md:py-16 border-b border-[#E6E8F0]" id="ch7">
<p class="kicker">Section 07</p>
<h2 class="h-chap font-display mt-3">Adopt now<br/><em>or wait?</em></h2>
<ul class="mt-7"><li><strong>Adopt now</strong> the mature parts: AI-assisted drafting with review, chapter tests and simple flags.</li><li><strong>Wait or pilot</strong> the early parts: adaptive paths and AI tutoring — test on your own content.</li><li><strong>Protect student data:</strong> know where it is stored and who can see it, especially for minors.</li></ul>
</section>
