    <section id="early-stage-risks">
      <h2>What early-stage educators actually risk when choosing an LMS</h2>
      <p>The biggest risk in your first year is not overpaying by a few thousand rupees. It is paying for a year of software before you know anyone will buy, or building on something you will have to leave once students arrive.</p>
      <h3>1. Paying before demand exists</h3>
      <p>A ₹29,999 yearly plan is a large bet when you have not sold a course yet. A trial, a no-upfront-fee plan or a small annual plan keeps that bet small.</p>
      <h3>2. A low start that gets expensive later</h3>
      <p>A plan with no upfront fee is the lowest-cost start. But if you later need your own domain or app, add-ons can cost more in a year than a plan that includes them. Check the add-on prices on day one, not after your first batch.</p>
      <h3>3. Starting on something you will have to leave</h3>
      <p>Moving students later is work. If you think you will want your own website and app within a year, it is easier to start there than to move a running batch.</p>
      <div class="v3-q">
        <p class="l">Question educators ask</p>
        <h3>Can I start with only YouTube, WhatsApp and UPI?</h3>
        <p>Yes, and many educators sell their first batch that way. It costs nothing beyond your phone. It gets hard once you are checking payment screenshots and adding students by hand for 30 or more people, which is usually the moment to move to one platform.</p>
