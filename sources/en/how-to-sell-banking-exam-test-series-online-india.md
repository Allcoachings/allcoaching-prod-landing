<p class="mt-7">Write questions from the current pattern and previous-year papers, in the difficulty mix the exam actually uses. Never copy questions from another publisher’s paid series. A realistic first release is 10 to 15 full mocks plus sectional tests, with more added on a published schedule.</p>
