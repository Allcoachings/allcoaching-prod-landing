Shuru karne se pehle

Educators ke sabse common sawaal.

Kya sach me 14 din free hai?

Haan. Sign up kijiye, apni academy banaiye aur course daaliye — 14 din tak kuch pay nahi karna, aur card bhi nahi chahiye. Uske baad aage chalana ho to Starter, Growth ya Pro chuniye.

Mujhe technical knowledge nahi hai. Kya main bana paunga?

Haan. Sab drag and drop se hota hai. Agar aap WhatsApp chala lete hain, to apni academy bhi bana lenge. Kahin atak gaye to WhatsApp par message kijiye.

Kya mobile se sign up kar sakte hain?

Haan. Apna mobile number daaliye aur OTP se verify kijiye. Google se bhi sign in ho sakta hai, lekin Instagram ya Facebook ke andar se khola ho to mobile number wala tareeka sabse aasaan hai.

Students ko mera naam dikhega ya AllCoaching ka?

Aapka. Aapki academy ka naam, logo aur colours har jagah — website, Google Play par aapka app, aur receipts. Hum peeche se infrastructure chalate hain.

14 din ke baad kitna lagta hai?

Starter ₹6,999 saal ka hai (GST alag), aur har sale par 10% platform fee. Growth ₹12,999 me fee 7.5% aur Pro ₹24,999 me sirf 5% hai. Koi setup fee nahi.

Paisa kab aur kaise milta hai?

Har sale ke 3 din baad (T+3) aapka hissa seedha aapke bank account me aa jaata hai. Students UPI ya card se pay karte hain.

Apne domain ke liye alag paisa lagta hai?

Domain connect karna har plan me shamil hai. Sirf domain naam aap khud khareedte hain, lagbhag ₹600–1,200 saal, GoDaddy ya Hostinger jaisi jagah se, apne naam par.

Agar renew nahi kiya to kya hoga?

Saal khatam hone par academy students ke liye offline ho jaati hai. Aapka content aur student data export ke liye hamesha available rehta hai, aur renew karte hi academy wapas live ho jaati hai.
