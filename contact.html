Before you write

Quick answers.

Do I need a demo or a sales call to start?

No. You can start the 7-day free trial yourself, with no card needed, build your academy and then choose a plan. Talk to us only if you want to.

Where can I see AllCoaching prices?

All plans are published on the pricing page. Monthly plans take 0% commission: Starter ₹3,999, Growth ₹5,999 and Pro ₹7,999 a month. Yearly plans are Starter ₹6,999, Growth ₹12,999 and Pro ₹24,999 a year with a 10%, 7.5% or 5% fee on sales. Prices exclude GST.

I run a large academy. Can we discuss a plan?

Yes. If you have many teachers, more than one brand or very high monthly sales, use the Contact us button on this page and we will set up a plan around your academy.

How quickly will you reply?

WhatsApp is quickest during working hours, Monday to Saturday, 10am to 7pm IST. Emails get a reply within 24 hours.

I am a student. Who should I contact?

For access to a course you bought, contact your educator first, since they manage their own academy. For questions about the AllCoaching app, email contact@allcoaching.in.
