Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 April 2026

AllCoaching ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and platform (the "Services"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email, phone number, profile details provided when you register as an educator, student, or parent.
  • Transaction data: payment records, invoices, payouts, and GST details where applicable (processed via regulated payment partners; full card data is never stored by us).
  • Content & usage: courses, batches, recordings, messages, classes attended, assignments, device and log data, cookies, and analytics events.
  • Support communications: messages you send us on WhatsApp, email, or inside the platform.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate and improve the Services — authentication, class delivery, payouts, analytics, fraud prevention.
  • To help students discover educators through the marketplace.
  • To send transactional updates (enrollments, payouts, classes) and occasional product announcements.
  • To comply with legal obligations including tax and anti-fraud regulations in India.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with:

  • Service providers (hosting, payments, analytics, messaging) under confidentiality agreements.
  • Authorities when required by Indian law or a valid legal process.
  • Educators — only the minimum information needed for a student to enroll in or attend their batches.

4. Data retention

We retain data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

5. Security

We use industry-standard measures — encrypted transport (HTTPS), access controls, and regular reviews — to protect your data. No system is 100% secure, so please use a strong password and inform us immediately if you suspect any compromise.

6. Your rights

You may access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data by writing to us at allcoachingss@gmail.com. Some data may be retained for legal or accounting reasons.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and analytics cookies to understand product usage. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser without losing core functionality.

8. Children

The Services are intended for users aged 13 and above. Where a learner is below the age of majority, a parent or guardian is responsible for the account.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified on this page or by email.
