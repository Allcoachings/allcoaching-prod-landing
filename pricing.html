Pricing questions, answered

Frequently asked about cost.

How much does AllCoaching cost for an Indian educator in 2026?

AllCoaching is free to start and free forever. There is no subscription, no setup fee, and no card or KYC required to sign up — you launch your branded coaching app and start in about a minute. The only charge is a single flat 10% platform fee on what you actually sell; you keep 90%, paid out daily in INR. There are no tiers, no per-message credits, and no annual price escalation, because there is no annual price.

Is there really no subscription or setup fee?

Correct — there is no subscription and no setup fee, ever. You do not pay anything to start, to host your courses, or to be listed on the marketplace. The platform earns only a flat 10% when you make a paid sale, which means in any month you do not sell, you pay nothing at all.

Do I need a card, KYC, or GST details to sign up?

No. You can sign up with phone OTP, Google, or email and land straight in the dashboard to create and publish a course — no card, no KYC, no GST or bank details at signup. Bank and KYC details are requested only later, at the exact moment you set up a paid course or request your first payout, so your money is secured when it actually moves.

What does the 10% platform fee include?

The flat 10% covers payment processing (UPI, cards, EMI, net-banking), GST-compliant invoicing, secure video streaming with anti-piracy protection, refund handling, DPDP-aligned data storage, daily payout settlement, AI-driven marketplace discovery, and ongoing platform engineering. It is a single inclusive number charged only on paid sales — there is no separate payment-gateway markup stacked on top.

If it is free, how does AllCoaching make money?

Only from the flat 10% on what you actually sell — nothing else. That is the point: because there is no subscription, the platform earns only when an educator earns, so its incentive is aligned with yours. If you have a quiet month, the platform earns nothing from you that month; it has to keep helping you sell to be paid at all.

Are there limits on storage, bandwidth, or live classes?

There is generous free storage and fair-use bandwidth included in the free plan, and live streaming is metered pay-per-use — you are only charged for the live viewer-hours you actually stream. This keeps the platform sustainable without a subscription, and it means you never pay for capacity you are not using. Usage meters are visible in your dashboard, so there are no surprises.

Is the 10% inclusive of GST?

The 10% is the platform fee; GST applies on that fee per Indian tax law and is shown as a separate, ITC-eligible line on your invoice, so you can claim Input Tax Credit. Because there is no subscription, there is no annual subscription GST to pay — GST applies only to the platform's service fee on your sales. AllCoaching handles the GST-compliant invoicing for the platform-level revenue.

What happens to the 10% fee on a refund?

If a student is refunded, the platform fee is reversed proportionally — a full refund reverses the full 10%, and a partial refund reverses it in proportion. Failed payments and chargebacks carry no fee. Your earned payouts on completed sales settle on the normal daily cycle and are not affected by an unrelated refund.

How does AllCoaching pricing compare to Classplus, Graphy, or Teachmint?

White-label vendors like Classplus, Graphy and Teachmint typically charge ₹2,500–₹1,25,000 per month in software fees (₹30K–₹15L/year), plus setup of ₹25K–₹2L, plus a transaction commission and per-message credits, plus 15–25% annual escalation — a real Year 1 cost of ₹4–11 lakh before you have sold anything. AllCoaching has none of that: ₹0 to start and a flat 10% only on what you sell. For a ₹10 lakh-revenue educator, that is about ₹1 lakh in total versus ₹4–7 lakh on white-label.

Can I export my data and leave anytime?

Yes. Your student list, course content, payment history and analytics are exportable at any time in standard formats, in line with data-portability rights under the DPDP Act 2023. Nothing is locked — you keep ownership of your brand, your students and your content. The only thing you give up by leaving is the marketplace traffic the platform was sending you.
