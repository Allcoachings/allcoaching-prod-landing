Pricing questions, answered

Frequently asked about cost.

How much does AllCoaching cost for an Indian educator in 2026?

AllCoaching offers three tiers: (1) Free 30-day trial — full platform access, no card required; (2) Early Launch Offer — ₹11,999/year + 10% per transaction, locked for 2 years, limited to the first 50 educators only; (3) Standard Plan — ₹24,999/year + 10% per transaction. Educators keep 90% of revenue with daily payouts. There is no setup fee, no per-message credits, no annual price escalation, and no hidden transaction stacking.

What does the 10% transaction commission include?

The 10% per-transaction fee covers: payment gateway costs (UPI, cards, EMI, net-banking), GST-compliant invoicing automation under SAC 999293, secure video streaming with HLS+AES-128 + DRM, anti-piracy watermarking, refund processing under Consumer Protection E-Commerce Rules 2020, DPDP-compliant data storage, grievance officer infrastructure, daily payout settlement, marketplace AI recommendation, and ongoing platform engineering. It is a single inclusive number — no payment gateway markup is added on top.

Is there a setup fee or onboarding cost on AllCoaching?

No. Unlike white-label coaching app vendors who typically charge ₹25,000–₹2,00,000 in setup fees plus monthly software licenses, AllCoaching has zero setup cost. The annual subscription (₹11,999 or ₹24,999) is the only fixed cost. Branded coaching app, marketplace listing, payment infrastructure, and onboarding support are all included in the annual fee.

How does the Early Launch Offer work?

The Early Launch Offer is ₹11,999/year locked for 2 years (₹23,998 total over 2 years vs ₹49,998 on Standard tier — a 52% discount). It is strictly limited to the first 50 educators who commit. After 50 slots fill, no new Early Launch enrolments are accepted; future educators move to the ₹24,999/year Standard tier. The 10% transaction fee is the same across all paid tiers.

What happens after the 30-day free trial ends?

During the trial you have full access to live classes, branded coaching app, marketplace listing, and payment infrastructure with no card on file. After 30 days, if you have not enrolled into a paid tier, the platform pauses outbound payments and limits new student enrolments — but no data is deleted. You can resume any time by enrolling in Early Launch (if slots remain) or Standard. There is no automatic charge.

Is AllCoaching pricing inclusive of GST?

AllCoaching's subscription pricing is shown exclusive of GST. 18% GST is added at the time of invoice (₹11,999 + 18% GST = ₹14,159 total for Early Launch). The 10% transaction commission is also exclusive of GST — invoices show the GST line item separately for educator ITC (Input Tax Credit) purposes. AllCoaching handles full GST compliance under SAC 999293 for the platform-level revenue.

What is the refund policy for AllCoaching subscriptions?

AllCoaching follows the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 — refunds are processed within 30 days of request. For annual subscriptions, pro-rated refunds are available for cancellations within the first 7 days. After 7 days, the subscription continues for the year. Educator payouts (the 90% revenue share) are not affected by subscription cancellation — earnings already accrued are paid out on the normal daily payout cycle.

How does AllCoaching pricing compare to Classplus, Graphy, or Teachmint?

White-label vendors like Classplus, Graphy, and Teachmint typically charge ₹2,500–₹1,25,000 per month in software fees plus 1.5–4% transaction commission on top of payment gateway costs (0.3–1%), plus setup ₹25K–₹2L, plus per-message credits ₹2K–₹8K/month, plus 15–25% annual escalation. Real Year 1 cost for a typical institute on white-label: ₹4–11 lakh. On AllCoaching Early Launch: ₹11,999 fixed + 10% of revenue. For a ₹10 lakh annual revenue educator, AllCoaching costs ~₹1.32 lakh total vs ₹4–7 lakh on white-label — a 3–5× cost difference. Full breakdown in our white-label cost analysis.

Does AllCoaching commission decrease as revenue grows?

The 10% per-transaction commission is flat across all revenue brackets — there are no volume discounts because the marketplace value (AI discovery, branded space, payment infrastructure) compounds, not diminishes, with educator scale. Top educators on AllCoaching often find that the 10% they pay is significantly less than the 30–40% of revenue they would otherwise spend on marketing, SEO retainers, payment processing markups, and content distribution combined.

Can I cancel my AllCoaching subscription and migrate my student list elsewhere?

Yes. Educator data — including student lists, course content, payment history, and analytics — is exportable at any time in standard CSV/PDF formats. AllCoaching follows data portability requirements under the DPDP Act 2023. Cancellation does not lock data; you retain ownership of your educator brand, your student relationships, and all content you uploaded. The platform-level marketplace traffic you no longer receive is the only thing you give up by leaving.
