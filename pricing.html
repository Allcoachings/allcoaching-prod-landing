How to buy

From sign-up to your plan, in four steps.

AllCoaching Technology runs a SaaS LMS platform. Educators pay a monthly or yearly subscription to use the studio. When students buy an educator's course, payment is collected through our gateway and settled to the educator after the platform fee (0% on monthly plans; 10%, 7.5% or 5% on yearly plans).

1. Sign up

Go to studio.allcoaching.in and create your account.

2. Try free for 7 days

Your trial starts at once. No card is needed.

3. Choose a plan and pay

In the studio, pick Starter, Growth or Pro, monthly or yearly, and pay through the secure checkout (UPI, cards, net banking). GST is added at checkout.

4. Plan active

Your plan is active on your account as soon as the payment is confirmed. A GST tax invoice is issued.

Transparent by design

The fine print, in plain words.

7-day free trial

Try your academy free for 7 days on any plan. Pick a plan when you are ready to continue.

GST and charges

Prices exclude GST, which is added at checkout, and the yearly platform fee is plus GST. Payment-gateway charges on student payments are separate and always shown. Payouts follow Razorpay settlement, usually T+2 days, and instant settlement is available.

Monthly plans

Billed every month with 0% commission. No lock-in: stop any month, and your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for.

No sales limit

No plan caps what you sell, monthly or yearly. Sell as much as you can.

Upgrades

Take effect the moment payment clears. What you already paid is credited.

Downgrades

Contact support and the lower plan applies from your next renewal.

Refunds

Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, so plan payments are non-refundable. Duplicate or failed payments are refunded. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Delivery

Online software access only, active on your studio account right after payment. Nothing is shipped. See the Delivery Policy.

If you don't renew

The academy goes offline to students; you then have 30 days to export your content and data, after which uploaded videos are deleted.

Questions

Asked before choosing a plan.

How much does AllCoaching cost for an Indian educator in 2026?

There are two ways to pay. Monthly plans take 0% commission, so you keep 100% of every sale: Starter is ₹3,999, Growth ₹5,999 and Pro ₹7,999 a month. Yearly plans have a lower fixed fee and a small fee per sale: Starter ₹6,999 a year at 10%, Growth ₹12,999 at 7.5% and Pro ₹24,999 at 5%. No plan has a sales limit. The yearly fee is plus GST. Every plan includes unlimited YouTube video and live classes and your own Android app; your own iOS app comes with Growth and Pro, after 30 days on the plan. Prices exclude GST, and every plan starts with a 7-day free trial.

Is there a zero-commission plan?

Yes. All three monthly plans take 0% commission, so you keep 100% of every sale: Starter at ₹3,999, Growth at ₹5,999 and Pro at ₹7,999 a month, plus GST, with no sales limit. Payment-gateway charges on student payments are separate.

Is there a limit on how much I can sell?

No. None of the plans, monthly or yearly, has a sales limit. What changes between plans is video storage and streaming, video quality, built-in live classroom hours, team seats and landing pages.

Monthly or yearly: which suits me?

It depends on how much you sell. Below about ₹4 lakh a year, yearly Starter at ₹6,999 plus a 10% fee usually suits you. Above that, a monthly plan with 0% commission usually leaves more in your pocket. The plan finder on this page works it out for your sales.

Who publishes my app on the Play Store and App Store?

Your app is published on your own Google Play Console and App Store Connect account, so it carries your name and stays yours. Google and Apple review every app, and their developer-account fees are paid to them.

How does AllCoaching work?

It works the way Shopify works for an online store, built for coaching: you sign up, choose a plan, upload your course, connect your domain and start selling. Payments, course access, your apps and hosting then run automatically. There is no developer, sales call or demo involved.

Do I get my own Android and iOS app?

Your own white-label Android app comes with every plan, in your name, logo and colours, alongside your website. Your own iOS app comes with Growth and Pro, monthly or yearly, after 30 days on the plan; it is not part of Starter. Both apps are published on your own Google Play Console and App Store Connect account.

Which plugins and marketing tools are included?

Every plan includes plugins for Zoom, Razorpay and YouTube, with more being added, plus Meta Pixel and Google Tag Manager so you can track sign-ups and run ads to your academy. You switch them on from the studio.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan comes with a 7-day free trial, so you can set up your academy, add a course and see your website before you pay, and no card is needed to start. When the trial ends, pick a monthly plan at 0% commission or a yearly plan to continue.

Which plan should I start with?

If you are just starting and expect to sell less than about ₹4 lakh a year, yearly Starter at ₹6,999 usually suits you. If you already sell, or want to keep 100% of every sale, start with monthly Starter at ₹3,999 and move up as your sales grow. The plan finder on this page shows which plan fits your sales.

Is my own domain included on Starter?

Yes. Every plan, including Starter, lets you connect your own domain from the studio. You buy the domain name from any registrar, usually about ₹600 to ₹1,200 a year, so it stays in your name. Until you connect one, your academy runs on a free allcoaching.in address.

Do premium themes come with a plan?

No plan includes a premium theme. The Classic theme is free on every plan; it is fully customizable, includes course pages, faculty profiles and an enquiry form, and works with your own domain. Premium themes are ₹10,000 each, paid once, and stay yours for life.

Do I need a developer or a sales call to set up?

No. You sign up, choose a plan, upload a course, connect your domain, set up payment and publish, all by yourself from the studio. There is no sales call, no demo dependency and no technical team required.

Where will students find and use my courses?

Students can buy from your own website and study in your own white-label Android app (and iOS app on Growth and Pro, after 30 days), or on the web. Your courses can also be discovered on the AllCoaching marketplace by students searching for your exam or subject.

What happens if I upgrade, downgrade or don't renew?

An upgrade takes effect as soon as payment clears, and what you already paid is credited. On a monthly plan you can stop any month, and the plan runs to the end of the month you paid for. To move a yearly plan to a lower one, contact support and it applies from your next renewal. If you don't renew, your academy goes offline to students; you then have 30 days to export your content and data, after which uploaded videos are deleted.

Is marketplace discovery included on every plan?

Yes. Every plan lists your courses in the AllCoaching app, where students search by exam, subject, language and city. It is not an upgrade. It is extra discovery on top of your own marketing, not a promise of students or sales.

Are live classes limited?

Live classes on YouTube Live or Zoom are unlimited on every plan. The built-in AllCoaching live classroom comes with hours each month: 20, 50 and 90 hours on monthly Starter, Growth and Pro, and 5, 10 and 15 hours on the yearly plans. There is no per-hour charge.

Can I export my data and leave anytime?

Yes. Your student list, course content, payment history and analytics are exportable any time while your plan runs, and for 30 days after you stop, in standard formats, in line with data-portability rights under the DPDP Act 2023. Nothing is locked - you keep ownership of your brand, your students and your content.

When does the money from a sale reach my bank?

Student payments are collected through Razorpay and settled to your bank on Razorpay's cycle, usually T+2 days. Instant settlement is also available. Payment-gateway charges are separate.

Are there any other charges?

Prices exclude GST, which is added at checkout, and the yearly platform fee is plus GST. Payment-gateway charges on student payments are separate and shown to you. If you need more storage, it can be extended on any plan. There is no setup fee and no separate charge for your domain connection or marketplace listing.
