Questions

Asked before choosing a plan.

How much does AllCoaching cost for an Indian educator in 2026?

AllCoaching has three annual plans. Starter is ₹6,999 a year with a 10% platform fee, so you keep 90% of every sale. Growth is ₹12,999 a year at 7.5%, keeping 92.5%. Pro is ₹24,999 a year at 5%, keeping 95% and including a premium website template. Most educators start with Starter and upgrade later. Prices exclude GST, and there is no sales call or demo required to sign up. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial, so you can set up your academy, add a course and see your website before you pay, and no card is needed to start. When the trial ends, pick Starter, Growth or Pro to continue.

Which plan should I start with?

Most educators start with Starter at ₹6,999 a year. It already includes your academy website, your own domain, listing on the AllCoaching app, 512 GB storage and 5 live-class hours a month. Move up when you need more storage or live hours, or once a lower platform fee saves you more than the higher plan costs.

Is my own domain included on Starter?

Yes. Every plan, including Starter, lets you connect your own domain from the studio. You buy the domain name from any registrar, usually about ₹600 to ₹1,200 a year, so it stays in your name. Until you connect one, your academy runs on a free allcoaching.in address.

What is the premium website template on Pro?

Pro includes a premium website template worth ₹10,000, a more polished design for an established academy. Starter and Growth use the standard templates, which already include course pages, faculty profiles and an enquiry form, and work with your own domain.

Do I need a developer or a sales call to set up?

No. You sign up, choose a plan, upload a course, connect your domain, set up payment and publish, all by yourself from the studio. There is no sales call, no demo dependency and no technical team required.

Where will students find and use my courses?

Students can buy from your own website and study inside the AllCoaching app on Android, iOS and the web. Your courses can also be discovered on the AllCoaching marketplace by students searching for your exam or subject.

What happens if I upgrade, downgrade or don't renew?

An upgrade takes effect as soon as payment clears, and what you already paid this year is credited. To move to a lower plan, contact support and it applies from your next renewal. If you don't renew, your academy goes offline to students, but your content and data stay exportable.

Is marketplace discovery included on every plan?

Yes. AI-driven marketplace discovery - where students search by exam, subject, language and city and find you without ad spend - is included on Starter, Growth and Pro alike. It is not a Pro-only upgrade.

Are live classes metered separately on top of the plan price?

No. Each plan's live-class hours are included in its annual price - 5 hours a month on Starter, 10 on Growth, 15 on Pro. There is no separate per-hour charge within that allowance.

Can I export my data and leave anytime?

Yes. Your student list, course content, payment history and analytics are exportable at any time in standard formats, in line with data-portability rights under the DPDP Act 2023. Nothing is locked - you keep ownership of your brand, your students and your content.

Are there any other charges?

Prices exclude GST, which is added at checkout. Payment-gateway charges on student payments are separate and shown to you. There is no setup fee, no monthly bill and no separate charge for your domain connection or marketplace listing.
