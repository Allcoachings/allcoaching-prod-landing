Teachmint pricing, answered

Frequently asked questions.

How much does Teachmint cost in 2026?

Teachmint does not publish a price. We checked teachmint.com/pricing on 17 October 2026 and it lists no plans or prices; to get one, you book a demo or call its sales line.

Is Teachmint free for teachers?

Teachmint's current website does not mention a free plan or a free trial for teachers or tutors. The only free item it lists is the CBSE Result Analyser, a results-reporting tool.

What does Teachmint sell now?

Teachmint's homepage leads with Teachmint X, an AI-powered connected-classroom device: an interactive flat panel in 65, 75 and 86 inch sizes with classroom software, an AI assistant and device management. It lists solutions for schools, higher education and coaching.

Is Teachmint good for individual tutors who teach online?

Its site does not list a product for individual tutors. If you teach online and want your own website, app, courses and test series, an LMS built for educators fits better. AllCoaching publishes its plans from ₹6,999 a year with a 14-day free trial.

How is AllCoaching different from Teachmint?

Teachmint now focuses on classroom technology for institutions, sold through a demo. AllCoaching is an LMS you build yourself in a few clicks, with your own website on your domain, your own Android app (iOS app on Pro and Scale), published prices and a listing on the AllCoaching marketplace.

What should I ask Teachmint before paying?

Ask for the price in writing and what it includes, whether there is a plan for an individual tutor, whether it is a yearly fee and what renewal costs, whether any fee is taken on your sales, whether GST is extra, and how you can export your students and content if you leave.

Can I move from Teachmint to AllCoaching?

Yes, and you can do it yourself. Export your student list and content from Teachmint first, start the AllCoaching 14-day free trial, build your academy, add your courses, connect your domain and set up payments.

Does AllCoaching charge a setup fee or lock me in?

There is no setup fee. Plans are annual: an upgrade applies as soon as you pay, with the unused value of your current plan credited, and a downgrade applies from your next renewal. If you do not renew, your academy goes offline to students, but your content and student data stay exportable.
