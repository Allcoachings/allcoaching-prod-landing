Graphy pricing, answered

Frequently asked questions.

How much does Graphy cost in 2026?

Graphy's pricing page (graphy.com/pricing, checked 17 October 2026) lists three plans: Launch at ₹24,999 a year, Grow at ₹49,999 a year and Rise at ₹99,999 a year, plus a custom Unlimited plan. Each plan also takes a fee on every sale. The page does not state whether GST is extra.

Does Graphy take a cut of every sale?

Yes. Graphy lists a fee of 10% per sale on Launch, 7.5% on Grow and 5% on Rise, or ₹10 a sale if that is higher. On low-priced items such as a ₹49 test, the ₹10 minimum works out to about 20%.

Is Graphy cheaper than AllCoaching?

No, not on published prices. Both charge 10%, 7.5% or 5% per sale, but Graphy's plans cost ₹24,999, ₹49,999 and ₹99,999 a year, while AllCoaching's yearly plans cost ₹6,999, ₹12,999 and ₹24,999. At the same fee rate, AllCoaching is ₹18,000, ₹37,000 or ₹75,000 less a year. AllCoaching also has monthly plans at 0% commission (₹3,999, ₹5,999 or ₹7,999 a month); monthly Pro costs ₹95,988 for 12 months, below Rise's ₹99,999 before its 5% fee.

Does Graphy have a free trial?

Yes. Graphy's pricing page says it is free for 14 days. AllCoaching also starts with a 7-day free trial, and no card is needed to start it.

What does Graphy include that AllCoaching does not?

Graphy lists an AI website builder, AI agents from Grow and 5,000 to 15,000 marketing emails a month. Both offer unlimited live classes and Android and iOS apps, but Graphy's iOS app comes only with its Rise plan, while AllCoaching includes your own Android app (iOS app on Pro) on every plan, with unlimited live classes through YouTube Live.

What does AllCoaching include that Graphy's price does not mention?

Every AllCoaching plan carries your own Android app (iOS app on Pro), logo, colours and own domain, while Graphy removes its own branding only from the Grow plan up and keeps its iOS app for Rise. AllCoaching also lists your courses on the AllCoaching marketplace, where students on Android, iOS and web discover educators.

Can I move from Graphy to AllCoaching?

Yes, and you can do it yourself. Export your student list and content from Graphy first, start the AllCoaching 7-day free trial, build your academy, add your courses, connect your domain and set up payments. Keep Graphy running until its term ends, so your students never lose access.

Does AllCoaching charge a setup fee or lock me in?

There is no setup fee. Monthly plans have no lock-in: you can stop in any month and your plan runs to the end of the paid month. On yearly plans, an upgrade applies as soon as you pay, with the unused value of your current plan credited, and a downgrade applies from your next renewal. If you do not renew, your academy goes offline to students, but your content and student data stay exportable.
