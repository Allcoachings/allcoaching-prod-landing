Migration questions, answered

Frequently asked about switching.

How is AllCoaching different from Classplus?

Classplus is a white-label coaching app builder — it ships a branded container for each educator but no shared audience. AllCoaching is a marketplace — branded educator space PLUS shared discovery through an AI recommendation engine. The structural difference: Classplus educators acquire 100% of their students through self-funded marketing (₹6–18 lakh/year typical); AllCoaching educators get discovered through marketplace traffic plus their own brand. Year 1 cost difference for a ₹10 lakh revenue educator: Classplus ₹4–11 lakh, AllCoaching ₹1.32 lakh.

What does Classplus actually cost in 2026?

Classplus headline pricing starts around ₹2,500–₹1,25,000 per month, but the true Year 1 cost includes setup ₹25K–₹2L, monthly software 30K–15L, 1.5–4% transaction commission on top of payment gateway 0.3–1%, SMS/WhatsApp credit packs ₹2K–8K/month, branding refresh fees, 15–25% annual escalation, and marketing the educator pays themselves (₹6–18L/year). Real Year 1 all-in for a ₹10L institute: ₹4–11 lakh.

Can I migrate from Classplus to AllCoaching?

Yes. The AllCoaching onboarding team handles migration: student list import (CSV/Excel), course content transfer, payment history reconciliation, batch reassignment. Migration is free — included in the annual subscription, no separate fee. Typical timeline: 3–7 days for a 200–500 student institute. Existing students receive migration emails with new login credentials and retain full course access.

Will I lose my branding when I leave Classplus?

No. Your educator brand — logo, color palette, course content, student relationships, teaching style — is yours. AllCoaching's branded space replicates these on a new infrastructure. The only thing you lose is the Classplus-managed Play Store/App Store app, but AllCoaching provides an equivalent branded Android + iOS app under your name with no separate build cost.

What about my existing Classplus contract?

Many Classplus contracts include annual lock-in clauses. Migration timing depends on your contract: (1) if ending within 90 days, migrate ahead of renewal; (2) if mid-cycle, weigh breakage cost against savings (typically AllCoaching's Year 1 cost is ₹3–10 lakh lower, so even a 6-month overlap is net-positive); (3) consult your contract for data export clauses — most allow CSV export at any time, which is all you need for migration.

Why is AllCoaching cheaper than Classplus?

Architecture, not undercutting. AllCoaching is a marketplace that amortises platform infrastructure (live streaming, payments, DRM, GST, marketing) across thousands of educators. Classplus is a white-label app builder where each educator gets their own app instance, multiplying per-educator infrastructure cost. The 10% transaction commission AllCoaching takes is a single all-inclusive fee covering payment gateway + DRM + GST + marketplace discovery. Classplus's monthly software fee + transaction commission stack independently and grow with educator scale.

Is the AllCoaching marketplace worth the 10% commission?

For 90%+ of Indian educators, yes. The 10% replaces what would otherwise be ₹6–18 lakh/year in self-funded marketing on Classplus. For a ₹10 lakh revenue educator, that 10% (₹1 lakh) is structurally cheaper than ₹6 lakh+ of marketing. Above ₹2 Cr annual revenue with proven brand, the math shifts — at that scale, a custom build with controlled marketing may make sense. Below that threshold, marketplace economics win decisively.

Is AllCoaching as feature-rich as Classplus?

In core features: yes. Both have branded Android + iOS app, live classes with attendance + recording, payment processing, course catalog, student CRM, basic analytics, anti-piracy, GST compliance. AllCoaching adds: marketplace discovery via AI recommendation, daily payouts (Classplus is weekly/monthly), included multi-format catalog (PDFs + test series without add-on), pooled anti-piracy with marketplace-scale Telegram/YouTube monitoring, and DPDP/POCSO compliance baked in. Classplus has: longer track record, heavier marketing services up-sells (which are also additional costs). Different value propositions for different educator stages.
