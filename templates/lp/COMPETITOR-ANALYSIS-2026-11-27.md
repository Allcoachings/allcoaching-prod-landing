## 6. What this means for AllCoaching

1. **"Alternative" searches are small but contested.** "classplus alternative" is only 10–100 a month, yet four rivals bid on it and we already rank #1 organically. Bid there for cover, but don't expect volume.
2. **The real volume is people searching a rival's name + "pricing".** "classplus pricing", "graphy pricing", "learnyst/exly/tagmango pricing" are 100–1K each, with cheap bids for Classplus (₹7–₹95) and few or no ads. These match our `/vs/` pricing pages exactly. **Start here.**
3. **Brand-name terms (classplus, graphy, teachmint, tagmango: 10K–100K each) are mostly students and existing users** logging in. Use only with the "Educator only" negative list, exact/phrase match and a small budget, and expect a low conversion rate.
4. **Teachmint is no longer an LMS competitor in ads** (hardware). Teachmint-searching educators are open to switching; "teachmint pricing" bids are very cheap (₹3–₹46).
5. **Winuall is dead** — its users are looking for a new home. Low volume (100–1K), no bids found.
6. **Rivals' main claims:** "0% / keep 100%" (Edmingle, Learnyst), "#1 LMS" (Edmingle, Graphy, Learnyst), big discounts (Classplus 60–70% off, AppX 50%, Learnyst "lowest ever"). We **cannot** copy "#1" or fake urgency (`04-voice-and-claims.md`). Our honest edge: published price on the page, 0% from ₹3,999/mo, own Android on every plan, no demo needed, 7-day trial with no card. Edmingle and Learnyst lock 0% and branded apps behind ₹14,999+/mo plans.
7. **Graphy and Edmingle already use the "switch to us" message.** Our ads must say why: price on the page, no sales call, own app included.
8. **New rivals to add to `/vs/`:** Vacademy and Igniter (both target "classplus alternative"), TrainerCentral (ranks for "graphy alternative").
