Our story

Built by educators, for educators.

AllCoaching exists for one reason — so teachers can spend their days teaching, not marketing, chasing payments, or fighting platforms that treat them like vendors.

Why we started

India has millions of brilliant educators. Many of them teach in a small classroom, a Telegram group, or a YouTube channel — and earn a fraction of what they deserve because the systems around them aren't built for them. They spend half their time on ads, tech, payouts, and support tickets. The other half goes to teaching.

We've seen this firsthand. We've watched gifted teachers burn out not from teaching, but from running everything else. AllCoaching was born to collapse that gap.

What we're building

AllCoaching is a two-sided platform for the Indian education ecosystem:

  • A branded platform — your own web & mobile experience, live classes, payments, analytics, and student CRM in one login.
  • A marketplace — real learners come here to find educators like you, so discovery isn't your full-time job anymore.

One account. Your brand. 90% of revenue back to you. Daily payouts. Zero "aggregator math."

Our principles

  • Educator-first economics. Transparent fees. No locked-in pricing. No surprise deductions.
  • Craft over clutter. The software should feel calm, premium, and fast — like a tool you're proud to hand to your students.
  • Built in India, for India. INR-native, GST-ready, UPI-first, Hindi-English bilingual, priced for Bharat.
  • Democratize education. A Tier-3 teacher with real skill should be able to reach a Tier-1 student. We build for that.

Where we are today

Thousands of educators across India run their coaching with AllCoaching — from solo tutors to institutes with 5,000+ students. We're growing carefully, because a platform that handles people's livelihood has to be worth their trust.
