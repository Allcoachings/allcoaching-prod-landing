Questions

Before you click.

Is the AllCoaching demo free, and do I need to sign up?

The demo is free and needs no sign-up at all — no login, no phone number, no email and no card. Open any of the three demos and click straight into the educator studio, an academy website or the student app. It runs in the browser on a laptop or a phone, and nothing you do is saved or charged.

Is this the real product?

Yes. These are the same screens educators use on studio.allcoaching.in, filled with sample data so you can click freely. The website and student app show a sample chemistry academy, ChemPsi.

Will anything I do here be saved or charged?

No. Payments, students and live viewers are simulated, and no money moves. Changes stay only in your browser — tap Live demo → Start over to reset.

Does the demo work on a phone?

Yes. All three demos are built for a phone as well as a laptop. The student app demo is designed for a phone in particular, since that is where students actually use it, and it installs to the home screen straight from the browser with no app store.

Will my students see my brand or AllCoaching's?

Yours. Your academy name and logo lead in the student app and on your website. AllCoaching runs the infrastructure in the background.

What does AllCoaching cost after the demo?

Selling courses is free — ₹0 to start, no card at signup, no subscription — and a flat 10% is charged only on what you actually sell, so you keep 90%, paid out daily. Connecting your own domain, which puts the full student app on your address, is a one-time ₹10,000 per domain with nothing to renew. Live classes are metered separately on a plan that is free for 5 hours a month and up to 100 students at once. Full detail is on the pricing page.

What happens when I join for real?

You get the same studio you just clicked through, under your own name — a free yourname.allcoaching.in web address, your courses, tests and notes, UPI checkout, daily payouts and a listing on the AllCoaching marketplace so students can find you. Nothing carries over from the demo, because the demo holds only sample data.

Can I talk to a person first?

Of course. Message us on WhatsApp and someone from the AllCoaching team will reply.
