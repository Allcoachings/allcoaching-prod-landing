    How connecting works

    Three steps. We show you exactly what to copy.

    1Buy a domain anywhere

    GoDaddy, Namecheap, Hostinger — any registrar works. We don't sell domains.

    2Add two records

    A TXT that proves the domain is yours, and a CNAME that points it here.

    3We switch it on

    We check every few minutes and turn on HTTPS by itself. Usually a few minutes.

    Free — you only pay your registrar for the domain.
