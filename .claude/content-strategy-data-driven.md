Search Console data** (Apr 26 – Jul 25, 2026).

## Ground rules (non-negotiable)
1. **Only educator topics.** Audience = Indian coaching-institute owners, tutors,
   exam mentors, subject teachers. If a topic does not serve THAT reader, do not write it.
2. **Write to proven India search demand only.** Every blog must target queries the
   GSC data shows real Indian educators type. No speculative / futuristic essays.
3. **India + mobile first.** 97% of clicks are India (891 of ~918). Mobile CTR 1.81% > desktop 0.91%.
4. **Optimise for CLICKS + POSITION, never impressions.** Impressions inflate on junk
   foreign + news traffic; ignore the impressions number.
5. Every blog: full `blog-post` scaffold (6 schemas, TL;DR, glossary, FAQ verbatim),
   registered (sitemap/llms/index), ≥4 inbound internal links, CTR-optimised title/meta.
