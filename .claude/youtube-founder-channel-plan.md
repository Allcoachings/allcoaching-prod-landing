# AllCoaching — Founder Personal Channel: 12-Video Plan (from 2026-08-15)

Speaker: Amit Ratan, Founder & CEO. Personal channel, Hinglish, educator audience.
Data: user-supplied YouTube volume list (Aug 2026) + repo GSC export (May 12 – Aug 11 2026).

## Ground rules
1. **YouTube demand ≠ Google demand.** Proven in this repo: "daily payout" had YouTube volume but **zero** Google queries in 3 months. Never pick video topics from GSC, or blog topics from YouTube volume.
2. **A zero-subscriber channel cannot rank on 50k+ head terms in week 1.** Early wins come from timely spikes, founder-only authority, and exact long-tail.
3. **Founder-only content is the moat.** Anything a generic creator can film is a losing bet. Real numbers, real economics, and real refusals ("mat banwao") cannot be copied.
4. Brand voice holds on camera: no exclamation marks, say *educator* (not creator), *studio* (not dashboard), ₹ ranges only, no "#1 platform" claims, no fabricated stats.
5. Every video links its paired blog in the description. Blog feeds video authority; video feeds blog traffic.

## The data insight driving this plan
`/blog/white-label-coaching-app-development-cost-india` → **453 impressions, position 6.04, 0 clicks** on Google.

Demand exists; the article format fails. **Cost and breakdown queries want video, not prose.** This is the single biggest opening for the channel.

# PHASE 1 — AUTHORITY (Week 1–2)
Founder-only content. Low competition because it requires insider standing.

## V1 — PM Modi free coaching → educator impact
- **Keyword:** free online coaching PM Modi teachers par asar · coaching wale ab kya karein
- **Volume:** live spike (announced 15 Aug 2026)
- **Title:** PM ne Free Coaching ka ailaan kiya — ab Coaching walon ka kya hoga?
- **Thumbnail:** अब कोचिंग वालों का क्या?
- **Hook (0–15s):** "Aaj Lal Qile se jo kaha gaya, use har coaching wala galat padh raha hai. Ye ailaan kharche ke baare mein tha — competition ke baare mein nahi."

**Context:** News channels are covering the news. Nobody is covering *"coaching teacher ka kya hoga"*. That gap is the entire opportunity. Window is 48–72 hours.

**Approach:** Reframe first (cost ≠ competition). Then the verified quote. Then what was **not** announced — no scheme name, mechanism, timeline or exam list. That admission is the credibility moment. Then: free has been the competitor for a decade (NCERT, SWAYAM, DIKSHA, YouTube) and paid coaching grew anyway. Close on information vs accountability.

**Avoid:** Do not claim "3.5 lakh apps band pade hain" — unverified. Use manifesto framing: *"3.5 lakh educators, unke apps ek tapu bankar reh gaye."*

**Blog:** `/blog/free-government-coaching-impact-on-private-educators` + Hinglish twin

## V2 — Coaching app ka asli kharcha
- **Keyword:** coaching app kitne me banta hai · **Volume:** 25,000–45,000
- **Title:** Coaching App banane mein asal mein kitna lagta hai? (Founder ki sacchai)
- **Thumbnail:** ₹30,000 या ₹3,00,000?
- **Hook:** "Mujhse roz poocha jata hai — app ka kitna lagega. Aaj asli number dikhata hoon, aur ye bhi bataunga ki shayad aapko app chahiye hi nahi."

**Context:** Roughly 90% of videos on this topic are made by app-development agencies who are selling the app. You are the one person who can say *don't build it*. That refusal is the differentiator. And the GSC data proves this query wants video.

**Approach:** Show a line-by-line breakdown on screen. Cover both models honestly — one-time build (AppX-style, reported ₹15,000–65,000+) vs subscription + share (Classplus-style, reported ₹8,000–50,000+/yr plus reported setup and share). Then the costs nobody quotes: maintenance, OS and Play Store policy changes, hosting, gateway, and marketing — which usually dwarfs all of it. End on the crossover logic.

**Blog:** `/blog/white-label-coaching-app-development-cost-india` + `/blogs/hinglish/coaching-app-banane-me-kitna-paisa-lagta-hai` + `/blog/appx-vs-classplus`

## V3 — Bina ad spend ke students
- **Keyword:** bina ad spend ke online students kaise milenge · **Volume:** 20,000–45,000
- **Title:** Bina ek rupaya Ad kharch kiye Students kaise laayein
- **Thumbnail:** ₹0 Ads. फिर भी Students.

# PHASE 2 — DECISION / BUYER INTENT (Week 3–6)
Highest CTR band. These viewers are close to switching.

## V4 — Honest platform comparison (with disclosure)
- **Keyword:** classplus review honest (12,000–25,000) + teachmint vs classplus (6,000–12,000)
- **Title:** Classplus, Teachmint, AppX — Founder hokar main sach bata raha hoon
- **Thumbnail:** Founder की Honest तुलना
- **Hook:** "Main AllCoaching ka founder hoon — yaani main neutral nahi hoon, aur ye main pehle hi bata raha hoon. Isliye main opinion nahi dunga. Sirf public pricing aur structure dikhaunga, faisla aap kijiye."

**Context:** Comparison keywords carry the highest CTR of any category. Disclosure converts the conflict of interest into credibility — a founder who declares bias upfront is trusted more, not less.

**Approach:** Use the exact discipline from the `appx-vs-classplus` blog. Label every competitor figure as "publicly reported range, not a quote". Give at least one concession **against AllCoaching** on camera — at high revenue a flat 10% costs more than a one-time fee. That single line carries the whole video's credibility.

**Hard avoid:** No "hidden charges exposed" framing. It violates the brand voice rule on hostile competitor references and guarantees *"apna platform bech raha hai"* in the comments.

**Blog:** `/blog/appx-vs-classplus` + `/blog/classplus-vs-graphy-vs-allcoaching`

## V5 — 7 sawaal before buying any coaching app
- **Keyword:** coaching app lene se pehle kya dekhein (long-tail, derived from V4 intent)
- **Title:** Koi bhi Coaching App lene se pehle ye 7 sawaal poochho
- **Thumbnail:** 7 सवाल. फिर पैसे दो.
- **Hook:** "Har hafte koi educator mujhe message karta hai ki contract sign karne ke baad pata chala. Ye 7 sawaal pehle pooch lijiye."

**Context:** Captures comparison-intent traffic without naming anyone. Zero hostility, pure utility, and it positions you as the educator's advocate rather than a vendor.

**Approach:** Setup fee? Share of sales? Maintenance included, and for how long? Who owns student data and how is it exported? What happens if you stop paying? Payout timing? Exit terms? Make each one answerable on screen.

**Blog:** `/blog/appx-vs-classplus` (7-question checklist section)

# Publishing order
- **Today:** V1 (48–72 hour news window)
- **Week 1–2:** V2, V3 — authority foundation
- **Week 3–6:** V4, V5, V6, V7 — buyer intent
- **Week 7–12:** V8–V12 — compounders

One video per week is enough. Consistency beats volume for a new channel.

# Deliberately excluded, with reasons
- **"Online padhane ke liye best app" (45–80k)** — highest volume, but you own a platform. Self-serving, and a new channel will not be trusted on it.
- **"Smart class setup" / "Digital board cost" (10–22k)** — hardware content. No founder authority, no funnel.
- **"Daily payout" (4–8k)** — confirmed zero Google demand in this repo's GSC export; too thin to open a channel with.
- **Any "hidden charges exposed" angle** — violates brand voice, invites bias accusations.

# Measurement
Judge weeks 1–4 on **CTR and average view duration**, not views. A new channel's early videos are a signal-collection exercise. Views follow once the algorithm trusts retention.
