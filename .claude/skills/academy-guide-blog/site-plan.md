### A3. How Shopify handles competitors
1. **The blog never attacks a competitor.** It teaches. Comparisons live on `/compare` pages.
2. **Compare pages are product-led and use one framework**: four reasons ("Manage your way · Sell more · Scale without compromise · Get more features") then proof, migration and FAQ.
3. **Every claim has a number and a source** (TCO 36% better than WooCommerce, 99.9% uptime, storefront speed studies, analyst reports).
4. **Against do-it-yourself tools (WooCommerce, WordPress) they sell "we handle it all"**: hosting, security, updates, compliance. This is exactly AllCoaching's "WordPress LMS + 9 plugins" creative.
5. **Three-way pages** (`wordpress-vs-wix-vs-shopify`) catch searches where the reader has not even considered Shopify yet.
6. **Switching is made easy** ("Transfer your data", migration partners), and switcher quotes come from real customers with case studies.
7. **Free trial CTA on every compare page.**

### A4. How Shopify positions itself
- One platform from the first sale to enterprise. The merchant owns the brand and the store.
- **Sell everywhere from one admin**: website, social, marketplaces, in person.
- "Let Shopify handle the complex so you can focus on growing your business."
- Free trial is the constant next step.

**AllCoaching mirror:** the academy platform for educators. Own your academy (website on your domain + your own Android and iOS app). Teach and sell everywhere from one studio: website, app, live classes, YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp, plus discovery on the AllCoaching marketplace. "Focus on teaching. We handle the tech."

## Part B — The AllCoaching plan

### B1. Message house (use on every page)
- **Promise:** Build your own academy in a few clicks: your website, your app, your brand.
- **Proof points:** own domain and own Android + iOS app on every plan; unlimited live classes and videos via YouTube; UPI/card payments with automatic Razorpay payouts; Meta Pixel + Google Tag Manager; keep 90–95% of every sale; 7-day free trial, no card; 500+ educators already selling.

### B3. Blog system

**Topic hubs** (like `/blog/topics/guides`): `/blog/topics/start`, `create-course`, `brand-website`, `sell`, `marketing`, `grow`, `ai-for-educators`, `stories`. Every post belongs to one hub; hubs are ItemList pages with a short intro and the best 10 posts.

**Content mix (per month)**
- 50% how-to guides (start, build, sell, market)
- 15% channel guides (YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, email, Google)
- 10% definitions and pillars ("What is...")
- 10% "Sell X online" companions to the use-case pages
- 10% lists and tools (best free tools for educators, templates)
- 5% real educator stories (when available)

**Volume:** 3 posts a week (about 12 a month). That is roughly 150 posts in a year — enough to own the educator-growth topic in India, at about one-eighth of Shopify's blog size.

**Clusters to fill, in order**
1. The 20-post English roadmap in `roadmap.md` (start, course, brand, sell, marketing, growth).
2. Channel cluster: YouTube channel to academy funnel; Instagram for educators; WhatsApp admissions; Telegram channel to paid course; Google Business Profile; email for course sellers.
3. "Sell X online" cluster: one guide per use-case page (NEET, UPSC, yoga, spoken English, and so on).
4. AI for educators: AI to create course outlines, notes, tests, thumbnails, ad copy; AI search for educators.
5. Definitions: LMS, white-label app, online academy, course funnel, CPA/CPL for coaching ads, cohort course, drip content.
6. Operations: refunds, GST for online educators (exists), student retention (exists), hiring teachers, multi-teacher academies.
7. Stories: "How [real educator] moved from WhatsApp groups to their own academy".

**Linking rule (the Shopify flywheel):** every post links to 1 product page + 1 use-case page or tool + 3–5 related posts. Every product and use-case page links to its 3 best guides.

### B4. Competitor rules (summary)
- Blogs teach and stay neutral; no attacks, no "cheapest" claims.
- Competitor names appear in `/vs/` pages and honest comparison posts only, with a sourced price per figure and the date checked.
- The strongest comparison is against do-it-yourself setups (WordPress, WhatsApp + Drive), framed as "we handle it all".
- Switching help on every compare page; real switcher quotes only.

### B5. SEO mechanics
- **Sitemaps:** split into `sitemap-pages.xml`, `sitemap-blog.xml`, `sitemap-compare.xml`, `sitemap-tools.xml` under a sitemap index (Shopify does the same split).
- **Schema by page type:** product pages = SoftwareApplication + FAQPage; use-case = Service + FAQPage; compare = ItemList/Table + FAQPage; tools = WebApplication; hubs = CollectionPage + ItemList; posts = the six-block set from the skill.
- **llms.txt:** add sections for Product pages, Use cases, Compare, Tools, matching the sitemap split.
- **Refresh cadence:** every pillar reviewed each exam cycle; Search Console review monthly (pages with impressions and CTR below the site average get title/meta rewrites first).
- **Measurement:** Search Console grouped by page type; GTM events for "Start free trial" clicks by page type; monthly report: impressions, clicks, CTR, trials per page type.
