# 04 · Voice and claims

## Voice
- **Who speaks:** AllCoaching, or founder **Amit Ratan** (blog byline, manifesto, founder posts). Calm, confident, editorial, educator-first. Confidence comes from accuracy, not hype.
- **How:** name the educator's pain in their words → show the fix → one clear action. Short sentences. Indian English spelling (colour, organise). Indian number format (₹1,49,990; ₹4 lakh; ₹1.2 crore). ₹ only, never $ or "INR"/"rupees" in copy.
- **Words:** educator (not creator/user/tutor-as-generic) · studio (not dashboard/admin panel) · your students · your academy · academy website · own app.
- **Banned:** exclamation marks · "unlock", "maximize", "game-changer", "revolutionary", "seamless", "onboard" (verb), "creator" · "#1", "best platform" as fact, "India's leading" · "cheap", "cheapest", "affordable", "budget", "lowest price" · "free forever", "free plan", "₹0" · emojis in long-form (fine sparingly in social/ads hooks) · ALL-CAPS shouting.
- **Word swaps:** creator/user → educator · dashboard → studio · cheap/affordable → "0% commission, one clear price" · book a demo → "start free for 7 days" · LMS software → "your own academy" (LMS is fine in headlines/SEO).
- **Illustrative numbers** must say "illustrative" and be computed with current pricing (`02-pricing.md`). Example allowed: "Illustrative: 50 students × ₹999 = ₹49,950, and on a monthly plan all of it is yours (before GST and gateway charges)."

## Hinglish
Used in ads, social, WhatsApp and `blogs/hinglish/`. Roman script, natural mix ("Apni academy banaiye", "7 din free, card nahi chahiye", "5 jagah ka kaam, ek login me"). Same claims rules apply. Keep product nouns in English (studio, app, domain, 0% commission, test series).

## Competitor mentions
Allowed when sourced (page + check date) and fair. Show their transaction fee next to their fixed price. Never round up their price, never say they are bad; the enemy is complexity. On ad landing pages (`/landing/*`) don't name competitors.

## Sensitive topics
- Earnings: no promises; use ranges and "illustrative".
- Exams/results: never claim AllCoaching improves ranks or results.
- Government/accreditation: certificates are completion proofs, not degrees.
- Data/privacy: DPDP Act 2023 compliant privacy page exists; don't over-claim security certifications.
