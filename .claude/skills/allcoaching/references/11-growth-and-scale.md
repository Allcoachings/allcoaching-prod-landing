# 11 · Growth and scale

Facts are marked; everything else here is a **working plan / recommendation** for the owner to adjust — never publish it as a company claim.

## Who we serve (fact)
India's ~3.5 lakh independent educators: coaching-institute owners, exam tutors (NEET, JEE, UPSC, SSC, banking, CUET, CLAT, CAT, state PSC, CA/CS, GATE, CTET), school-subject tutors, and skill coaches (yoga, spoken English, digital marketing, music, coding, stock market, vastu, Vedas). Hinglish- and regional-language aware; tier-2/3 cities included. 500+ educators already sell on AllCoaching.

## Ideal customer profiles (plan)
| ICP | Signal | Best plan to suggest | Main message |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo starter (YouTube/Instagram teacher, first paid course) | < ₹4 lakh/yr sales | Yearly Starter ₹6,999 + 10% | Own academy + app without big fixed cost |
| Growing tutor (steady batches) | ₹4–20 lakh/yr | Monthly Starter/Growth (0%) | Keep 100%, stop any month |
| Coaching institute (multiple teachers, test series, live daily) | Team of 3–7, iOS students | Growth/Pro monthly (or Pro yearly if sales lower) | Team seats, 4K, iOS, live hours, rank/percentile |
| Switcher (Classplus/agency app, WordPress, tool-stack) | Pain with control, cost or complexity | By sales level | One login, own name, data export, published prices |

## Funnel and metrics (definitions)
Visit → Lead (OTP-verified) → Studio created (sign_up) → Trial or Purchase → Activated (theme set, first course published, domain or app started) → Paid → Retained (renewal / months on monthly) → Expanded (upgrade).
North-star: **paying academies** (and their monthly recurring revenue, ex-GST). Track weekly: Leads, CPL, Lead→trial %, trial→paid %, CPA, activation rate within 7 days, monthly churn on monthly plans, upgrades, revenue by plan and billing.

## Growth engines (plan)
1. **Paid acquisition** — Meta (Lead → Purchase optimisation), Google Search on high-intent terms, YouTube walkthroughs. See `05-ads-and-performance.md`.
2. **Content/SEO + AI search** — guide blog roadmap, `/vs/` pages, `/sell/<exam>` use-case pages, free calculators (price, income, plan finder). See `07-seo-and-content.md`.
3. **Social + founder brand** — Instagram reels/carousels, YouTube tutorials, LinkedIn founder posts. See `06-social-media.md`.
4. **Product-led** — 7-day trial that gets the educator to a live branded academy fast; onboarding lands in Own branding; in-studio checklists (theme → course → domain → app); upgrade prompts at storage/live-hour/seat limits.
5. **Referrals and showcase** — referral programme (details only once the owner confirms terms), showcase academies with permission.
6. **Partnerships** — educator-YouTubers, exam communities, coaching associations, domain registrars, Razorpay ecosystem; theme designers (premium themes).
7. **Marketplace flywheel** — more academies listed → more student discovery in the AllCoaching app → extra channel for educators (supporting message only).

## Activation and retention playbook (plan)
- Day 0: academy name + theme + logo applied; free `.allcoaching.in` live.
- Day 1–3: first course (YouTube or uploaded video + PDF + one test), checkout tested with ₹1 coupon if available, WhatsApp share link.
- Day 3–7: domain connected, Android app build started, Meta Pixel/GTM added; trial-end reminder with plan recommendation by expected sales (`02-pricing.md` maths).
- Monthly plans: monthly value recap (sales kept at 0%), live hours used, storage used; upgrade prompts at 80% of a limit.
- Exit: remind of the 30-day export window; ask why (price, features, results) — feed back to product.

## Scale levers (plan)
- Vertical landing pages and ads per exam/skill (NEET, JEE, UPSC/State PSC, SSC/banking, spoken English, yoga, digital marketing).
- Hindi and regional-language surfaces (Hinglish ads/social already; consider Hindi pages).
- Theme marketplace (premium exam themes like Rajya Seva).
- Institute tier needs (more seats, multi-branch) — only after owner confirms product.
- Data: weekly dashboard from GA4 + Meta + Razorpay + studio exports; fix tracking P1–P8 first so decisions use clean numbers.

## Risks to watch
Over-promising (ad rejections, refunds, trust) · tracking contamination (student purchases in the educator dataset) · price confusion between monthly 0% and yearly fee · app-store review delays (set expectations; never promise timelines) · storage/streaming overages on uploaded video (push YouTube for unlimited).
