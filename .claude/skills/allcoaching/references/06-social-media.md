## Content pillars (mix per week)
1. **How-to for educators (40%)** — "How to sell your first course", "Connect your domain in 3 steps", "Price your course", "Get students from YouTube to your app", "Meta ads for coaching". Ties to blog posts.
2. **Product moments (25%)** — real studio screen recordings: build a course, apply a theme, connect domain, Android app ready, a UPI payment arriving, test series with rank. Caption sample data.
3. **Own-your-academy ideas (15%)** — manifesto lines, complexity vs one login, own brand vs renting an audience. No bashing competitors.
4. **Proof (10%)** — showcase academies (Biology Lessons, CHEMPSI, DigiteKM: name, logo, site screenshot only), "500+ educators", product updates.
5. **Founder (10%)** — Amit Ratan on why AllCoaching exists, building in public, educator lessons.

## Formats and rules
- **Reels/Shorts (9:16, 20–45 s):** hook in 1–2 s (pain line or product moment), on-screen captions in Hinglish, real UI, end card "7 din free · allcoaching.in". Trending audio OK, no misleading text.
- **Carousels (1080×1350, 6–10 slides):** slide 1 hook headline (Inter Tight 200 + 700, one blue phrase), steps, last slide CTA + price line. Brand style per `03-brand-and-design.md`.
- **YouTube long-form (8–20 min):** tutorial format; chapters; link the matching blog post and `/start`; pin a comment with the trial link.
- **LinkedIn:** founder text posts (story → lesson → product in one line), carousels as PDF.
- **Captions:** first line = the hook; then 2–4 short lines; CTA; 3–6 relevant hashtags (#onlineteaching #coachingapp #NEETteacher #educatorsofindia #onlinecourse). No exclamation marks, same claims rules as everywhere.
- **Comments/DMs:** reply within a day; pricing answers exactly as `02-pricing.md`; move sales questions to WhatsApp; never promise students or approval timelines.

## Weekly cadence (starting point; owner adjusts)
Instagram: 4 reels + 2 carousels + daily stories · YouTube: 1 long tutorial + 3 Shorts (re-cut reels) · LinkedIn: 2 founder posts · X: 3–5 posts · Telegram: 2 updates · Facebook: auto-share. Re-use one core idea across formats (blog → carousel → reel → short → LinkedIn post).

## Community and partnerships
- WhatsApp/Telegram groups of educators: share guides, not spam.
- Collaborations with educator-YouTubers: product walkthroughs on their channel (disclose paid partnerships).
- Never post screenshots of an educator's dashboard numbers without written permission; showcase list is fixed (see `SKILL.md` §1).

## Measurement
UTM every link (`utm_source=instagram|youtube|linkedin|x|telegram|whatsapp&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<theme>`). Track: reach, saves/shares (carousels), 3-s hold and completion (reels), profile link clicks, `/start` visits, trials. Monthly review of what topics lead to trials.
