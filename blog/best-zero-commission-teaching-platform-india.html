    The best zero-commission platform depends on your sales. A 0% plan suits an educator whose yearly sales are large enough that a fixed fee costs less than a percentage. Below that level, a low fixed price with a percentage usually costs less.

    What "zero commission" really means

    Zero commission means the platform takes no percentage of your sales; it does not mean the platform is free. Every 0% plan in this guide has a fixed monthly or yearly price, and most still pass on payment-gateway or card-processing charges.

    1. You pay before your first sale

    A fixed fee is due whether you sell or not. Learnyst's Professional plan is ₹97,188 a year billed annually; TagMango Ultimate is ₹3,60,000 a year plus GST. For a new educator, that is a large cost to carry before students arrive.

    2. Payment charges still apply

    Kajabi says card processing fees apply, Thinkific prices its payments at 2.9% in line with Stripe and PayPal, Teachable marks its 0% with processing fees, and TagMango lists a gateway charge from 1.9%. Compare these separately from the platform fee.

    Question educators ask

    Is a zero-commission platform cheaper than one that takes a percentage?

    Only above a certain sales level. Below it, the fixed fee of a 0% plan costs more than a percentage of your sales. Divide the 0% plan's yearly price by the percentage you would otherwise pay to find roughly where they cross, or use the table in the cost section.

    3. Add-ons change the total

    On some platforms your own domain and branded app are add-ons. Thinkific's branded app is $199 a month; TagMango's custom domain is ₹24,999 a year and its own-app setup ₹74,999 + GST. Add these before you compare.

    How to decide between a 0% plan and a percentage

    The decision takes four steps and one honest sales estimate.

    1. Estimate your yearly salesUse last year's course sales, or a cautious estimate for the next 12 months.
    2. Convert every fee into rupeesMultiply each percentage by your sales and add the plan's yearly price; convert dollar plans at today's rate.
    3. Add gateway charges and add-onsInclude payment-processing fees, GST, and the cost of your own domain and branded app where they are add-ons.
    4. Test the best fit in a free trialBuild one course and sell to a few real students before you commit to a yearly plan.

    Glossary

    Zero commission
    A pricing model where the platform takes no percentage of your sales and charges a fixed monthly or yearly fee instead.
    Platform fee
    A percentage a platform keeps from each sale. AllCoaching's is 10%, 7.5% or 5% depending on the plan, charged only when you sell.
    Payment-gateway fee
    The charge for processing a card or UPI payment, separate from any platform fee or commission.
    Breakeven sales
    The yearly sales level at which two pricing models cost the same; above it, the lower-percentage plan costs less.
    Add-on
    A feature sold separately from the main plan, such as a branded app or custom domain.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best zero-commission teaching platform in India?

    It depends on your sales. Learnyst publishes 0% transaction fees on plans from ₹8,999 a month (₹8,099 billed annually), and TagMango Ultimate is zero commission at ₹30,000 a month plus GST. For educators selling in dollars, Kajabi, Thinkific and Teachable's Builder and Growth plans publish 0% platform fees.

    Are zero-commission teaching platforms free?

    No. Every platform in this guide that publishes a 0% fee on sales charges a fixed monthly or yearly price for that plan, and most still pass on payment-gateway or processing charges.

    Does AllCoaching charge a commission?

    Yes. AllCoaching charges a platform fee of 10% on Starter (₹6,999 a year), 7.5% on Growth (₹12,999) or 5% on Pro (₹24,999), GST extra. It is not a zero-commission platform.

    When is a 0% plan cheaper than a percentage fee?

    When your sales are high enough. On published prices, Learnyst's 0% Professional plan billed annually costs less than AllCoaching above about ₹14.4 lakh of sales a year, and TagMango Ultimate above about ₹67 lakh.

    Do zero-commission platforms still charge payment fees?

    Usually yes. Kajabi and Teachable mention processing fees, Thinkific prices its payments at 2.9%, and TagMango lists a gateway charge from 1.9% on Ultimate.

    Is there a teaching platform with no upfront fee in India?

    Yes. Exly Starter has no annual fee and TagMango Basic has no fixed fee, but both take 10% commission, and a custom website or branded app costs extra.

    Is Learnyst zero commission?

    Only on its monthly plans. Professional, Premium and Business list 0% transaction fees; the Launcher plan at ₹10,000 a year takes 10% of sales. Taxes are extra on all plans.

    Should a new educator choose a zero-commission platform?

    Usually not at the start. A 0% plan's fixed fee is due before your first sale. A lower fixed price with a percentage costs less until your sales grow, and you can move to a 0% plan later if the numbers favour it.
