    Why educators look for an Adda247 alternative

    Educators who look for an Adda247 alternative usually want their own name to grow, not to escape the work. Here is what a big brand gives, and what to think through before you move.

    What a big exam-prep brand does well

    Adda247 offers government-exam and upskilling preparation in many languages, with online courses, mocks and books.[1] For an educator, that means a large audience, production at scale and a team handling marketing and sales. Many educators learn to teach to camera inside companies like this, and that experience is worth a lot.

    1. What you can take, and what stays

    Your agreement decides who owns the videos, PDFs, mocks and question banks you made while working there. Read it before you plan anything. Your teaching method, your shortcuts and your name are yours; the files you made for a company may not be. The safe default is to create fresh material for your own academy.

    Question educators ask

    Can I build my own academy while I still work at the company?

    It depends on your contract. Some agreements include exclusivity, non-compete or notice-period terms. Adda247 does not publish its educator terms, so we cannot say what yours contains. Read it, ask the company in writing if anything is unclear, or speak to a lawyer. This guide is not legal advice.

    2. A known face at a new address

    Aspirants who learned SSC maths or banking reasoning from you often follow you by name on YouTube or Instagram. When you open your own academy, telling them where to find you is usually the fastest way to your first students.

    3. Setting your own price and language

    On your own academy you decide the price of each series and course, and you can teach in Hindi, Bengali, Tamil or any language your aspirants prefer. See teaching in regional Indian languages.

    4. One place instead of many

    Educators who start alone often stitch together a live-class app, Drive folders and UPI screenshots. One academy login replaces that from the first day.

    Glossary

    Exam-prep brand
    A company that sells courses, mocks and books for competitive exams under its own name, taught by many educators.
    Content-ownership clause
    A contract term that says who owns the videos, notes and questions you create while working for a company.
    Notice period
    The time a contract asks you to keep working after you say you are leaving.
    Own academy
    A teaching business under your own name, with your own website, app, prices and student list.
    Test series
    A planned set of mock tests sold together, usually with solutions and results after each test.
