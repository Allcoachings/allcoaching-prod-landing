    What actually differs between LMS platforms

    Almost every LMS on this list can host a course, run a test and take a payment. Four things separate them for a coaching institute.

    1. The shape of the bill

    Some charge a fixed yearly or monthly price (Classplus, Edmingle, Learnyst's higher plans). Some charge a percentage of sales with little or nothing upfront (Exly Starter, TagMango Basic). Some do both (AllCoaching, Learnyst Launcher). Which is cheaper depends on your sales, so convert every percentage into rupees at your own numbers.

    2. Where your own domain and app sit

    On some platforms your own domain and branded app are included; on others they are a higher plan or a paid add-on. Ask which plan, at what price, before you compare anything else.

    Question educators ask

    Do I need an LMS or a coaching app?

    Today most platforms are both: an LMS to run courses and tests, plus a website and an Android and iOS app for students. What matters is whether the app carries your name, who publishes it, and which plan includes it.

    3. Rupees or dollars

    Tutor LMS, LearnDash, Kajabi, Teachable and Thinkific price in US dollars. Your cost then moves with the exchange rate while your students pay in rupees, and GST is not included.

    4. Self-serve or sales-led

    Teachmint and AppX start with a demo. Others let you sign up and build. Neither is wrong, but a self-serve trial lets you test with real students before you pay.

    How to choose and move to a new LMS

    The safe way to change LMS is to shortlist, test with real students, and run both platforms until the current batch ends.

    1. Shortlist two platforms by use caseUse the table above to pick two that match your main product, and note their price and fee at your sales level.
    2. Test one complete course in a trialUpload one course with its tests and a demo lesson, and enrol a few real students.
    3. Run both side by sideKeep current students on the old platform until their batch or validity ends.
    4. Move new admissions and close the old planSend new students to your new academy, export records, then let the old plan end at renewal.

    The full checklist, including messages to send students, is in how to switch coaching apps without losing students.

    Glossary

    Learning management system (LMS)
    Software that hosts courses, tests and live classes, enrols students and tracks progress.
    Platform fee
    A percentage a platform keeps from each sale. AllCoaching's is 0% on every monthly plan, and 10%, 7.5% or 5% on the yearly Starter, Growth or Pro plan, charged only when you sell.
    White-label app
    An Android or iOS app that carries your institute's name and logo, built on a platform's technology.
    Custom domain
    Your own web address, such as youracademy.in, connected to your academy website instead of a platform subdomain.
    Self-hosted LMS
    LMS software you install on your own hosting, such as a WordPress plugin or Moodle, where you run updates, security and payments.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which is the best LMS for a coaching institute in India in 2026?

    It depends on your use case. Learnyst suits test-heavy academies, Edmingle suits training businesses, Classplus suits institutes wanting a flat yearly price at higher sales, and AllCoaching suits institutes that want to build their own branded website and app themselves, with 0% commission on every monthly plan (from ₹3,999 a month), or Starter at ₹6,999 a year plus 10% of sales.

    What does an LMS cost for a coaching institute in India?

    On official pages checked 24 Oct 2026, entry prices range from no upfront fee with 10% commission (Exly Starter, TagMango Basic) to ₹6,999 a year plus 10% (AllCoaching), ₹10,000 a year plus 10% (Learnyst), ₹29,999 a year (Classplus) and ₹7,499 a month billed annually (Edmingle). Taxes are extra where stated.

    Which LMS platforms charge a fee on sales?

    AllCoaching (0% on monthly plans; 10%, 7.5% or 5% on yearly plans), Learnyst Launcher (10%), Exly (10%, 6% or 3%) and TagMango (10%, 5.5% or 3.5%, zero on Ultimate) publish a fee on sales. Learnyst's monthly plans list 0%. Classplus and Edmingle do not state a fee on their pricing pages.

    Which LMS includes a custom domain and own app on its entry plan?

    AllCoaching includes your own domain and your own Android app (iOS app on Pro or any yearly plan) on every plan. On Learnyst and Edmingle, branded apps start on higher plans; on Exly and TagMango, custom domain and apps are paid add-ons.

    Is a WordPress LMS like Tutor LMS or LearnDash good for a coaching institute?

    It can be, if you already run WordPress and have someone to maintain it. Tutor LMS has a free core and Pro plans from $199 a year; LearnDash bundles start at $350. Hosting, payment gateway, app and upkeep are separate.

    Is Moodle a good choice for a coaching institute?

    Moodle is open-source software you host yourself, so it suits institutes with technical staff. We did not price its hosting for this guide; budget for hosting, setup and maintenance.

    Which LMS is best for selling test series and mock tests?

    Learnyst is built around test series and mock tests. You can also run chapter and full-length tests inside an AllCoaching academy alongside courses and live classes.

    Will an LMS bring me more students?

    Mostly no. An LMS runs your courses; finding students stays your job through YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp and search. AllCoaching also lists your courses on its marketplace app for discovery, but that supports your own marketing rather than replacing it.
