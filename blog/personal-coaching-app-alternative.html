    There are four ways to give your students an app of your own: have an agency build a custom app, use an app-builder platform, use an all-in-one academy platform that includes your app and website, or run on WhatsApp and Telegram without an app. They differ most in what you pay before you earn, who can change the app after launch, and whether payments, live classes and tests come built in.

    Owning your app was never the problem. Owning one you cannot change, update or afford to maintain is. The right choice depends on how many students you have, how much you want to do yourself, and whether you need a website on your own domain as well.

    We are AllCoaching, so read this knowing who wrote it. We are one of the four options. Every other price below comes from the company's own website, with the link and the date we checked it (24 Oct 2026), and we say where another option fits better.

    Why educators look for an alternative to a custom-built app

    Most educators who search for an alternative already have an app, or a quote for one, and something about it is not working. The reasons usually fall into three groups.

    1. Every change needs a developer

    A custom app does what it was built to do on launch day. Adding a new batch type, changing a price page or fixing a bug after an Android update means going back to the developer, and paying again.

    2. The app is only one piece

    An app on its own does not take payments, host live classes, run tests or give you a website on your own domain unless each was built in. Educators end up running the app alongside WhatsApp, Zoom, Google Forms and UPI screenshots.

    Question educators ask

    Is building a personal coaching app a mistake?

    No. Having your own app is a strong asset for a coaching brand. The mistake is owning one you cannot change yourself, cannot afford to keep updated, or that runs without payments, tests and a website around it.

    3. Nobody downloads it

    An app does not bring students by itself. If downloads are the problem, a new app will not fix it; read why a coaching app gets no students first.

    Which option fits you

    You are starting your first batch

    Start on WhatsApp and YouTube, or build your academy inside a 14-day free trial. A spoken English trainer or a Class 10 maths tutor can test demand for free before paying. Our guide on how to sell courses online in India covers the first sale.

    You have paying students and want your own brand

    An all-in-one academy gives you your own app and website together. For a NEET biology educator or a UPSC mentor building a name, the website on your own domain matters as much as the app. See LMS with a custom domain.

    You run a large institute with steady sales

    A flat-price app builder can cost less above about ₹2.3 lakh a year. Ask whose account the app is under and whether iOS is included.

    You need something no platform does

    An agency-built app makes sense only here, and only with a maintenance contract and code ownership in writing.

    You already have an app and are deciding whether to move

    Money already spent should not decide the next step. Compare what the next year costs on each option. The own app or marketplace guide covers the trade-off.

    How to move from your current app without losing students

    The safe way to move is to build first and move students batch by batch. Current students keep the old app until their batch ends; new admissions go to the new one.

    1. List where your students come fromWrite down how your last 20 students found you: YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp, referrals or the app store.
    2. Decide what you must ownChoose which matter most: your own domain, your own Android app (iOS app on Pro and Scale) listing, or your student data export.
    3. Price each option at your sales levelAdd plan price, any add-on and any fee on sales at your real yearly sales, before GST.
    4. Get ownership terms in writingAsk each vendor whose account the app is under and what you can export if you leave.
    5. Build and test with one batchStart the 14-day free trial, connect your domain and run one paid batch on the new academy.
    6. Move the rest batch by batchKeep current students on the old app until their batch ends, and send all new admissions to your own academy.

    Glossary

    Personal coaching app
    An Android or iOS app that carries one educator's or institute's name, built by an agency or on a platform.
    White-label app
    An app that carries your brand's name and logo, built on a platform's technology.
    App-builder platform
    A vendor that sets up a branded app for you on its own technology, usually on a yearly plan.
    Developer account
    The Google Play or Apple account an app is published under. Whoever owns it controls the listing.
    Educator marketplace
    One app that lists many educators' courses so students can browse and buy from them.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best alternative to a personal coaching app?

    It depends on your stage. An all-in-one academy platform such as AllCoaching gives you your own Android app (iOS app on Pro and Scale) and website from ₹6,999 a year plus a fee on sales; an app builder such as Classplus lists ₹29,999 a year; WhatsApp and Telegram are free for a first batch; an agency-built app suits only unusual needs.

    Is building a personal coaching app a mistake?

    No. Your own app is a strong asset for a coaching brand. The mistake is owning one you cannot change yourself, cannot keep updated, or that runs without payments, tests and a website around it.

    How much does a personal coaching app cost in India?

    Agency-built apps have no standard price and are quoted by scope. On published prices, Classplus lists ₹29,999 a year with an Android app, Exly's app add-on starts at ₹60,000 a year, and AllCoaching includes Android app (iOS app on Pro and Scale) from ₹6,999 a year plus a fee on sales, GST extra.

    What is the difference between a white-label app and a marketplace?

    A white-label app carries your name and shows only your courses. A marketplace lists many educators in one app. AllCoaching gives you your own white-label app and also lists your courses on its marketplace app for discovery.

    Will a marketplace put me next to my competitors?

    On the marketplace app, yes, your courses appear alongside other educators. Your own website and your own app on AllCoaching show only your academy.

    Do I need an app at all to teach online in India?

    Not to start. Many educators run a first batch on YouTube and WhatsApp. An app of your own helps once students are paying and you want one place for classes, notes, tests and payments.

    Whose developer account is my app published under?

    It depends on the vendor, so ask in writing. Learnyst's pricing page says its apps are published under Learnyst's developer account; with an agency-built app, insist on your own account.

    Can I move from my current app without losing students?

    Yes. Build the new academy, keep current students on the old app until their batch ends, send new admissions to your own website and app, then export records and close the old app.
