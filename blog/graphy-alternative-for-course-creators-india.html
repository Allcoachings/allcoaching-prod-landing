    Why educators look for a Graphy alternative

    Graphy is a capable, well-known platform, and for many educators selling to an existing YouTube or Instagram audience it does the job well. The reasons people start comparing usually fall into three groups.

    1. Not being sure what you will pay in rupees

    When we opened Graphy's pricing page on 24 Oct 2026, it showed US dollar plans from our connection, and the rupee figures inside the page did not agree with each other. That does not mean anything is wrong with Graphy's pricing. It means you should see the rupee plan, the fee per sale and GST in writing before you compare.

    Question educators ask

    Is Graphy too expensive, or am I just not selling enough yet?

    Usually it is about stage, not the platform. A yearly plan feels heavy when sales are small and light when they grow. Ask Graphy for the rupee price and fee per sale for your plan, then compare the total at your real sales level, not the headline number.

    2. Wanting your own app on every plan

    Many educators now expect their own Android app (iOS app on Pro and Scale), under their own name, from day one. Check which Graphy plan includes the iOS app; on several platforms the app is a higher tier or a paid add-on.

    3. Wanting students to find you, not only buy from you

    A course platform gives you a place to sell. Bringing buyers is still your job: your YouTube channel, Instagram, WhatsApp groups and Google search. Graphy's pricing page does not describe a built-in student marketplace, so ask whether one exists. We cover this angle in detail in our guide to a Graphy alternative with organic traffic.

    How to switch from Graphy without losing students

    The safe way to switch is to run both platforms side by side. Current students keep access on Graphy until their batch or validity ends; new students join your new academy.

    1. Build your new academyStart the 14-day trial, connect your domain and upload one complete course with a free demo lesson.
    2. Run both platforms side by sideKeep current students on Graphy until their batch or course validity ends, so nobody loses access.
    3. Send new students to your academyPut your own website and app link in your YouTube descriptions, Instagram bio and WhatsApp groups.
    4. Export records and close the old planDownload student and payment records from Graphy, then let the plan end at its renewal date.

    Glossary

    Platform fee
    A percentage a platform keeps from each sale. AllCoaching's is 10%, 7.5% or 5% depending on the plan.
    Commission
    Another name for a fee on each sale, used by platforms such as Exly and TagMango.
    White-label app
    An Android or iOS app that carries your academy's name and logo, built on a platform's technology.
    Custom domain
    Your own web address, such as youracademy.in, connected to your academy website instead of a platform subdomain.
    Parallel run
    Keeping the old and new platforms active at the same time during a switch, so current students keep access.
