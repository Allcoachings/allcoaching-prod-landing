    The short answer: three paths for a K-12 teacher

    A K-12 teacher who wants to move beyond Toppr or Extramarks has three paths: teach for an online platform, keep using a school product where your school chooses it, or build a tuition academy under your own name. Pick by what you want more of: steady pay, or your own brand.

    Many teachers combine paths. They keep a teaching job or platform income and build a small academy of their own on the side, so the new income grows before the old one stops.

    Why K-12 teachers look beyond Toppr and Extramarks

    Most teachers who search for a Toppr or Extramarks alternative want the same thing: students and parents who know them by name and pay them directly. Big K-12 brands built national reach, and that took capital, content teams and sales teams no single teacher can match. The question is what fits you now.

    1. Toppr cannot be checked today

    When we tried toppr.com on 24 Oct 2026, the site did not respond.[1] We therefore do not describe Toppr's current product, prices or teacher arrangements here. If you teach with Toppr or bought from it, ask it in writing for current terms before planning around them.

    2. Extramarks is built for schools

    Extramarks describes "Cutting-Edge Solutions for Schools, Teachers, Students, and Parents": smart class, an assessment centre and a learning app.[2] If your school uses it, it can be a useful classroom tool. But its homepage lists no programme for independent educators and no prices, so it is not a way to sell your own tuition.

    Question educators ask

    I teach in a school. Can I sell my own tuition online?

    Often yes, but check first. Your school, and the board your school follows, may have rules about private tuition, especially for students you teach in class. Read your appointment letter and school policy before you sell. Many school teachers teach students from other schools, or teach a different class level, online.

    3. Wanting your own name on your teaching

    A parent choosing tuition looks for a few things: a syllabus that matches the board and class, a sample lesson, test results that improve, and a teacher they can name. When the teaching sits inside a large brand, the brand gets the credit. When it sits in your own academy, the next admission comes to you.

    What a direct-to-student K-12 academy should contain

    A K-12 academy that parents trust needs four things: lessons that follow the board's chapter order, tests that show progress, a regular live doubt class with you, and clear fees. Build these first; everything else can wait.

    Chapter-wise lessons in your own words

    Record lessons chapter by chapter, in the order your board follows (CBSE, ICSE or your state board), and in the language your students learn in. Short videos per topic and a PDF note per chapter are easier to sell than one long course. Our guide to an online tuition app for Class 10 and 12 CBSE teachers covers board-wise course plans.

    Chapter tests and full-syllabus tests

    A parent sees value in a score that improves. Add a short test after each chapter and a full-syllabus test before exams. See how to create mock tests online.

    Doubt solving under your own name

    A weekly live doubt class, on YouTube Live, Zoom or the built-in live room, is where students get to know you. On AllCoaching, live classes are unlimited on every plan.

    Fees parents can pay in a minute

    Monthly, term-wise or full-year fees with UPI or card checkout, and access that starts the moment the fee is paid. In AllCoaching this runs on its own, with automatic payouts through Razorpay.

    Which path fits you

    School teacher with a few evening hours

    Start small with one subject and one class level, after checking your school's rules on private tuition. A self-serve academy with a 7-day trial lets you test demand before paying much. See side income from online coaching for school teachers.

    Home tutor for classes 6 to 10

    Parents in your area already know you; move them online with your own website and app, then add students from other cities. Building a recognisable name matters here: see personal branding for educators.

    Class 11 and 12 teacher with board plus JEE or NEET foundation

    Test series carry more weight. Build chapter tests and full mocks into your academy, and price board and entrance batches separately.

    Teacher on a platform who wants a brand later

    Keep the platform income while you build a small academy on the side, if your contract allows it. Shift the balance only when your own students are enough.

    Tuition centre with several teachers

    Look for separate teacher logins and batch ownership. AllCoaching includes multi-teacher institutes on every plan; see running a multi-teacher coaching platform.

    Glossary

    K-12
    School education from kindergarten to class 12. In India this covers primary, secondary and senior secondary classes under CBSE, ICSE and state boards.
    Direct-to-student tuition
    Tuition where students or parents pay the teacher directly, through the teacher's own website or app, rather than through a larger brand.
    Chapter-wise course
    A course split into one module per chapter of the board syllabus, so students can buy or study the part they need.
    White-label app
    An Android or iOS app that carries your academy's name and logo, built on a platform's technology.
    Platform fee
    A percentage a platform keeps from each sale. AllCoaching's is 0% on Growth and Pro, and 10% on Starter, charged only when you sell.
