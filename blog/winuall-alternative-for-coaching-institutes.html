    What to secure before you choose a new platform

    Before comparing platforms, collect everything that belongs to your institute. Most of it you can gather even if the old app no longer opens.

    1. Student list. Export it from the old admin panel if it still opens. If not, rebuild it from admission forms, fee receipts and your batch WhatsApp groups: name, phone, batch, course and validity end date.
    2. Payment records. Download statements from your payment gateway account and bank so you know who paid for what, and until when.
    3. Original content. Find the source files of your recorded lectures, PDF notes and question banks on your computer, Google Drive or YouTube. Do not rely on copies inside the old app.
    4. Your domain. Log in to the registrar where your web address was bought and check it is registered in your name. If it is, you can point it to your new academy.
    5. Your app listing. Check whether your Android app was published under your own Google Play developer account or the vendor's. If it was the vendor's, plan for students to install a new app.

    How to move from Winuall without losing students

    The safe way to move is to rebuild first, tell students clearly, and move batch by batch while honouring every paid validity.

    1. Secure your dataExport or rebuild your student list, download payment records, and collect original videos, PDFs and question banks.
    2. Check your domain and app listingConfirm the domain is registered in your name, and find out whose Google Play account your app was published under.
    3. Rebuild your academyStart the 14-day trial, connect your domain, add teachers and upload one complete course per batch.
    4. Tell students with a dateSend one message to every batch group with the new link, the switch date and how their paid validity carries over.
    5. Move batch by batchEnrol each batch in the new academy with its remaining validity, starting with the smallest batch as a pilot.
    6. Open new admissions on your academySend all new students to your own website and app, and stop using the old app once the last batch has moved.

    Two rules make this calm. First, never ask a student to pay twice: carry their remaining validity over to the new academy. Second, give one clear date and one link, and repeat it in every batch group. The full checklist, including message templates, is in how to switch coaching apps without losing students.

    Glossary

    Data export
    Downloading your student, payment and content records from a platform so you can keep them or move them.
    Google Play developer account
    The account an Android app is published under. Whoever owns it controls the app listing.
    Custom domain
    Your own web address, such as youracademy.in, which you can point to any platform if it is registered in your name.
    Course validity
    The period a student has paid to access a course or batch. Carry it over in full when you move.
    Parallel run
    Keeping the old and new platforms active at the same time during a move, so current students keep access.
