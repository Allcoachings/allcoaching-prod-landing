    The short answer: pick by what you need

    There is no single best Exly or Rigi alternative. There is a best fit for each kind of coaching business, so start from what you want to change.

    Why coaches look for an Exly or Rigi alternative

    Exly is a well-built tool for selling to an audience you already have, with a storefront, payments, CRM and WhatsApp and email automation. The reasons coaches compare usually fall into three groups.

    1. Rigi has changed direction

    When we checked rigi.club on 24 Oct 2026, it presented "Rigi TV - Instant Entertainment", a short-drama video app, with no coach or course product shown.[2] Whether Rigi's earlier coach product still runs elsewhere, we could not verify. If you sold through Rigi, export your client list and plan a new home.

    2. Paying extra for your own app and website

    Exly lists its mobile app (iOS and Android) as an add-on from ₹60,000 a year, a custom website from ₹60,000 a year and white-labelled branding from ₹20,000 a year. For a coach who wants their own brand everywhere, these add up quickly. Ask for the full yearly total for the plan and add-ons you need.

    Question educators ask

    Exly's Starter plan has no upfront fee. Why would I pay a yearly plan?

    You may not need to. If you sell sessions to followers through a link in your bio and do not need your own app, Exly Starter can cost less. A yearly plan starts to make sense when you want your own Android and iOS app and website included, or when your sales grow enough that a lower percentage saves more than the yearly price.

    3. Moving from sessions to structured courses

    Many coaches start with one-off sessions and later want recorded courses, live batches, PDF notes and tests in one place, inside an app their students install. That is the shape of an academy, not a storefront. A yoga teacher moving from drop-in classes to a 12-week programme, or a spoken English coach adding a test at the end of each level, often reaches this point.

    How to switch from Exly or Rigi without losing clients

    The safe way to switch is to run both platforms side by side. Current clients finish their sessions or cohorts where they are; new sales go to your new academy.

    1. Export your client and payment recordsDownload your client list, purchase history and content from Exly or Rigi before anything else.
    2. Build your academyStart the 14-day trial, connect your domain and set up one course or programme with a free demo lesson.
    3. Run both side by sideLet current clients finish their sessions or cohorts on the old platform.
    4. Move new sales to your academyChange the link in your Instagram bio, YouTube descriptions and WhatsApp messages to your own website and app.
    5. Close the old planEnd the old plan once the last cohort finishes and no payments are pending.

    The full checklist, including messages to send students, is in how to switch coaching apps without losing students.

    Glossary

    Commission
    A percentage of each sale that a platform keeps. Exly lists 10%, 6% or 3% by plan; AllCoaching's platform fee is 10%, 7.5% or 5%.
    Add-on
    A feature sold separately from the plan, such as Exly's mobile app or custom website.
    Link-in-bio selling
    Selling sessions or products through a single link placed in an Instagram or YouTube profile.
    White-label app
    An Android or iOS app that carries your brand's name and logo, built on a platform's technology.
    Parallel run
    Keeping the old and new platforms active at the same time during a switch, so current clients keep access.
