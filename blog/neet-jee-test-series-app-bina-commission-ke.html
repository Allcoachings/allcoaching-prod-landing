Reframe

"Bina commission ke" ka
asli matlab.

Apni coaching ka NEET ya JEE test series app bina commission ke kaise banaye? Sabse honest jawab hai: ek aisa app jo aapki test series ko aapke apne naam se bechta hai, kisi aggregator ke brand ke neeche nahi — jisme sectional tests, full-length mocks with All India rank, PYQ bank aur detailed solutions sab ek jagah ho, Rs 0 upfront me, flat 10% sirf sales par, 90% aapka rakhna. Lekin sirf "app kaunsa" poochna is decision ko chota kar deta hai. Asli sawal yeh nahi hai ki kaunsa app use karein — asli sawal hai ki test series bikti kiske naam se hai.

Bohot se NEET/JEE teachers ek galat assumption ke saath shuru karte hain: ki test series bechne ke liye kisi bade aggregator platform par jaana padega, jo apna commission le lega — aur "commission" ko woh ek unavoidable cost maan lete hain, jaise UPI ya Razorpay ka processing fee. Yeh galat hai. Ek payment gateway ka processing fee 1-2% hota hai, transaction ko possible banane ke liye. Ek aggregator ka commission alag cheez hai — 40-70% jaisa hissa, aapki test series ko us aggregator ke brand ke naam se bechne ke badle, jisme aapka naam student tak kabhi pahunchta hi nahi.

Yeh guide is confusion ko clear karti hai. Ek strong NEET ya JEE test series product me kya hona chahiye, dono exams ek hi app se kaise bikte hain, aggregator commission ka structure kaise kaam karta hai, honest illustrative economics, aur ek weekend ka launch roadmap — sab apne naam se, bina kisi aur ko credit diye.

Commission ka structure

Aggregator commission asal me
kaam kaise karti hai.

Test-series aggregator ya reseller-style apps ka model do shapes me aata hai. Pehla: revenue-share — aggregator apki test series apne catalogue me list karta hai, apne existing users ko bechta hai, aur collection ka ek bada hissa (aksar 40-70% range me, platform se platform alag) apne paas rakhta hai, aapko baaki deta hai. Doosra: outright content-buyout — aggregator aapse content ek fixed, aksar chhoti, one-time price par khareed leta hai, phir usse jitna chahe utna bech sakta hai, jitni bhi revenue bane, sab uski.

Dono shapes me ek common problem hai: aapka naam kabhi student tak nahi pahunchta. Student ko lagta hai woh "XYZ aggregator ki test series" kharid raha hai, na ki "aapki test series" — jabki solutions aapne likhe, questions aapne curate kiye, aur teaching quality aapki hai. Isse do nuksaan hote hain: pehla, aapki revenue share choti ho jaati hai; doosra, jo bhi trust student ne aapke content se banaya, woh aggregator ke brand me chala jaata hai — agla product bhi aggregator hi bechta hai, aapka apna brand kabhi nahi banta.

Commission ka asli hisaab yeh nahi hai ki kitna % katega — asli hisaab yeh hai ki jab student agli baar test series khareedega, woh kiska naam yaad rakhega. Aggregator model me, kabhi aapka nahi.

Product

Ek strong test series me
kya hona chahiye.

Test series product ke paanch layers hain. Sectional tests — har chapter aur topic ke liye alag, jisse student apni weak areas identify kar sake, exam ke fikar se pehle. Full-length mocks with negative marking aur All India rank — exam-pattern ka poora paper, jo real attempt jaisa lage aur ek position de, jaisa interactive mock tests online banane ka craft demand karta hai. PYQ bank — pichle saalon ke actual questions, organize karke, jo students exam-relevance ke liye sabse zyada dhoondte hain.

Chautha layer sabse important hai: detailed, step-by-step solutions jo sirf sahi answer nahi batate, balki galat option kyu galat hai woh bhi explain karte hain — yahi teaching hai, aur yahi cheez hai jo koi generic aggregator apni scale par utni acchi tarah nahi de paata jitni ek subject-focused teacher de sakta hai. Paanchwa layer measurement hai: section-wise accuracy aur rank movement, jaisa student progress tracking aur analytics tools me detail se cover kiya gaya hai.

Question Often Asked

Kya main content-writing me weak hu to bhi apni test series bana sakta hu?

Haan — content-writing aur teaching alag skills hain, aur test series me teaching wali skill zyada matter karti hai. Questions PYQ se ya standard reference books se curate kiye ja sakte hain (jo legally publicly available hain); jo cheez aapki hai woh hai explanation likhne ka tareeka — kaise ek concept ko step-by-step break karke samjhaya jaaye. Agar aap classroom me acha samjhate hain, wahi skill likhit solutions me bhi transfer hoti hai, thoda practice ke saath. Shuruaat me 20-30 questions ke solutions likhkar dekhein — yeh muscle jaldi ban jaati hai.

Dono exams

NEET aur JEE,
ek hi app se.

NEET aur JEE alag subjects test karte hain — NEET Biology, Physics, Chemistry par focused hai; JEE Physics, Chemistry aur Maths par — lekin product ka structure same hai: sectional tests, full mocks with rank, PYQ, detailed solutions. Isliye agar aap dono exams ke liye content bana sakte hain (jaise Physics/Chemistry dono me common hai), ek hi branded studio par NEET series alag product ki tarah aur JEE series alag product ki tarah list kar sakte hain, ek hi student account, ek hi checkout aur ek hi payout ke saath — jaisa NEET coaching platform ke liye best app guide me full coaching ke liye discuss kiya gaya hai, yahan sirf test series ke focused product ke liye.

Multiple subjects ya multiple exams akela handle karna mushkil lage to multi-teacher support free-tier me included hai — ek colleague ko dusre exam ya subject ke liye add kar sakte hain, ek hi brand ke neeche. Yeh option boutique test-series practices ke liye especially useful hai jaha ek teacher NEET Biology me strong hai aur doosra JEE Maths me.

Economics

Illustrative economics:
flat 10% vs commission.

Illustratively — promise nahi: Rs 499 ki ek test series 300 students ko bechi jaaye to lagbhag Rs 1.5 lakh collect hote hain, jisme se aap flat 10% ke baad lagbhag Rs 1.35 lakh (90%) rakhte hain. Isi test series ko agar ek aggregator 50% revenue-share model par bechta, jo publicly reported models me common range hai, aapko usi Rs 1.5 lakh me se sirf lagbhag Rs 75,000 milte — half, sirf ek aur brand ke naam ke liye. Ek season me do test series (NEET + JEE) chalayein to yeh farak double ho jaata hai.

Structural point yeh hai: AllCoaching ka 10% sirf sale ke saath aata hai, koi fixed cost neeche nahi baitha hota. Koi subscription off-season ko nuksaan nahi pahunchati, koi seat-based pricing doosre teacher ko punish nahi karti — platform tabhi kamaata hai jab aap kamaate hain, jaisa bina monthly subscription ke online courses bechne ki discipline me argue kiya gaya hai. Apni series ko uske value ke hisaab se price karna, aggregator ke discount-driven pricing ke hisaab se nahi, online courses price karne ke guide me cover hai.

Question Often Asked

Agar platform sirf 10% leta hai to catch kya hai?

Model alignment aur volume par chalta hai, kisi ek educator se bade margin par nahi — platform tabhi kamaata hai jab aap actual me kamaate hain, jo ek aggregator ke revenue-share model se bilkul alag incentive hai jo kisi bhi haal me apna bada hissa le leta hai. Ek platform jo 10% sales par kamaata hai, sirf ek tareeke se grow karta hai: zyada teachers ko zyada bechne me madad karke — isliye test tools, discovery aur marketplace layer enrolments badhane ke liye hote hain, kisi brand ko protect karne ke liye nahi. Disclosed guardrails: storage aur bandwidth par fair-use limits, aur normal batch usage se zyada live streaming par pay-per-use. Jo nahi hai: koi content-buyout, koi 40-70% revenue-share, ya aapke questions aur students ka ownership. Craft aapki rehti hai; platform sirf uske saath kamaata hai.

Verdict

Is guide ka verdict.

To — apni coaching ka NEET ya JEE test series app bina commission ke kaise banaye? Ek aisa app jo aapki mehnat — sectional tests, full mocks, PYQ, detailed solutions — apne naam se bechta hai, kisi aggregator ke 40-70% commission ke bina, ₹0 upfront me. AllCoaching par questions, solutions aur students aapke rehte hain; rank aur results teaching prove karte hain; aur platform sirf flat 10% kamaata hai jab student actual me pay karta hai. Jab aapka naam student ke saath jud jaata hai, agla product bhi aapke naam se hi bikta hai — kisi aur brand ka nahi.

Jin NEET/JEE teachers ne is transition ko acche se kiya hai, unme ek pattern dikhta hai:

  • Woh solutions par sabse zyada time dete hain — questions to aggregator ke paas bhi hote hain, aacha explanation nahi.
  • Woh dono exams handle karte hain agar skill hai — ek hi brand ke neeche NEET aur JEE dono products.
  • Woh free sectional test se shuru karte hain — proof pehle, sale baad me.
  • Woh apna naam bar-bar repeat karte hain — students ko yaad rehna chahiye kiski test series thi.

Test simple hai: jab student agli baar test series khareedega, kiska naam yaad rakhega — aapka, ya kisi aggregator ka? studio.allcoaching.in par jaayein, is weekend pehla sectional test publish karein, aur apni mehnat par apna naam lagayein.

Glossary

Glossary —
key terms.

Term

Sectional Test

Ek chapter ya topic-specific test jo students ki understanding ek narrow area me check karta hai — full mock se pehle weak areas identify karne ka tool.

Term

Full-Length Mock

Exam-pattern ka poora paper, negative marking aur time-limit ke saath, jo ek real NEET ya JEE attempt jaisa experience deta hai aur All India rank generate karta hai.

Term

PYQ (Previous Year Questions)

Pichle saalon ke actual exam questions ka organized set. Publicly available hote hain, lekin unke detailed solutions me hi asli teaching value hoti hai.

Term

All India Rank

Ek mock test attempt karne wale sabhi students ke beech marks ke hisaab se generate hui rank list — students ki exam-readiness ka comparative signal.

Term

Aggregator Commission

Wo revenue-share ya buyout jo ek test-series aggregator app educator se leta hai apna content us aggregator ke brand ke naam se bechne ke badle — educator ka naam student tak nahi pahunchta.

Term

Branded Test Series

Ek test series jo educator ke apne naam aur brand ke saath bikti hai, na ki kisi aggregator ya marketplace ke naam se — student seedha educator ko trust karta hai.

Term

Marketplace Discovery

AllCoaching ka AI-driven system jisse NEET ya JEE search karne wale students educator ko organically dhoondte hain, unke apne naam se, bina paid ads ke.

Term

Keep-Rate

Har sale me se educator ka hissa platform fee ke baad. AllCoaching par yeh 90% hai, sirf flat 10% paid sales par, koi upfront fee nahi.
