    The short answer: partner, sell your own, or both

    An educator looking for a Testbook alternative has three real options: partner with Testbook, sell your own mock tests under your name, or do both. Partnering suits a question setter who wants marketing handled. Selling your own suits an educator with an audience, or a plan to build one, who wants the brand and the student list.

    SSC CGL, IBPS PO and RRB NTPC aspirants buy a lot of mocks, and they follow educators whose questions feel like the real paper. That is why so many educators want their own series.

    Why educators look for a Testbook alternative

    Educators who search for a Testbook alternative usually want their own name on the tests they write. Here is what Testbook offers, and why educators also build their own.

    What Testbook offers educators

    Testbook focuses on government-exam preparation with mock tests, live classes and the Testbook Pass, a student subscription.[2] Its Partner Faculty page says Testbook takes care of "marketing, promotions & technology", with telesales and DTP support.[1] For an educator who wants to write and teach and let someone else sell, that is a real offer.

    1. Your name on the paper

    Aspirants remember who set the paper that felt closest to the real exam. When the series carries your name, that memory brings them back for your next course.

    2. Your own price and bundles

    You may want a ₹199 sectional quant pack, a ₹499 full SSC CGL series and a combined banking bundle. On your own academy you decide each price.

    3. A direct line to your aspirants

    When results come out, you want to tell your own students about the mains series. On your own academy the student list is yours and exportable.

    Question educators ask

    If Testbook already has the aspirants, isn't my own series just extra work?

    It is more work, because you have to bring the aspirants. It pays back when you already have them, for example a YouTube channel where you solve SSC maths daily. If you have no audience yet, partnering first and building on the side can be the better order.

    What selling your own mock tests needs

    A real test series is more than a PDF. Before you choose any platform, check it supports: sectional and full-length mocks, a timer per section, negative marking, detailed solutions, and a result screen students understand.     Glossary

    Mock test
    A practice paper that copies the real exam's pattern, timing and marking.
    Sectional mock
    A mock test for one section of an exam, such as quantitative aptitude in SSC CGL.
    Negative marking
    A rule that deducts marks for a wrong answer, used in SSC, banking and railway exams.
    Partner faculty
    An educator who creates content or teaches for a brand under its programme, on the brand's terms.
    Test series
    A planned set of mock tests sold together, usually with solutions and results after each test.
