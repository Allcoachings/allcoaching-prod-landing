The real difference

The model,
not the list.

Two platforms can have near-identical feature lists and still be completely different businesses to use. Teachmint is a tool you pay for: a subscription (plus setup and add-ons) that you commit to before — and regardless of whether — your revenue arrives. That is a perfectly reasonable model, and for an established institute with predictable income it is fine. The risk lands on you only if your earnings have not yet caught up with the cost.

AllCoaching is a revenue-share marketplace: ₹0 upfront, 10% on paid earnings, 90% to you, daily payouts. The platform earns only when you do, so the risk is shared, not front-loaded. And because it is a marketplace, not just a tool, it carries something a subscription tool structurally cannot — demand. This is the tool-versus-ecosystem distinction at the heart of how the marketplace model solves discovery: a tool stores and delivers your teaching; an ecosystem also helps an audience find it.

A tool charges you to be ready for students. A marketplace charges you only after students arrive — and helps them arrive.

For new educators especially, the combination of a free premium stack plus marketplace discovery removes both the cost risk and the cold-start problem at once. The same logic applies whether you are weighing this as a zero-commission platform question or a subscription-versus-share one.

The model that charges you before you earn and the model that charges you after are simply not the same financial product.

Glossary

Key terms
in this guide.

Term

White-Label App

A coaching app branded entirely with the educator's name, logo, and colours, where students see the educator's brand rather than the platform's. Both Teachmint paid plans and AllCoaching's free studio offer white-labeling.

Term

Custom Domain

The educator's own web address, such as yourcoaching.in. It is a paid-tier feature on most platforms including AllCoaching; not required to launch, since a branded studio runs on a shareable platform link.

Term

Revenue Share Model

A monetisation model where the platform charges only when the educator earns — no upfront fee, no subscription. On AllCoaching it is a 10% revenue-share on paid earnings; the educator keeps 90%, paid out daily.

Term

Subscription / SaaS Model

A model where the educator pays a recurring fee (and often setup and add-on costs) to use the software, regardless of how much they earn. It front-loads cost and risk onto the educator before any revenue arrives.

Term

Branded Studio

An educator's own name-and-logo coaching space that works as both a website and a mobile app, where content, payments, and students are managed. On AllCoaching the studio is white-labeled even on the free tier.

Term

Marketplace Discovery

AllCoaching's AI-driven, multi-educator marketplace where students search by subject, exam, or language and are matched to educators organically — a source of students that a pure software tool does not provide.

Term

Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

The real all-in cost of a platform over time — subscription plus setup, custom domain, payment commission, add-ons, and migration — not just the advertised monthly price. TCO is how a "low monthly" plan can reach lakhs in a year.

Term

Vendor Lock-in

The difficulty and cost of leaving a platform once content, students, and payments are tied to it, often reinforced by annual contracts. A ₹0-upfront, no-lock-in model lets an educator test and leave freely.
