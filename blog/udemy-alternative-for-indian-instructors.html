    The short answer: keep the reach, own the relationship

    Udemy and your own academy do different jobs. Udemy brings learners who are already shopping on Udemy. Your own academy is where students who know your name buy directly from you, at your price, and stay in your student list. Pick by what you need next.

    The next sections show how we compared the options, what Udemy is good at, and what each own-academy platform costs.

    How we compared Udemy and the alternatives

    Because Udemy is a marketplace and the others are platforms you pay for, we compared the instructor's options rather than features alone. We looked at five things.

    1. How you earn: a marketplace's instructor terms, or a published plan price plus fee on sales.
    2. Who sets the price students pay.
    3. What you own: your brand, domain, app listing and student contact list.
    4. Checkout for Indian students: rupee pricing and UPI.
    5. Where students come from: the platform's audience, or your own YouTube, Instagram and WhatsApp.

    Platform prices come only from each company's own pricing page. For Udemy we used only official Udemy pages; where they could not be retrieved, we say so instead of repeating figures from other websites.

    Why Indian instructors look beyond Udemy

    Udemy works, and for many instructors it is where the first sale happens. Instructors usually start looking beyond it once they have some audience of their own.

    What Udemy does well

    Udemy is a global course marketplace where instructors publish courses to Udemy's existing learner base.[1] That is its real strength: a new instructor with expertise but no following can put a course in front of buyers without building an audience first. No software bill, no website to run, no payment setup.

    1. Wanting your own price

    On your own academy, you decide what a course costs and when to offer a discount, whether that is ₹499 for an SSC maths crash course or ₹4,999 for a full-year batch. On a marketplace, pricing works within the marketplace's rules. Read Udemy's own pricing and promotions policy for instructors so you know exactly how it applies to your courses. Our guide on how to price online courses in India covers rupee pricing.

    2. Wanting to teach the same students again

    A teaching business grows on repeat students: the learner who took your spoken-English basics comes back for interview practice. Check what student information Udemy's instructor terms let you receive and how you may contact learners. On your own academy, the student list is yours and you can export it.

    Question educators ask

    If Udemy brings me students, why does owning them matter?

    Because a single sale is not a business. When students buy from your own academy, you can tell them about your next batch, test series or live class directly. Udemy can still bring new learners; your academy keeps the ones who already trust you.

    3. Selling to Indian students in rupees

    Many Indian students and parents pay by UPI and think in rupees. An academy with rupee prices and UPI checkout, on an app with your name on it, fits that buyer. Our guide to the best UPI payment gateway for online courses explains why the checkout matters.

    Udemy, your own academy, or both?

    New instructor with no audience yet

    Udemy's built-in learners are a real head start. Publish there, and start collecting an audience on YouTube or Instagram at the same time. Our guide on getting your first 500 students covers that early phase.

    Instructor with a YouTube or Instagram following

    Send your own audience to your own academy, where you set the price and keep the student list. Keep Udemy for learners who find you there. See how to earn from a YouTube teaching channel with your own app.

    Exam educator teaching Indian students (SSC, banking, NEET, CA, UPSC)

    Your students pay in rupees, often by UPI, and want live classes, tests and notes for a specific exam. An academy with your own app fits this best. See how to sell courses online in India.

    Skill instructor selling worldwide (coding, design, marketing)

    Keep Udemy for global buyers. Add your own academy for Indian students and for your most loyal learners, under your own name; see building a personal brand as an educator.

    How to start your own academy without losing Udemy

    You do not need to delete anything. The safe path is to add your own academy beside Udemy, and let the numbers decide how much moves over.

    1. Build your academyStart the 7-day trial, connect your domain and upload one complete course, ideally a new or extended version of your best Udemy course.

    Glossary

    Course marketplace
    A platform that lists many instructors' courses for its own learners, such as Udemy, and sets the terms for selling there.
    Revenue share
    The part of each sale an instructor receives, or a platform keeps, under the platform's terms.
    Own academy
    A course website and app under your own name and domain, where you set prices and keep the student list.
    Platform fee
    A percentage a platform keeps from each sale. AllCoaching's is 0% on every monthly plan, and 10%, 7.5% or 5% on the yearly Starter, Growth or Pro plan, charged only when you sell.
    White-label app
    An Android or iOS app that carries your academy's name and logo, built on a platform's technology.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best Udemy alternative for Indian instructors?

    For most instructors, the best alternative is their own academy run alongside Udemy. AllCoaching fits instructors who want their own website, Android app (iOS app on Pro or any yearly plan) and UPI checkout in rupees, with 0% commission on every monthly plan from ₹3,999 a month, or yearly from ₹6,999 plus a 10% fee on sales. Learnyst suits test-heavy academies, Exly suits coaches selling sessions, and Graphy suits creators with an audience.

    How much does Udemy pay instructors?

    Udemy's instructor revenue share is set by its own terms. Its official revenue-share page could not be retrieved on 24 Oct 2026, so we do not quote a percentage here. Read it on Udemy's support site before you compare.

    Does Udemy own my students?

    Udemy's terms decide what student information you receive and how you may contact learners; check them directly. On your own academy, the student list belongs to you and you can export it.

    Can I set my own price instead of marketplace pricing?

    Yes, on your own academy you set every price and discount yourself. On Udemy, pricing works within Udemy's own rules, so read its pricing and promotions policy for instructors.

    Will I lose discovery if I leave Udemy?

    If you leave completely, you lose Udemy's built-in audience, so most instructors keep Udemy and add their own academy. AllCoaching also lists your courses on its marketplace app, which helps discovery, but it does not replace Udemy's global reach.

    Can I move my Udemy course to my own academy?

    You can upload your own course videos and notes to your academy. Check Udemy's instructor terms on exclusivity and on promoting outside Udemy before you publish the same course in both places.

    Is AllCoaching only for exam coaching?

    No. Exam educators (NEET, JEE, SSC, banking, UPSC) and skill instructors (spoken English, coding, yoga, music, marketing) can both use the same tools: courses, tests, notes, unlimited live classes and their own app.

    How long does it take to start my own academy?

    You can start the 7-day free trial, connect your domain and upload a first course on the same day. Allow a few weeks to move your own audience to it while Udemy keeps running.
