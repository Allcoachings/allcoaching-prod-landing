    AppX vs Classplus: the short answer

    Pick AppX if you want a team to set up your app and website for you and you are comfortable getting the price in a demo. Pick Classplus if you want a listed yearly price and an established coaching app. Pick a self-serve academy if you want to build and change everything yourself.

    None of these is the right answer for everyone. The sections below show what each company publishes, what it leaves out, and how to compare the two quotes you will get.

    What AppX and Classplus each offer, in their own words

    AppX and Classplus solve the same problem, getting a coaching business online under its own name, but they describe themselves differently.

    AppX: a branded app and website, set up for you

    AppX says: "Launch your own branded app and website for your courses and community. No code needed." Its site describes branded apps on Android, iOS and desktop, DRM security for videos and low-latency live streaming.[2]

    Its clearest strength is speed with little effort from you. The homepage says "Setup in under 48 hours", and its FAQ says "It takes 3-5 days to deliver your finished application and website". For an owner who never wants to touch settings, a team that builds it for you is real value.

    Classplus: a coaching app and website with a listed yearly price

    Classplus says: "Get your own brand's teaching app, create your own website & sell courses". Its plan lists an Android app, a website, an admin portal, unlimited recorded courses, live classes, student enrolments and storage, plus "A.I. Powered Leads".[1]

    Classplus is one of the better-known names in Indian coaching apps, and it now publishes its price: ₹29,999 for one year and ₹39,999 for two years, shown against struck-through prices of ₹34,999 and ₹44,999.

    Question educators ask

    Is AppX a one-time payment?

    AppX does not say on its website. It lists no price and no plan table, and pricing starts with a free personalised demo. Ask in writing whether the quote is a one-time build fee, a yearly renewal or both, what the renewal costs, and what happens to your app and website if you stop paying.

    The same question applies to Classplus. Its pricing page has an FAQ titled "What are the renewal charges?", but the answer was not in the page when we checked. Ask for it.

    What to ask AppX and Classplus before you sign

    Ask both companies the same questions and get the answers in writing. That turns two demos into two quotes you can actually compare.

    1. What is the total price for year one, including GST and any setup fee?
    2. What does renewal cost in year two and year three?
    3. Is any percentage or fee taken on my course sales? Ask about payment gateway charges separately.
    4. Are both the Android and the iOS app included, and under whose developer account are they published?
    5. Can I connect my own domain to the website?
    6. Who makes changes after launch: can I edit courses, prices and pages myself, or do I raise a request?
    7. If I stop paying, what happens to the app, the website and my student data? Can I export students, payments and content?

    We ask ourselves the same questions. Our answers are on the pricing page: published plan prices with 0% commission on every monthly plan, every plan includes your own domain and your own Android app (iOS app on Pro or any yearly plan), and if you stop you get 30 days to export your data.

    AppX or Classplus: which fits your coaching

    You want it done for you

    If you teach full time and do not want to learn any software, AppX's done-for-you setup is its main advantage. Budget time for the demo and get the quote, renewal and change process in writing.

    You are an established institute with steady sales

    If you already sell well above ₹2.3 lakh a year, Classplus's flat listed price can cost less than a percentage model. Confirm GST, fees on sales and renewal first. Our guide to the best LMS for coaching institutes covers more options at this size.

    You are starting out or growing (under ₹2 lakh a year)

    A low fixed price and a free trial matter more than anything else here. A self-serve academy lets you build, test with one batch and sell before paying much. Start with how to sell courses online in India.

    You want your own domain and brand above all

    For an SSC maths educator growing on YouTube, or a yoga teacher selling a 30-day course, the academy on your own domain is the asset that lasts. See LMS with a custom domain.

    How to move from AppX or Classplus later without losing students

    Whichever you choose now, you can move later without losing students if you run both platforms side by side. Current students stay where they are until their batch ends; new admissions go to the new academy.

    1. Build your new academyStart the 7-day trial, connect your domain and upload one complete course with a free demo lesson.
    2. Run both platforms side by sideKeep current students on AppX or Classplus until their batch or validity ends, so nobody loses access.
    3. Send new admissions to your academyShare your own website and app link on WhatsApp, YouTube and Instagram for every new batch.

    Glossary

    White-label app
    An Android or iOS app that carries your coaching brand's name and logo, built on a platform's technology.
    Done-for-you setup
    A setup where the vendor's team builds your app and website for you, usually after a demo, instead of you building it yourself.
    Self-serve platform
    A platform you sign up for and set up yourself, without a demo or sales call.
    Renewal price
    What you pay to keep the platform running after the first term ends. Ask for it in writing before you sign.
    Platform fee
    A percentage a platform keeps from each sale. AllCoaching's is 0% on every monthly plan, and 10%, 7.5% or 5% on the yearly Starter, Growth or Pro plan.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the main difference between AppX and Classplus?

    AppX sets up a branded website and app for you and lists no price on its website, while Classplus lists ₹29,999 for one year and ₹39,999 for two years. Both give you a branded coaching app and website; they differ in how you start and what they publish before you sign.

    Is AppX a one-time payment?

    AppX does not say on its website, which lists no price and starts with a free personalised demo (checked 24 Oct 2026). Ask in writing whether the quote is a one-time build fee, a yearly renewal or both, and what happens to your app if you stop paying.

    Which is cheaper, AppX or Classplus?

    It cannot be compared from public information, because AppX publishes no price. Get AppX's written quote, including any setup fee, renewal and fee on sales, and compare it with Classplus's listed ₹29,999 a year after asking Classplus about GST and fees.

    How much does Classplus cost in 2026?

    Classplus lists ₹29,999 for 1 year and ₹39,999 for 2 years on classplusapp.com/new-web (checked 24 Oct 2026). The page does not state GST, any fee on sales or the renewal price.

    Does AppX or Classplus bring students to my coaching?

    Both are tools for running your own coaching app; finding students stays mainly your job through YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp and referrals. Classplus lists A.I. Powered Leads on its plan, so ask what that includes.

    What should I ask before signing with AppX or Classplus?

    Ask for the year-one total with GST, the renewal price, any fee on sales, whether Android and iOS apps are included and under whose account, whether you can use your own domain, who makes changes after launch, and what you can export if you leave.
