    Why Indian educators look for a Teachable or Thinkific alternative

    Teachable and Thinkific are mature, well-built course platforms. Indian educators rarely leave because of a missing feature. They leave because of currency, checkout and the cost of an app.

    What Teachable and Thinkific do well

    Teachable sells course, coaching and membership tools with global payments and tax handling, and its tagline is "Launch, sell, and scale your online business."[1] Thinkific combines courses, community and live cohorts with commerce tools, and describes itself as "Built for businesses that can't afford to outgrow their platform".[2] Both have years of product work behind them, and both let you try before paying.

    1. Paying in US dollars for an Indian business

    Your students pay ₹499 for a test series or ₹2,999 for a batch. Your platform bill is in dollars, so its rupee cost changes with the exchange rate, your card may add a foreign-currency charge, and Indian GST may apply. Our guide to GST and income tax for online tutors explains the tax side in plain words.

    2. A checkout your students can finish

    Many Indian students have no credit card, and a parent often pays from a UPI app. Neither the Teachable nor the Thinkific pricing page lists UPI as a payment method. Thinkific's FAQ says Thinkific Payments is priced "in line with other processors like Stripe and PayPal at 2.9%", and that third-party processors add a gateway fee. Ask both platforms whether your Indian students can pay by UPI and what it costs. Our guide to the best UPI payment gateway for online courses covers why this decides conversions.

    Question educators ask

    Should I leave Teachable or Thinkific if some of my students are abroad?

    Not necessarily. If a real share of your buyers pay by card in dollars, keep the US platform for them. Many educators run an Indian academy alongside it for rupee and UPI buyers, and decide later from the numbers.

    3. Your own app costs extra

    Thinkific sells a branded mobile app as a US$199 a month add-on, included only on select Plus plans. For Teachable, ask whether an app under your own name is available and on which plan. For educators whose students live on their phones, an app included on every plan changes the maths.

    4. Product limits on lower plans

    Teachable's Starter plan allows 5 products, Builder 10 and Growth 50. A coaching educator who sells separate batches, test series and notes can reach those caps quickly.

    Glossary

    UPI
    Unified Payments Interface, India's real-time payment system used from apps such as Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm.
    Transaction fee
    A percentage a platform keeps from each sale, separate from the payment processor's fee. Teachable Starter's is 7.5%.
    Payment processing fee
    What a payment provider charges to collect a card or UPI payment, separate from any platform fee.
    Foreign-currency charge
    An extra amount a bank may add when an Indian card pays a bill priced in another currency, such as US dollars.
    White-label app
    An Android or iOS app that carries your academy's name and logo, built on a platform's technology.
    Parallel run
    Keeping the old and new platforms active at the same time during a switch, so current students keep access.
