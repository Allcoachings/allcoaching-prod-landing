The reframe

The reframe: pricing model,
not feature list.

A multi-teacher coaching platform on revenue share is one where an institute runs several teachers under a single branded studio and pays the platform partly or wholly as a share of sales rather than per teacher seat — and for an institute with five or more teachers, the pricing model, not the feature list, is the decision that matters most. Most owners compare dashboards, video players and test engines and find them broadly similar. They are. What differs is how you pay.

At institute scale the platform cost stops being a small fixed line. Whatever the platform charges is applied across your whole faculty and carried through every slow month. A per-seat fee that looks reasonable for one teacher becomes a large controllable expense at five, eight or twelve — especially when some teachers are new and not yet earning.

So the right question is not "which platform has the most features" but "which pricing shape fits our sales and our headcount". This guide lays out the options honestly, how the split actually works, and what to check before you commit — the same discipline behind choosing a zero-commission teaching platform in India, applied to a faculty.

The split

How the split
actually works.

"Revenue share" can mean two things in an institute. On AllCoaching, any fee per sale is between the platform and the institute: 0% on every monthly plan, or 10%, 7.5% or 5% on yearly Starter, Growth or Pro. It is calculated on sales, not on the number of teachers.

What the platform does not do is decide how the institute pays its teachers. That internal split is the institute's own arrangement — a revenue percentage, a salary, a per-batch share, whatever the institute and its faculty agree. Payouts reach the institute's bank; the institute pays its teachers on its own terms.

Question Often Asked

Does the platform automatically pay each of my teachers their share?

No — and that is by design. Payouts go to the institute: 100% of each sale on a monthly plan, or 90%, 92.5% or 95% on a yearly plan, and the institute divides its share among teachers itself. There is no automated owner-to-teacher payout split, which keeps faculty arrangements in the institute's hands.

The hidden tax

Why per-seat pricing
punishes growth.

The sharpest edge of per-seat pricing shows up when an institute does the right thing: hires a promising new teacher. That seat is billed from day one, while the first batch is still being built — so the institute pays for capacity before it earns from it.

On AllCoaching the plan price covers the plan's seats, so adding a teacher within your seats does not raise the bill; moving beyond them means moving up a plan (5 seats on Growth, 7 on Pro). Running fee collection across a growing faculty is its own workload, covered in automated fee management software for teachers.

Question Often Asked

We rotate teachers and run seasonal batches — does revenue share really fit better?

Often, yes. On a yearly plan the fee per sale applies only when students pay, so a quiet pre-exam lull adds no commission; on a monthly plan you pay a flat fee at 0% commission and can stop any month. Institutes with very uneven seasons and lower sales usually suit yearly Starter; those selling steadily above about ₹4 lakh a year usually keep more on a monthly plan.

The distribution edge

Discovery, per teacher,
not just the institute.

Pricing decides what an institute keeps; discovery decides whether there is anything to keep. On a standalone app, individual teachers stay invisible behind the brand. With course pages per teacher and a marketplace listing, students can find the course for the exact exam, subject or language they need — a strong physics teacher and a strong history teacher are each found by the aspirants looking for them.

An institute that depends only on its own ad budget caps its growth at what it can spend. Adding search-friendly course pages, teacher-led YouTube content and a marketplace listing gives it more routes to students — none of them guaranteed, all of them compounding. Glossary

Glossary —
key terms.

Term

Revenue-Share Model

A pricing model where the platform takes a percentage of each sale, instead of or alongside a fixed fee. On AllCoaching it is 0% commission on every monthly plan or a 10% fee per sale on yearly Starter; the institute keeps 90% or 100%, with the branded studio included in every plan.

Term

Subscription Model

A pricing model where the platform charges a fixed recurring fee — typically annual — regardless of how much the institute earns or how many of its teachers are actively selling. The fee is owed in slow seasons and during every new teacher's ramp-up.

Term

Multi-Teacher Institute Support

One branded studio that carries separate logins for each teacher, with individual content management and batch ownership under a single institute brand. On AllCoaching it is included on every plan, with 3, 5 or 7 team seats and no per-teacher fee.

Term

Per-Seat Pricing

A cost structure where the platform fee scales with the number of teacher seats or logins, so the bill rises with headcount. It charges for a teacher's seat whether or not that teacher is yet earning; a plan with seats included avoids that.

Term

Batch Ownership

An arrangement where each teacher manages their own batches, courses, recorded lessons and students under the institute brand. It lets subject experts run their own teaching while the institute keeps one unified, branded student experience.

Term

Institute-Managed Revenue Split

How a multi-teacher institute divides its share of sales among individual teachers — an arrangement the institute sets and settles itself. On AllCoaching any fee per sale is between the platform and the institute; the institute handles its own internal teacher payments.

Term

Automatic payouts

A settlement model where the institute's share of a student payment reaches its bank account automatically, rather than on a monthly cycle. It keeps cash flow with the institute as fees are collected across all teachers.

Term

Marketplace Discovery

AllCoaching's marketplace listing, where students browse by exam, subject or language and can find an institute's courses. An extra channel alongside the institute's own marketing, not a promise of students.
