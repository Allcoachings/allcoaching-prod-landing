    Why educators look for a Spayee or Learnyst alternative

    Both platforms have served Indian educators for years, and Learnyst in particular is strong for test series. The reasons educators compare usually fall into three groups.

    1. Spayee is now Graphy

    When we visited spayee.com on 24 Oct 2026, it redirected to graphy.com.[1] Graphy is the platform built by the Spayee team. If you signed up with Spayee, your plan, prices and support now sit with Graphy, so compare Graphy's current offer, not Spayee's old one.

    2. The fixed cost before you earn

    Learnyst gives you two shapes of price. Launcher is ₹10,000 a year with 10% of sales and "No monthly fee". Professional is ₹8,999 a month, or ₹8,099 a month billed annually, which is ₹97,188 a year, with 0% transaction fees. The second is strong at high sales and heavy at low sales. Many educators look for something in between.

    Question educators ask

    Is my Learnyst plan too expensive, or am I just not selling enough yet?

    Usually it is about stage. A 0%-fee monthly plan suits an academy with steady, large sales; a plan with a low yearly price and a fee on sales suits one that is still growing. Compare the total at your real sales level, as in the cost section below.

    3. Who owns the app listing

    Learnyst's pricing page says apps "will be available in Google Play store & Apple App store under Learnyst's Developer Account", and a branded Android app starts from the Premium plan. Some educators want their own app on every plan. Ask each platform whose developer account the app is published under.

    How to switch from Spayee or Learnyst without losing students

    The safe way to switch is to run both platforms side by side. Current students keep access on the old platform until their batch or validity ends; new admissions go to your new academy.

    1. Export your content and recordsDownload your videos, PDFs, question banks and student list from Learnyst or Graphy before you start.
    2. Build your new academyStart the 7-day trial, connect your domain and rebuild one complete course and one test series.
    3. Run both platforms side by sideKeep current students on the old platform until their batch or course validity ends.
    4. Send new admissions to your academyShare your own website and app link on YouTube, Instagram and WhatsApp for every new batch.
    5. Close the old plan at renewalLet the old plan end at its renewal date, after the last batch finishes.

    The full checklist, including messages to send students, is in how to switch coaching apps without losing students.

    Glossary

    DRM (digital rights management)
    Technology that encrypts video so it can only be played inside an authorised app or player, making it harder to copy.
    Transaction fee
    A percentage a platform keeps from each sale. Learnyst's subscription plans list 0%; AllCoaching's is 0% on every monthly plan, and 10%, 7.5% or 5% on the yearly Starter, Growth or Pro plan.
    Developer account
    The Google Play or Apple App Store account an app is published under; it decides whose name appears as the app's publisher.
    White-label app
    An Android or iOS app that carries your academy's name and logo, built on a platform's technology.
    Parallel run
    Keeping the old and new platforms active at the same time during a switch, so current students keep access.
