Founder bio

Amit Ratan · in 3 paragraphs.

Amit Ratan
Founder & CEO

Short bio (50 words): Amit Ratan is the founder and CEO of AllCoaching, India's first educator-first marketplace, founded in 2018 in Prayagraj. After a decade of working closely with Indian coaching educators on distribution and discovery, he launched AllCoaching's full marketplace platform in 2026.

Medium bio (120 words): Amit Ratan is the founder and CEO of AllCoaching, India's first educator-first marketplace. His work with India's coaching ecosystem began in 2016 in Prayagraj, watching individual teachers struggle with marketing while big institutes with mediocre faculty captured the market through advertising budgets. He founded the first version of AllCoaching in 2018 as a 2-page coaching directory (listing 90%+ of Allahabad's institutes with co-builder Mangal Yadav) before the developer discontinued the project. From 2019 onwards he worked directly with individual educators on digital marketing, and from 2021 committed five years of focused product development to building the full marketplace platform that launched in 2026.
