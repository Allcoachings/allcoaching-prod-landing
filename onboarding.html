Before you decide

The questions everyone asks.

Is ₹6,999 really all I pay?

Yes — once, and it does not repeat. No subscription, no renewal, no yearly increase, no setup fee on top. The only other money that moves is 10% of a sale, after a student has actually paid you.

What if I don't sell anything one month?

You pay nothing. The 10% only applies to completed sales. In a slow month the platform earns nothing from you — that is the whole point of charging once instead of monthly.

Do I pay extra for my own domain?

No — connecting your domain is included. The only thing you buy separately is the domain name itself, about ₹600–1,200 a year from GoDaddy, Namecheap or Hostinger, registered in your own name so it always stays yours.

Are live classes charged by the hour?

No. Live classes are included, with chat and automatic recordings. There is no per-hour meter and no separate live plan to buy.

Will students see my brand or AllCoaching's?

Yours. Your academy name, logo and colours lead everywhere — the website, the app on their home screen, the receipts. We run the infrastructure quietly in the background.

Can I see it before paying?

Yes — open the free demo. It is the real product with sample data. No sign-up, no card, nothing saved or charged.

What if I want to leave later?

Your students, payment history and content are exportable any time, and the domain is registered in your name, so it leaves with you. There is no lock-in and no notice period.

Why is onboarding paid now?

The free seats are full. Setting an academy up properly takes real time from a real person — your first course, your domain, your pricing. The fee pays for that. The 90/10 split has not changed.
