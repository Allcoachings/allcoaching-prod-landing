Before you start

Quick answers.

Is the price yearly? Are there extra charges?

Yes — ₹6,999, ₹12,999 or ₹24,999 a year, plus GST. After that, only a platform fee on what you sell: 10%, 7.5% or 5% by plan. No setup fee.

Do I really set it all up myself?

Yes. Sign up, upload your course, connect your domain and publish from one studio — no sales call, no developer. If you get stuck, WhatsApp us.

Where will students buy my courses?

On your own domain, on the AllCoaching app for Android and iOS, and on the web at learn.allcoaching.in.

Can I move from my current platform?

Yes. Export your content and student list, upload it to your studio and point your domain to AllCoaching. We will guide you on WhatsApp.

When do I get paid?

Students pay by UPI or card, and your share reaches your bank 3 days after each sale (T+3).

Can I upgrade or leave later?

Upgrade any time — what you paid is credited. Your content and student data always stay exportable.
