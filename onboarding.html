Before you decide

Questions educators ask us.

Is it really free for 14 days?

Yes. Sign up, build your academy and add your courses for 14 days without paying anything, and you don't need a card to start. After that, pick Starter, Growth or Pro if you want to keep going.

I'm not technical. Can I really build it myself?

Yes. Everything is drag and drop. If you can use WhatsApp, you can set up your academy — no developer, no code. If you get stuck, message us on WhatsApp.

Can I sign up from my phone?

Yes. Enter your mobile number and verify it with a one-time code. You can also sign in with Google, but if you opened this page inside Instagram or Facebook, the mobile number is the easiest way.

Is ₹6,999 really the entry price?

Yes — that is the Starter plan, per year. No setup fee on top, no card required just to sign up. Prices exclude GST, added at checkout. Growth and Pro cost more but lower the platform fee.

Do I need a sales call or demo to sign up?

No. Sign up, choose a plan, upload a course, connect your domain, set up payment, publish — fully self-serve. WhatsApp support is there if you want to ask something first, but it is optional.

Do I pay extra for my own domain?

No — connecting your domain is included on every plan, including Starter. The only thing you buy separately is the domain name itself, about ₹600–1,200 a year from GoDaddy, Namecheap or Hostinger, registered in your own name.

Are live classes charged by the hour?

No. Each plan's live-class hours are included in its price — 5 on Starter, 10 on Growth, 15 on Pro. There is no separate per-hour meter within that allowance.

What happens if I do not renew?

Your academy goes offline at the end of your paid year — students can no longer reach it. Your content and student data stay exportable, and renewing brings it back live.

Will students see my brand or AllCoaching's?

Yours. Your academy name, logo and colours lead everywhere — the website, the app on their home screen, the receipts. We run the infrastructure quietly in the background.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes, any time. Upgrades take effect the moment payment clears, and what you already paid this year is credited toward the higher plan — you only pay the difference.
