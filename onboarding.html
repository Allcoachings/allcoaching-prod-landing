Before you start

Quick answers.

Is ₹6,999 the full price?

It is the Starter plan price for a year, plus GST at checkout. After that you pay a 10% platform fee only on what you actually sell, so you keep 90%. Payment-gateway charges on student payments are separate. No setup fee.

Do I need a sales call or a demo?

No. Continue with Google, pay, upload your course and publish — fully self-serve. WhatsApp is there if you want to ask something first, but it is optional.

Where do students buy my courses?

On the AllCoaching app for Android and iOS, on the web app at learn.allcoaching.in, and on your own connected domain. Students follow you by name and pay by UPI or card.

Do I pay extra for my own domain?

No — connecting your domain is included. You only buy the domain name itself, about ₹600–1,200 a year from any registrar, in your own name.

Can I upgrade or leave later?

Upgrade any time — what you already paid this year is credited. If you do not renew, the academy goes offline at the end of the year, and your content and student data stay exportable.
