Questions educators ask us.

Do I need a developer or any technical skill?

No. You build your academy by dragging and dropping. If you can use WhatsApp, you can set it up yourself.

How fast can I go live?

Your studio opens as soon as you pay. Add your first course and share your academy link the same day. Your own Android app (iOS app on Pro or any yearly plan) goes live after Google Play and App Store review.

Are videos or live classes limited?

Not on YouTube or Zoom: every plan has unlimited videos through YouTube and unlimited live classes on YouTube Live or Zoom. The built-in live classroom comes with hours each month: 20, 50 or 90 on monthly Starter, Growth or Pro, and 5, 10 or 15 on yearly plans. Videos you upload use your plan's storage and streaming.

Will students see my brand or yours?

Yours. Your logo, your colours, your own domain like youracademy.in, and your own app in your name.

What do I pay in total?

Paid monthly, Starter is ₹3,999, Growth ₹5,999 and Pro ₹7,999 a month, with 0% commission, so you keep 100% of every sale. Paid yearly, they are ₹6,999, ₹12,999 and ₹24,999 a year plus 10%, 7.5% or 5% only when a student pays you. GST is extra. No setup fee and no sales limit.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. An upgrade starts as soon as you pay, with the unused value of your current plan credited. Monthly plans have no lock-in. If you stop, your academy goes offline to students at the end of the paid period and you have 30 days to export your content and data or come back.
