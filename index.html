01 — The educator's trap

If you've lived any of this, it was never your fault.

Having an app and having students are two different things. The app-builders sold you the app — what happened after launch became your problem. So you tried everything, and slowly stopped being a teacher.

01
Pain 01

A performer, not a teacher

The algorithm rewards 60-second drama, not depth. Hours on reels instead of a better class — and your instruction quietly got shallower.

02
Pain 02

Marketing ate your teaching

Every rupee on Meta and Google ads is a rupee not on curriculum. A ₹10,000 budget against a ₹100 crore EdTech — never a fair fight.

03
Pain 03

Accidental tech founder

Gateways failing at checkout, video hosting, DRM, OTP systems, support tickets on your phone. A software product you never signed up for.

04
Pain 04

Hidden costs everywhere

Listing fees, transaction cuts, maintenance, SMS and CDN bills. "Your own platform" quietly bled margin until every cost felt existential.

05
Pain 05

Completely alone online

A personal app is a walled garden. No shared discovery, no peers, no ecosystem. Visible only to people who already knew you existed.

06
Pain 06

You never got to teach

The deepest cost: India's best educators spend their most productive hours on everything except the one thing they're exceptional at.

02 — Where educators get stuck

Three places you've already tried. Each breaks differently.

Great teaching means nothing if no one can find you — or if you don't own what you build. Today's options each solve one problem and create two more.

YouTube

Built for entertainment, not learning.

The algorithm rewards viral content. Serious educators drown. You get views — not outcomes, not income, not ownership.

Telegram

A messaging app pretending to be a school.

Lessons buried in chats. Manual payment screenshots. Zero access control. The moment your group grows, it collapses.

Personal app

Total control — and total invisibility.

Nobody downloads an app they've never heard of. You burn lakhs on ads, fight uninstalls, and depend on developers just to stay discoverable.

"A personal app doesn't grow your coaching. It hides it."

03 — Two bad choices

For five years, educators had to pick a poison.

A personal app gives ownership but no reach. Big EdTech gives reach but no ownership. The ecosystem was stuck between two bad options — and no one built the third.

Personal app

Ownership, but invisible.

✓ Your brand, your students
✗ No discovery without paid ads

You own everything — and nobody can find you. A personal app doesn't grow your coaching. It hides it behind a download wall no student knows to climb.

vs
Big EdTech

Reach, but absorbed.

✓ Massive student traffic
✗ Brand & students aren't yours

Platforms like Classplus and Teachmint don't partner with you — they absorb you. Your name disappears behind theirs, your content becomes their asset, and if you leave, they keep the students you earned.

The third option · finally built

Keep your brand and get discovered.

A marketplace where your identity stays yours, your students stay yours, and the network does the finding — so you never choose between ownership and reach again.

18 — Questions educators ask first

Straight answers, no fine print.

Do I really keep 90%?+
Yes. We take a flat 10% on paid sales — courses, batches, test series and paid live classes. No monthly fee, no setup cost, and we take nothing on the free content you use to grow. Payouts hit your account over UPI every week.
Is the app really branded as mine?+
Completely. Your logo, your name, your accent colour, your domain. Students download your app from the Play Store and App Store — AllCoaching runs quietly underneath as the engine. You own the relationship.
How long does setup take?+
The studio itself is live in minutes — name it, pick a subject, publish. Your white-labelled app is typically ready in a few days. Most educators launch their first paid batch within 48 hours.
Will my content be safe from piracy?+
Every video is auto-watermarked with your handle, served over 256-bit encryption, and protected against screen recording on mobile. Your work stays yours.
What if I already teach on YouTube?+
Keep teaching there. Use your free shorts to bring students into your AllCoaching studio, where you can sell structured courses, run live batches and see exactly what each student needs — things a public feed can't give you.
Which exams and subjects are supported?+
JEE, NEET, UPSC, CAT, CLAT, board exams, SSC, banking and more — across English, Hindi and Hinglish. If you teach a serious subject, there are already students here searching for it.
