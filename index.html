Questions educators ask first

Straight answers, no fine print.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial, so you can set up your academy, add a course and see your website before you pay, and no card is needed to start. When the trial ends, pick Starter, Growth or Pro to continue.

Do I still need WordPress or another LMS?

No. Your academy website, your own domain, courses, tests, live classes, payments and the AllCoaching app on Android, iOS and web all run from one AllCoaching login. You don't need a separate website builder, LMS or developer.

Do I really keep up to 95%?

Yes. We take a platform fee from 10% down to 5% on paid sales, by plan — courses, batches, test series and paid live classes. One simple annual plan, no hidden cuts, and we take nothing on the free content you use to grow. Payouts reach your bank 3 days after each sale (T+3).

Where do students find and buy my courses?

On the AllCoaching marketplace — the AllCoaching app on Android and iOS, the web app at learn.allcoaching.in, and your own connected domain. Students follow you by name and buy your courses there. Your content, your students and your prices stay yours.

How long does setup take?

About 2 minutes to sign up, name your studio, add a course and publish. Your courses show up on the AllCoaching app as soon as you publish, and a custom domain goes live as soon as its DNS update is picked up.

Will my content be safe from piracy?

Every video is auto-watermarked with your handle, served over 256-bit encryption, and protected against screen recording on mobile. Your work stays yours.

Which exams and subjects are supported?

JEE, NEET, UPSC, CAT, CLAT, board exams, SSC, banking and more — across English, Hindi and Hinglish. If you teach a serious subject, there are already students here searching for it.
