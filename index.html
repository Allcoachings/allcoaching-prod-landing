Questions educators ask us

Is it really free for 7 days?

Yes. Sign up, build your LMS and add your courses for 7 days without paying anything, and you don't need a card to start. After that, pick a monthly plan with 0% commission or a yearly plan to keep selling.

Do I need any technical skill?

No. You build your academy by dragging and dropping. If you can use WhatsApp, you can set up your LMS — no developer, no code.

Can I sign up from my phone?

Yes. Enter your mobile number and verify it with a one-time code. You can also sign in with Google, but if you opened this page inside Instagram or Facebook, the mobile number is the easiest way.

Will it carry my brand?

Yes. Your logo, your colours and your own domain, like youracademy.in. You can also publish your own Android app (iOS app on Pro or any yearly plan). Students see your name.

Do I still need WordPress or another LMS?

No. Your website, courses, tests, live classes, payments and marketing tools all run from one AllCoaching login.

How much do you take from each sale?

Nothing on monthly plans: they take 0% commission, so you keep 100% of every sale. Yearly plans take 10% on Starter, 7.5% on Growth and 5% on Pro, only when a student pays you. Payouts go to your bank automatically.

How do I get paid?

Students pay by UPI or card on your academy's Razorpay checkout. Access opens for them automatically, and your share is paid out to your bank automatically.

Who owns my students and content?

You do. Your courses, your students and your data can be exported any time while your plan runs, and for 30 days after you stop.

Can I move from another app or a WhatsApp group?

Yes. Upload your existing videos, notes and tests as courses, set your prices and share your academy link with your students. If you get stuck, message us on WhatsApp.

Can I talk to someone before I start?

Yes. You don't need a call to start, but the team is on WhatsApp and email, Monday to Saturday, in Hindi and English.

Ready to build your own academy?

7 days free, no card needed. Drag in your first course tonight and share your academy link tomorrow.
