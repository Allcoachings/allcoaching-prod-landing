Questions educators ask us

Is it really free for 14 days?

Yes. Sign up, build your LMS and add your courses for 14 days without paying anything, and you don't need a card to start. After that, pick Starter, Growth or Pro if you want to keep going.

Do I need any technical skill?

No. You build your academy by dragging and dropping. If you can use WhatsApp, you can set up your LMS — no developer, no code.

Will it carry my brand?

Yes. Your logo, your colours and your own domain, like youracademy.in. You can also publish your own app on Google Play. Students see your name.

Do I still need WordPress or another LMS?

No. Your website, courses, tests, live classes, payments and marketing tools all run from one AllCoaching login.

How much do you take from each sale?

10% on Starter, 7.5% on Growth and 5% on Pro, only when a student pays you. You keep the rest, and it reaches your bank 3 days after the sale.
