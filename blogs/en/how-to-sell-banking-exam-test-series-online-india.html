Section 03

Exam stack —
which banking exams to cover and in what order.

The Indian banking-exam ecosystem in 2026 spans 12-15 active exam categories with different aspirant pool sizes, ARPU ranges, and competitive dynamics. Most solo educators try to cover too many exams too early and dilute their exam-pattern fidelity across the stack. The correct sequence is — start with 2 exams, validate the architecture, then expand. The three-tier stack:

Tier 1 — IBPS PO + SBI PO + IBPS Clerk + SBI Clerk. These four exams cover 85% of the annual Indian banking-aspirant search volume. Aspirant pool 1.2-2.5 crore combined annually. ARPU range ₹500-₹2,500 typical. Competitive density medium-high (incumbents like Adda247, Oliveboard, Career Power dominate). The starter stack for any new banking-exam test series — IBPS PO + SBI PO covering Prelims + Mains is the minimum viable launch.

Tier 2 — RBI Grade B + NABARD Grade A/B + LIC AAO/ADO + NIACL AO. These are premium positioning exams with smaller but materially higher-ARPU candidate pools. Aspirant pool 8-25 lakh combined annually. ARPU range ₹2,000-₹8,000 typical. RBI Grade B aspirants tolerate 50-80% pricing premium over IBPS aspirants because the exam is harder, the candidate pool is more committed, and the role is higher-status. Add Tier 2 in month 6-9 after Tier 1 architecture is validated.

Tier 3 — IBPS RRB Officer Scale I/II/III + IBPS RRB Office Assistant + SBI SO (Specialist Officer) + state cooperative banks. These are niche or regional exams with smaller aspirant pools (2-15 lakh annually each) but lower competitive density — solo educators can dominate specific niches (Hindi-medium IBPS RRB, regional cooperative bank exams) where incumbents have weak coverage. Add Tier 3 in month 12+ for breadth and niche revenue protection.

Strategic Definition

The 12-Month Banking Exam Stack Buildout Plan

Month 1-2: Launch IBPS PO Prelims + Mains complete series. Month 3-4: Add SBI PO Prelims + Mains. Month 5-6: Add IBPS Clerk + SBI Clerk Prelims (Mains is simpler, lower aspirant ARPU). Month 7-9: Add RBI Grade B Phase 1 + Phase 2 (descriptive section). Month 10-12: Add NABARD Grade A/B + LIC AAO/ADO + select Tier 3 niche exams. By month 12, a focused banking educator on AllCoaching can have 8-10 active exam test series operating at ₹6-15 lakh monthly aggregate revenue. The build sequence is what makes this operationally feasible — trying to launch all stacks simultaneously dilutes quality and breaks exam-pattern fidelity.

The sequencing rationale — IBPS PO is the highest-volume entry exam aspirants attempt first. SBI PO is the highest-prestige and highest-ARPU. Together they cover the dominant share of search intent. IBPS Clerk + SBI Clerk add breadth for aspirants who attempt all four. RBI Grade B adds premium positioning. Tier 3 exams add defensible niche revenue. Most amateur banking test series launch with 6-8 exams simultaneously, fail exam-pattern fidelity on all of them, and plateau at low revenue. The disciplined sequential buildout is the operational unlock.
