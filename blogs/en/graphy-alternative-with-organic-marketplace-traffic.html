    Where organic students actually come from

    Organic students reach an educator through four channels. A platform cannot replace any of them, but it can make each one work better or worse.

    1. Google search on your own domain

    Students type exact needs into Google: "SSC CGL maths course in Hindi", "spoken English classes online for housewives", "NEET biology test series". A page on your own domain that answers that search can rank for years. It is slow work. Google's own guide is plain about the timing:[1]

    "Some changes might take effect in a few hours, others could take several months."

    Google Search Central, SEO Starter Guide

    It also does not happen by default: an Ahrefs study found that 96.55% of content gets no traffic from Google.[2] Pages that rank are written for a specific search. Our SEO for an online academy guide shows how.

    2. YouTube

    For most Indian educators, YouTube is the largest free channel. Free lessons build trust; the link in the description turns viewers into students. What matters here is that the link opens your own academy, on your own name, with a clear course and price.

    3. Instagram, WhatsApp and Telegram

    These channels carry the people who already know you. A yoga teacher's reels or a banking educator's WhatsApp group can fill a batch, if the payment step is one tap and works with UPI.

    4. Marketplaces and listings

    A marketplace is a place where students already browse courses. It can add students you would not have reached, but it works on the marketplace's terms, and it is rarely the main source for an independent educator.

    How to switch from Graphy and keep your traffic

    The safe way to switch is to run both platforms side by side and change your links in order: new admissions first, current students last. That way no student loses access and no traffic lands on a dead page.

    1. Build your academy on your own domainStart the 7-day trial, connect your domain and upload one complete course with a free demo lesson.
    2. Run both platforms side by sideKeep current students on Graphy until their batch or course validity ends, so nobody loses access.
    3. Point every link to your new academyUpdate YouTube descriptions, pinned comments, your Instagram bio and WhatsApp group descriptions for new students.
    4. Publish one search page per courseGive each course a page that answers one search, such as the exam, subject and language, on your own domain.
    5. List your courses on the marketplaceTurn on your listing on the AllCoaching marketplace app as an extra channel.
    6. Export records and close the old planDownload student and payment records from Graphy, then let the plan end at its renewal date.

    The full checklist, including messages to send students, is in how to switch coaching apps without losing students.

    Glossary

    Organic traffic
    Visitors who reach your academy without a paid ad, for example from Google search, YouTube, social posts or a marketplace listing.
    Marketplace listing
    A page for your course inside a marketplace app or site where students already browse, in addition to your own academy.
    Custom domain
    Your own web address, such as youracademy.in, connected to your academy website; search visibility you build there stays with you.
    SEO
    Search engine optimisation: making pages that answer what students search, so Google can show them.
    Meta Pixel
    A small code from Meta that measures visits and purchases from Facebook and Instagram, so you can see which posts and ads bring students.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is a Graphy alternative with organic traffic?

    It is a platform that gives you more ways for students to find you, not only a place to sell. That means a website on your own domain that Google can rank, an app and links for your YouTube and social audience, and a listing where students already browse courses.

    Does Graphy provide organic traffic to educators?

    Graphy's pricing page does not describe a built-in student marketplace; it describes a website builder, branded apps, digital products and AI sales agents. Your Graphy site can still earn Google and YouTube traffic, so ask Graphy what discovery features your plan includes.

    Can any platform guarantee students?

    No. A platform can make it easier for students to find and trust you, but no honest platform can guarantee enrolments. Your teaching, your content and your channels still decide most of the result.

    How does the AllCoaching marketplace help discovery?

    Every AllCoaching plan lets your courses also be discovered on the AllCoaching marketplace app on Android, iOS and web. It is a supporting channel next to your own website, YouTube and social channels, not a replacement for them.

    Not on AllCoaching. Your academy keeps its own domain, logo, colours and Android app (iOS app on Pro or any yearly plan), and the marketplace listing is an extra place to be found. On a marketplace such as Udemy, your course sits on the marketplace's own site.
