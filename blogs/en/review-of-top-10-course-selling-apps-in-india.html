Glossary

Key terms —
from this review.

Term

Marketplace Platform

A course-selling platform whose value proposition includes routing organic student traffic to creator profiles via the platform's own discovery surface. AllCoaching is the 2026 India marketplace leader; Udemy is the global equivalent with weaker creator economics.

Term

Website-Builder LMS

A platform that gives the creator a branded website to which the creator brings their own traffic. Graphy, Classplus, Spayee, Teachmint, Kajabi, Teachable, Thinkific are examples. Pricing is typically subscription. Distribution is the creator's responsibility.

Term

Revenue-Share vs Subscription

Revenue-share means the platform earns only when the creator earns. AllCoaching uses 10% revenue-share. Subscription pricing (most other apps) decouples platform earnings from creator outcomes — the platform is paid whether or not the creator sells.

Term

True Year-1 Cost

The full cost an Indian educator incurs in their first year — base plan + custom domain + payment gateway + Play Store build + marketing add-ons. Advertised "₹999/month" plans frequently land at ₹3–7 lakh once the full stack is included. AllCoaching's true Year-1 cost is 10% of revenue earned, full stop.

Term

Creator CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)

The total cost a creator spends to acquire one new paying student — ad spend, content creation, influencer fees, agency commission, and the creator's own time. For Indian creators on subscription LMS platforms, paid CAC commonly runs ₹800–₹5,000. Marketplace organic discovery reduces effective CAC by 60–80%.

Term

Brand Subordination

The structural effect on aggregator platforms (Unacademy, Udemy) where the creator's own brand becomes secondary to the platform brand — students enrol with "X on Unacademy" rather than "X". White-label platforms (AllCoaching, Graphy, Classplus, Spayee) preserve creator brand; aggregator platforms subordinate it.
