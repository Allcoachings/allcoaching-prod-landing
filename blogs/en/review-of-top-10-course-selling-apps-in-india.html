    How we ranked the top 10 course selling apps

    We scored each app against five criteria that matter to an Indian educator selling their own courses, using only what each company publishes on its own website.

    1. Rupee price published, so you can see the cost before talking to anyone.
    2. Fee on sales stated, because a percentage changes the total as you grow.
    3. Own domain and own branded Android app on the entry plan, so students remember your brand.
    4. Start without a sales call: a free trial, a no-upfront-fee plan or free sign-up.
    5. Built for Indian educators: rupee pricing and courses for Indian students.

    Apps are ordered by score, then by how many kinds of educators they serve. We do not rank our own platform. AllCoaching is shown separately at the end, with its score, so you can compare it on the same criteria. These criteria are our choice; a different reader might weigh price before the first sale or test-series depth more heavily, and the order would change.

    The top 10 course selling apps in India, reviewed

    Each app below is described the way its own company describes it, with the published price, what to check and the use case it suits best. The score is the number of our five criteria it meets on its own website.

    1. Learnyst · meets 4 of 5

    Good at
    Courses, mock tests and live classes with content protection and test series; it pitches "Features required to market and sell your content online across globe".
    Published price
    Launcher ₹10,000/yr + 10% of sales; Professional ₹8,999/month (₹8,099 billed annually) with 0% transaction fees; higher plans up to ₹49,999/month; taxes extra.[2]
    Check
    Branded Android from Premium, branded iOS from Business, published under Learnyst's developer account.
    Best for
    Test series for NEET, JEE, SSC and banking exams.

    2. Exly · meets 4 of 5

    Good at
    Storefront, payments, CRM and WhatsApp and email automation for coaches selling courses, consultations and digital products; "an all-in-one solution to all things business".
    Published price
    Starter: no upfront fee + 10% commission; Pro ₹30,000/yr + 6%; Premium ₹1,08,000/yr + 3%.[3]
    Check
    A mobile app is an add-on from ₹60,000 a year; GST and a trial are not stated.
    Best for
    Coaches selling sessions, cohorts or digital products with nothing paid upfront.

    Good at

    Three names from our earlier list are gone, because their official websites no longer show a separate course-selling product.

    • Spayee: spayee.com now redirects to graphy.com (checked 24 Oct 2026).[13] Educators who used Spayee should look at Graphy, which is in this list.

    How to move to a new course selling app without losing students

    The safe way to change apps is to run both side by side: current students stay where they are until their batch ends, and new admissions go to the new app.

    1. Build your new academyStart a free trial, connect your domain and upload one complete course with its demo lesson.
    2. Run both apps side by sideKeep current students on the old app until their batch or validity ends, so nobody loses access.
    3. Send new admissions to the new appShare your own website and app link on WhatsApp, YouTube and Instagram for every new batch.
    4. Export records and close the old planDownload student and payment records, then let the old plan end at renewal.

    Glossary

    Course selling app
    Software that lets an educator publish courses, take payments and give students access, usually with a website and a mobile app.
    Platform fee
    A percentage the app keeps from each sale. AllCoaching's is 0% on every monthly plan, and 10%, 7.5% or 5% on the yearly Starter, Growth or Pro plan.
    Course marketplace
    A site such as Udemy where many educators list courses to the site's own learners, under the site's brand.
    White-label app
    An Android or iOS app that carries your name and logo, built on a platform's technology.
    Custom domain
    Your own web address, such as youracademy.in, connected to your academy website.
