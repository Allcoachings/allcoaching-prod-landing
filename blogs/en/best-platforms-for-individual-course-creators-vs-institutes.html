    Question educators ask

    Can one platform really serve both?

    Yes, if it lets a solo educator start simply and add teachers, batches and logins later without moving students. That matters because many institutes in India began as one teacher with a YouTube channel and grew. What one platform cannot do well is be built only for schools or only for one-person creators and stretch to the other.

    What an individual course creator needs from a platform

    A solo educator needs a platform that costs little before students pay, looks like their own brand, and takes admin off their hands. In order of what usually matters most:

    1. Low fixed cost. A trial or small yearly plan, so you are not paying for a year before your first batch sells.
    2. Your own brand. A website on your own domain and, as students grow, your own app, so students remember you and not the platform. See building a personal brand as an educator.
    3. UPI checkout that gives access automatically. No more checking screenshots and sending links by hand.
    4. Simple setup from a phone or laptop. You are the teacher, editor and support desk.
    5. Live classes and tests in the same place as recorded lessons.
    6. A way to leave. Student and payment data you can export.

    What a coaching institute needs from a platform

    An institute needs everything a solo educator needs, plus control over a team: who teaches which batch, who can see what, and one brand across all of it.

    1. Separate teacher logins. Shared passwords are the first thing to break. Each teacher should manage their own batches.
    2. Batches. Students grouped by class, subject and time slot, with the right content and live classes for each.
    3. One brand for the institute. Website, app and checkout under the institute name, not each teacher's.
    4. Fees the way you collect them. If you take fees in instalments, ask each platform how it handles them before you sign up.
    5. Records the owner can see. Students, payments and test results in one place.
    6. Branches, if you have them. Edmingle publishes branch management; for others, ask.

    Glossary

    Solo educator
    One person who teaches and sells their own courses under their own name; often called an individual course creator.
    Coaching institute
    A teaching business with several teachers running batches under one brand.
    Batch
    A group of students taught together, usually by class, subject and time slot.
    Teacher login
    A separate account for each teacher, so they manage their own batches without sharing the owner's password.
    White-label app
    An Android or iOS app that carries your name and logo, built on a platform's technology.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best platform for individual course creators in India in 2026?

    It depends on what you sell. Exly suits a coach selling from a checkout page with no upfront fee, Graphy suits a creator with a large audience, and AllCoaching suits a solo educator who wants their own website and Android app (iOS app on Pro and Scale) from ₹6,999 a year plus a fee on sales.

    What is the best platform for coaching institutes in India in 2026?

    Classplus suits institutes that want one flat yearly price (₹29,999 listed), Edmingle suits training businesses with batches and branches, AppX suits owners who want a done-for-you setup, and AllCoaching suits institutes that want their own brand with separate teacher logins.

    What is the difference between an individual course creator and a coaching institute?

    A solo educator teaches and sells their own courses; an institute runs several teachers and batches under one brand. That changes what matters most: low fixed cost and personal brand for a solo educator, teacher logins and batch control for an institute.

    Can the same platform serve both solo educators and institutes?

    Yes, if it lets you start alone and add teachers and batches later without moving students. AllCoaching includes separate teacher logins and batch ownership on every plan.

    When should a solo educator move to an institute setup?

    When a second teacher joins, when batches run at the same time, or when parents and students need updates you cannot send by hand. Those are signs you need teacher logins and batches.

    Are Udemy and Unacademy platforms for individual course creators?

    They are places you teach on rather than platforms you run. Neither gives you your own brand, domain or app, and Udemy's revenue share could not be verified on its own page on 24 Oct 2026.

    What should a coaching institute check before choosing a platform?

    Separate teacher logins, batches, how fees and instalments are handled, who owns the app listing, the full yearly cost at your sales level, and whether you can export your data.
