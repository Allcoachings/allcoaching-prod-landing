Section 01

"Online UPSC coaching from home" —
asli sawal kya hai?

Asli sawal yeh nahi ki ghar se padhaya ja sakta hai ya nahi — ja sakta hai. Asli sawal yeh hai: aap kis stage par sabse strong hain, aur aspirants tak aapki expertise kaise pahunchegi. Ek clear niche, ek simple product aur regular free content se shuruaat hoti hai.

· · ·

Section 02

Stage niche chunein —
5 options.

  • Prelims: sabse bada audience, sabse zyada competition; test series aur PYQ analysis.
  • Mains GS: paper-wise answer writing aur evaluation.
  • Optional subject: chhota audience, gehri expertise chahiye.
  • Interview: mock interviews aur DAF analysis; seasonal.
  • Current Affairs: daily ya monthly content, saal bhar chalta hai.

Jahan aapki expertise sabse sharp hai, wahi se shuru kariye.

· · ·

Section 03

Income reality —
honest tasveer.

UPSC mentors ki kamai bahut alag-alag hoti hai aur kisi ki guarantee nahi hai. Kamai is par depend karti hai: aapka niche, price, content ki quality, aur sabse zyada kitne aspirants aap tak pahunchte hain. Zyada tar mentors ke pehle kuch mahine audience banane me jaate hain. Job ke saath shuru karke, steady income aane par full time hona ek samajhdaar raasta hai.

· · ·

Section 04

Tools jo har
UPSC mentor ko chahiye.

  • PDF notes aur study material (DRM aur watermarking ke saath).
  • Mock tests aur sectional tests.
  • Live classes — YouTube Live ya Zoom par, ya built-in live classroom.
  • Answer-writing feedback ka ek tareeka (jaise copies upload karwa kar check karna).
  • UPI checkout aur paid access, taaki sirf paying aspirants content dekhein.
  • Apni website aur app, apne naam se.

AllCoaching par courses, tests, notes, live classes, checkout, apni website aur Android app har plan me hain; iOS app monthly Pro aur har yearly plan me.

Section 05

Pricing —
free se premium tak.

  1. Free: PYQ analysis PDF, weekly editorial ya ek free demo class — bharosa banane ke liye.
  2. Entry paid: test series ya ek focused course.
  3. Core: poora stage course (jaise Prelims ya ek GS paper).
  4. Premium: chhote batch me mentorship aur answer evaluation.

Price aap tay karte hain; doosre mentors ke prices dekhiye aur apni quality ke hisaab se rakhiye.

How to start online UPSC coaching from home in 2026 — practical steps kya hain?

Kya UPSC qualified nahi hone par bhi main UPSC mentor ban sakta hu?

Haan, agar aapke paas kisi stage ya subject ki gehri samajh hai aur aap sahi, updated content de sakte hain. Apne background ke baare me aspirants ko sach batayiye.

UPSC coaching se ghar baithe kitna kama sakte hain?

Iski koi fixed figure ya guarantee nahi hai. Kamai niche, price, quality aur aap tak pahunchne wale aspirants par depend karti hai. Pehle kuch mahine aksar audience banane me jaate hain.

UPSC mentor ke liye konsa stage niche best hai shuruwat me?

Jahan aapki expertise sabse sharp hai. Prelims me audience bada hai par competition bhi; Optional aur Interview chhote par focused niches hain.

UPSC coaching me konse tools chahiye?

PDF notes, mock tests, live classes (YouTube Live, Zoom ya built-in classroom), answer-writing feedback, UPI checkout aur paid access. AllCoaching par yeh sab apni website aur app ke saath milta hai.

Kya online UPSC coaching offline jitni effective hai?

Kai aspirants ke liye haan, agar structure, regular tests aur feedback ho. Kuch aspirants ko offline batch zyada suit karta hai.

UPSC coaching ka pricing kya rakhna chahiye?
