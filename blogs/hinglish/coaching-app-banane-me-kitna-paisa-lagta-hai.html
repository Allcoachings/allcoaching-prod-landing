The reframe

Asli sawal 'kitna paisa' nahi,
'kis route se' hai.

Coaching ka app banane me kitna paisa lagta hai, iska asli jawab yeh hai ki app banane ka dev cost sirf headline number hai, aur asli kharcha server, maintenance aur recurring costs me chhupa hota hai jo vendor aapko pehle nahi batata. Jab koi educator yeh sawal poochta hai, woh ek number sunna chahta hai, jaise "Rs 2 lakh". Lekin yeh galat sawal hai, kyunki app ek product nahi, ek ongoing service hai, aur uska cost ek baar ka nahi balki har mahine ka hai.

Sahi sawal yeh hai: "mere route ka poora cost kya hai, build se lekar usse chalu rakhne aur students laane tak?" Kyunki teen alag routes hain, aur har ek ka cost shape bilkul alag hai. Ek freelancer se app banwana sasta lagta hai par scale par tootta hai; ek agency se white-label lena mehnga par polished hota hai; aur ek ready platform par app Rs 0 me ban jaata hai par revenue ka ek hissa share hota hai. Number tab tak bemaani hai jab tak aap route aur uske chhupe kharche na samjhein.

Iss guide me hum app ke har cost layer ko khol kar dikhayenge, khaas kar woh do jo sabse zyada chhupte hain: server cost aur maintenance cost. Yahi distinction hai jo white-label coaching app development cost in India ka core hai, jaha hum dikhate hain ki "Rs X,XXX per month" ka asli Year-1 cost Rs 4-11 lakh tak kaise pahunch jaata hai.

The headline number

Dev cost, app ka
headline number.

Development cost woh hai jo har vendor sabse pehle batata hai, kyunki yeh ek-baar ka, samajhne me aasaan number hai. Yeh app ko design aur code karne ka kharcha hai, do platforms (Android aur iOS) plus ek web panel, plus backend. Iska range route par depend karta hai: ek freelancer se basic app Rs 50,000 se 3 lakh me, ek agency se proper app Rs 5-15 lakh me, aur ek bada custom build (PhysicsWallah-scale features ke saath) Rs 15 lakh se kai guna upar, kabhi crores tak.

Lekin yahi number sabse zyada gumraah karta hai, kyunki dev cost poore kharche ka sirf ek hissa hai, aksar aadha bhi nahi. App ban jaane ke baad usse chalu rakhna, secure rakhna aur students tak pahuchana, yeh sab dev quote ke bahar hai. Ek educator jo sirf dev cost dekh kar "Rs 3 lakh" ka budget banata hai, woh Year-1 me usse do-teen guna zyada kharch karta hai, kyunki recurring costs uske radar par the hi nahi. Jaise PhysicsWallah jaise coaching app kaise banaye me hum samjhate hain, asli tech ab commoditized hai, isliye dev par lakhs kharch karna sabse bada strategic blunder ho sakta hai.

Question Often Asked

Freelancer Rs 80,000 me app bana raha hai, kya yeh sahi deal hai?

Upfront sasta lagta hai, par poora cost dekhiye. Rs 80,000 me aapko ek basic app mil sakta hai, lekin uske baad hosting aapke khaate me, maintenance har baar paisa, OS update aane par fir se kharcha, aur koi security ya scaling support nahi. Jab 200 students ek saath live class join karenge, app crash ho sakta hai, aur usse theek karna ek naya bill hai. Sasta dev quote tab tak hi sasta hai jab tak aap chalte nahi; scale par yeh sabse mehnga route ban jaata hai.

The hidden recurring

Server cost, woh recurring
kharcha jo chhupta hai.

Yeh woh layer hai jo "kitna paisa lagta hai" ke jawab ko poori tarah badal deti hai. Server ya hosting cost recurring hai, har mahine, aur sabse important, yeh aapke success ke saath badhta hai. Jitne zyada students, jitne zyada video views, utna bada bill. Ek chhoti app ka hosting mahine ke kuch hazaar me chal sakta hai, par jaise students aur HD video lectures badhte hain, bandwidth aur storage ka kharcha tezi se chadhta hai.

Isme sabse bada villain hai video bandwidth. Ek 1-ghante ka HD lecture jab 500 students dekhte hain, woh massive data delivery hai, aur cloud providers per-GB bandwidth charge karte hain. Yahi reason hai ki video-heavy coaching app me hosting cost mahine ke Rs 20,000-30,000 ya usse upar ja sakta hai, jabki educator ne dev quote me yeh socha tak nahi tha. Yeh exactly woh structural cost hai jise secure video hosting for educational content aur recorded lecture hosting cheap in India me detail me khola gaya hai.

Reframe

Dev cost ek baar ka pul banane jaisa hai. Server cost us pul par har gaadi ke guzarne ka toll hai, jo jitni zyada traffic, utna zyada. Isliye ek safal app ka server bill ek nakaam app se kahin zyada hota hai, aur yahi paradox DIY route ko scale par mehnga banata hai.

The hidden layers

Chhupe kharche, jo dev
quote me nahi dikhte.

Dev aur server ke alawa, kai chhote kharche hain jo akele chhote lagte hain par milakar ek bada total bana dete hain. Inhe pehle se jaanna zaroori hai, kyunki yeh sab Year-1 budget ka hissa hain:

Recurring aur chhupe kharche

  • Domain aur SSL — Rs 800-1,500 saalana, plus SSL certificate.
  • Payment gateway fee — har transaction par 1.5 se 3 percent, hamesha.
  • App store fees — Google Play ek-baar ka chhota fee, Apple App Store saalana fee.
  • Maintenance aur updates — bug-fixes plus OS-compatibility, aam taur par dev cost ka 15-20% har saal.
  • Security aur backups — data protection, DPDP compliance, regular backups.
  • Distribution — students laane ka cost, jo aksar sabse bada chhupa kharcha hota hai.

Inme se har ek apne aap me chhota lag sakta hai, par yaad rakhein ki yeh sab recurring hain, ek-baar ke nahi. Payment gateway fee har payment par, maintenance har saal, hosting har mahine. Jab aap inko 12 mahine ke liye jodte hain, "Rs 3 lakh wali app" ka asli Year-1 cost aasaani se Rs 6-10 lakh ban jaata hai. Yahi reason hai ki cost ko hamesha Total Cost of Ownership ke roop me dekhna chahiye, headline number ke roop me nahi.

The myth

'Ek baar ka kharcha'
ek myth kyun hai.

Sabse khatarnaak galatfehmi yeh hai ki "ek baar paisa do, app ban jaata hai". App ek building nahi, ek living system hai jo har din chalu rehna chahiye. Ek baar ka dev cost dene ke baad bhi, woh app tab tak hi kaam karega jab tak koi usse host kare, update kare aur secure rakhe.

Myth (ek-baar ka kharcha)

"Rs 3 lakh do, app hamesha ke liye taiyar." Reality: dev cost ke baad hosting, maintenance, updates aur security har mahine ya har saal paisa maangte hain.

Reality (ongoing service)

App ek recurring kharcha hai. Ready platform par yeh recurring kharcha educator se chhupa kar platform sambhalta hai, 10% rev-share ke badle.

Yahi woh jagah hai jaha ek ready platform ka rev-share model sabse zyada samajh me aata hai. Aap ek bada upfront aur recurring kharcha lene ke bajaye, platform ko 10% dete hain sirf tab jab aap kamaate hain, aur badle me hosting, maintenance, security aur scaling sab platform ki zimmedari ban jaate hain. App ek ongoing service hai; isliye uska cost model bhi ongoing aur outcome-linked hona chahiye, ek-baar ka illusion nahi.

The biggest hidden cost

Asli chhupa cost,
students kaha se?

Ab tak humne build aur run karne ka cost dekha. Lekin sabse bada chhupa kharcha woh hai jo cost calculation me sabse zyada bhula jaata hai: app ban jaane ke baad students laana. Ek khaali app kisi ko nahi milega; ek standalone app ki apni koi discovery nahi hoti. Isliye students laane ke liye paid ads (Meta, Google) ya khud ki marketing par paisa lagana padta hai, jo har student ko mehnga banata hai, aur yeh kharcha hamesha chalta rehta hai.

AllCoaching ka structural answer ek marketplace hai. Aapka app ek shared, AI-driven discovery surface par list hota hai, aur jab koi student apne exam, subject ya language ke liye search karta hai, AI engine aapko us query se match karta hai. Matlab, naye students organically aate hain, bina ad budget ke. Yeh paid ads ka structural alternative hai, aur isi liye ek app ka asli cost calculation tab tak adhoora hai jab tak usme discovery ka kharcha shaamil na ho. Tooling commoditized hai; discovery hi asli scarce cheez hai.

App banwana aadha kharcha hai. Doosra aadha, jise log bhool jaate hain, woh hai us app tak students laana, jo bina marketplace ke sabse mehnga hissa banta hai.

The verdict

'Kitna paisa lagta hai',
ka asli answer.

To "coaching ka app banane me kitna paisa lagta hai" ka honest answer yeh hai: dev cost se mat poochho, total cost of ownership se poochho. DIY route Rs 2-8 lakh Year-1 tak ja sakta hai, white-label Rs 4-11 lakh, aur dono ke upar server, maintenance aur students laane ka recurring kharcha hai. Ek ready platform par yeh Rs 0 upfront hota hai, 10% rev-share ke saath, aur recurring technical costs platform sambhalta hai.

Hum dekhte hain ki jo educators apna paisa samajhdari se lagate hain, woh teen cheezein karte hain: woh dev quote ko poora cost nahi maante, woh recurring server aur maintenance ko pehle se budget me rakhte hain, aur woh discovery ke kharche ko cost calculation me shaamil karte hain. Pattern saaf hai:

  • Headline number nahi, total cost of ownership dekho, server aur maintenance sameth.
  • App ek ongoing service hai, ek-baar ka kharcha nahi, isliye cost model bhi ongoing rakho.
  • Discovery ka kharcha jodo, kyunki khaali app ka koi value nahi.
  • Tooling par lakhs mat lagao, woh ab Rs 0 me milta hai; paisa wahan lagao jo scarce hai.

Aapko apne app par kal nahi, aaj shuru karna ho sakta hai, bina ek rupaya upfront diye. Mobile lijiye, studio.allcoaching.in par jaayein, 60 second me branded app live ho jaayega, aur server, maintenance aur scaling ki tension platform ki ho jaayegi, aapki nahi.

Glossary

Key terms,
iss guide ke.

Term

Development Cost

App ko design aur code karne ka ek-baar ka kharcha, jo vendor headline number ki tarah dikhata hai. Yeh poora cost nahi hai, kyunki server aur maintenance recurring rehte hain.

Term

Server / Hosting Cost

App ko chalaane ka recurring kharcha, jaise cloud hosting, storage, database aur CDN. Yeh students aur video ke saath badhta hai, isliye success ke saath bill bhi badhta hai.

Term

Bandwidth Cost

Data delivery ka per-GB kharcha, jo har video view aur download ke saath lagta hai. Video-heavy coaching apps me yeh sabse tezi se badhne wala cost hai.

Term

Maintenance Cost

App ko chalu aur secure rakhne ka ongoing kharcha, jaise bug-fixes, OS-compatibility updates aur security patches. Aam taur par dev cost ka ek hissa har saal.

Term

White-Label App

Vendor ka ready-made app jise aapke brand me rebrand karke diya jaata hai. Setup fee plus recurring subscription plus lock-in iska asli cost banate hain.

Term

Payment Gateway Fee

Har transaction par liya jaane wala percentage (UPI ya card), aam taur par 1.5 se 3 percent. Yeh recurring cost hai jo har payment ke saath lagta hai.

Term

Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

App ka poora multi-year cost, sirf build quote nahi balki server, maintenance, fees, lock-in aur distribution sameth. Asli decision TCO par hona chahiye, headline number par nahi.

Term

Revenue-Share Model

Monetization model jaha platform tabhi commission leta hai jab educator paisa kamaata hai, no upfront ya server cost. AllCoaching par 10% rev-share on paid earnings, educator 90% rakhta hai, daily payout.
