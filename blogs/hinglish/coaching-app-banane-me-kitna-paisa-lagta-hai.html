The reframe

Asli sawal 'kitna paisa' nahi,
'kis route se' hai.

Coaching app ka kharcha is par depend karta hai ki aap kis raaste se banate hain: freelancer ya agency se custom build, ya ek ready platform jahan app, website aur payments pehle se bane hote hain. Har route ka pehla bill alag hai, aur har route me baad ke kharche bhi hain.

The headline number

Dev cost,
app ka headline number.

Custom app ka quote features par depend karta hai: login, video player, tests, payments, live class, admin panel, Android aur iOS. Do quotes compare karne se pehle likhit me poochhiye ki kaun se features shamil hain, source code kiske paas rahega, aur launch ke baad bug fix aur updates ka kharcha kaun uthayega.

The hidden recurring

Server aur video cost,
jo har mahine aata hai.

App banne ke baad usse chalana padta hai: server, database, video storage aur streaming (bandwidth), backups aur security updates. Video coaching me streaming sabse bada recurring kharcha hota hai, kyunki har student har lecture stream karta hai. Students badhenge, yeh bill bhi badhega.

The hidden layers

Chhupe kharche,
jo quote me nahi dikhte.

  • Google Play developer account ($25 ek baar) aur Apple Developer Program ($99 har saal).
  • Payment gateway charges har student payment par.
  • Android aur iOS updates jab naye versions aate hain.
  • Video piracy se bachav (DRM, watermarking).
  • Domain, email aur website.
  • Developer ka time har naye feature ya bug ke liye.

The real math

Teen routes
ka comparison.

  • Freelancer: upfront sasta ho sakta hai, par maintenance, updates aur security aksar aapke upar aati hai.
  • Agency ya white-label vendor: zyada complete, par setup fee, saalana fee aur kabhi fee per sale bhi; likhit me sab poochhiye.
  • Ready platform: app, website, hosting aur payments ek plan me; developer nahi chahiye, par aap platform ke features ke andar kaam karte hain.

The myth

'Ek baar ka kharcha'
ek myth kyun hai.

Koi bhi app ek baar banakar hamesha nahi chalta. Server ka bill har mahine aata hai, Apple ki fee har saal, aur Android aur iOS ke naye versions ke saath app update karna padta hai. Isliye quote ke saath pehle saal aur doosre saal ka chalane ka kharcha bhi likhwaiye.

The biggest hidden cost

Asli chhupa cost:
students kaha se?

App ban jaane se students nahi aate. YouTube, Instagram, Telegram ya WhatsApp par regular content, purane students ke referrals, aur marketplace listing — yeh sab mehnat aur time maangte hain.
