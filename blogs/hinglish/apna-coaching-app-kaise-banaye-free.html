Teesra jawab — vague claim ki "koi no-code platform try karo" — kaunsa, kis tarah, kiske jaisa, yeh kabhi nahi bataya jaata.

Iss guide me hum teeno paths ko honestly compare karenge, aur asli sawal jo aapko puchhna chahiye woh propose karenge. Section 02

Path 1 · DIY —
the 6-month timesink.

DIY route ka pitch glamorous hota hai — "apni khud ki app banwao, koi commission nahi, brand 100% aapka". Reality kuch aur hai. Solo educator jo Flutter ya React Native developer hire karke app banwata hai uska 9 me se 8 baar yeh outcome hota hai: app 4-9 mahine baad bani, launch hui, lekin marketing layer kabhi solve nahi hua, aur first 100 paid students aane me 1-2 saal lag gaye. Tab tak educator ne ₹3-15 lakh kharch kar diya hota hai aur energy bhi.

DIY ka honest cost breakdown teen layers me hota hai:

  • Developer hire — ₹50K se ₹2L/month × 4-6 months. Full-stack Flutter/React Native developer, aur ek backend developer (Node.js/Python). Solo freelancer cheaper hai par delivery slow aur bug-heavy. Agency ki team faster lekin 2-3x mahanga.
  • Designer + UX — ₹40K se ₹1.5L total. Figma designs, mobile + web UI/UX. Bina iske app "homemade" lagti hai aur students trust nahi karte. Skipping this step = 50% conversion drop.
  • Infrastructure — ₹15K se ₹50K/month recurring. AWS/GCP hosting, CDN for videos (BunnyCDN/Cloudflare), Razorpay account, SMS gateway, email service. Year-1 me total ₹1.8-6 lakh sirf infrastructure pe.

Yeh sab milkar ₹3-15 lakh upfront — aur abhi tak app sirf ban rahi hai. Launch hone ke baad ka marketing budget alag hai — Meta ads, Google ads, influencer marketing, content creation — yeh saari layers educator ke own time + cash se chalengi. Solo educator ke liye yeh sustainable nahi hai. Aur jab app finally chalti hai, tab tak market me 5 naye competitors aa chuke hote hain.

Question Often Asked

Kya Flutter seekhke main khud apni coaching app bana sakta hu — bina developer hire kiye?

Theoretically yes — agar aapke pass 6-12 months full-time hain aur software engineering background hai. Practically no — aap educator hain, developer nahi. Flutter seekhna chhota nahi hai (40-60 hours just basics), aur production-grade coaching app ke liye state management, video streaming, push notifications, in-app payments, analytics — yeh sab ek-ek karke crack karne me 12+ months chahiye. Yeh 12 mahine teaching ke nahi hain. Educator ka opportunity cost — student ko teach karne ke 12 mahine + sambandhit ₹4-12 lakh missed revenue — iss route ka asli hidden cost hai jo kabhi calculate nahi hota.

DIY ke liye sirf ek scenario justified hai — aap large coaching institute hain (5,000+ students), aapke pass dedicated tech team hai, aur aapko absolute customization chahiye. Solo educator ya 50-500 student institutes ke liye yeh route economic suicide hai. White-label coaching app development cost India guide me iss math ka line-by-line breakdown hai.

"Mai khud app banwa lunga" wala thought 99% educators ka phase-1 dream hota hai. Aur 99% educators ka phase-1 grave bhi yahi hota hai.

Glossary

Key terms —
iss guide ke.

Term

Coaching App

Mobile + web platform jaha educator apne students ko courses, live classes, tests, aur study material deliver karta hai. Modern coaching apps me payment gateway, student CRM, video DRM, aur discovery layer integrated hote hain — sirf "video upload" platform nahi.

Term

White-Label SaaS (Coaching)

SaaS platform jaha educator apne logo + brand name pe ek pre-built coaching app ka access leta hai. Advertised price ₹X,XXX/month hoti hai, lekin Year-1 hidden costs (migration, domain, gateway, DRM, marketing) ₹4–11 lakh tak ja sakti hain. Classplus, Teachmint type platforms iss category me aate hain.

Term

Revenue Share Model

Educator paisa kamaata hai tabhi platform commission leta hai (typically 10–20%). No upfront fee, no monthly subscription. AllCoaching me 10% rev-share on paid earnings only — educator 90% rakhta hai. Yeh model educator aur platform ke incentives align karta hai.

Term

Video DRM (Anti-Piracy)

Digital Rights Management — encrypted video streaming (HLS+AES) + forensic watermarking + device-binding + screen-record blocking. Coaching apps me critical layer hai kyunki recorded lectures asset hain jo easily pirated ho jaate hain agar plain MP4 hosting kiya jaaye. Standalone DRM ₹20-60K/year; AllCoaching me included.

Term

Payment Gateway (Coaching)

Software layer jo student se UPI/card/net-banking/EMI me payment accept karta hai aur educator ke bank account me transfer karta hai. India me Razorpay aur Cashfree dominant hain. Standalone integration me 1–3% transaction fee + setup chahiye; AllCoaching me built-in, daily payouts.

Term

Student CRM (Coaching)

Tooling for tracking student attendance, fee payment status, course progress, communication history, aur engagement signals. 20+ active students par WhatsApp + Excel breakdown ho jaata hai — Excel formulas tut-te hain, WhatsApp groups overflow karte hain. AllCoaching free tier me built-in CRM milta hai.

Term

Educator Marketplace

Multi-educator platform jaha har educator ka apna branded studio hota hai aur saare studios ek shared discovery surface (AI-matched recommendations) par list hote hain. AllCoaching India ki first AI-driven educator marketplace hai — combining the branding advantages of white-label apps with the discovery advantages of platform marketplaces.

Term

Marketplace Listing (AllCoaching)

Educator ka discoverable profile inside AllCoaching ki AI-driven multi-educator marketplace. AI recommendation engine educator ka content student search query (exam, subject, language, level, location) se match karke organic enrolment generate karta hai — paid Meta/Google ads ka structural alternative.
