aur 3 honest paths.

"Apna coaching app kaise banaye free?" — yeh sawal bahut se Indian educators Google par poochhte hain. Aur sawal galat nahi hai. Galat sirf yeh hai ki jab aap iska answer dhundhte hain, aapko teen tarah ke jawab milte hain, aur teeno me se ek bhi poori honesty se nahi diya gaya.

Pehla jawab — "Flutter seekh lo, app banao". Yeh technically possible hai, lekin "free" nahi hai. Aap apne mahine aur developer ka kharcha khud daalenge. Doosra jawab — "Classplus / Teachmint type ka white-label SaaS le lo". Inka plan paid hota hai, aur GST, sales fee ya renewal jaise kharche signup ke time saaf nahi dikhte. Teesra jawab — vague claim ki "koi no-code platform try karo" — kaunsa, kis tarah, kiske jaisa, yeh kabhi nahi bataya jaata.

Iss guide me hum teeno paths ko honestly compare karenge, aur asli sawal jo aapko puchhna chahiye woh propose karenge. Yeh sawal hai — "kis architecture me kaam karu jis se mera coaching business chale aur technology mera bottleneck na bane?" Kyunki jab aap iss sawal ka answer sahi chunte hain, to kam risk me shuru karne ka raasta (free trial, chhota published plan) apne aap mil jaata hai.

Section 02

Path 1 · DIY —
the 6-month timesink.

DIY route ka pitch glamorous hota hai — "apni khud ki app banwao, koi commission nahi, brand 100% aapka". Reality kuch aur hai. Solo educator jo Flutter ya React Native developer hire karke app banwata hai, uske saath aksar yeh hota hai: app mahino baad bani, launch hui, lekin marketing ka sawal waise hi khada raha. Tab tak kaafi paisa aur energy lag chuki hoti hai.

DIY ka honest cost breakdown teen layers me hota hai:

  • Developer hire — ₹50K se ₹2L/month × 4-6 months. Full-stack Flutter/React Native developer, aur ek backend developer (Node.js/Python). Solo freelancer cheaper hai par delivery slow aur bug-heavy. Agency ki team faster lekin 2-3x mahanga.
  • Designer + UX — ₹40K se ₹1.5L total. Figma designs, mobile + web UI/UX. Bina iske app "homemade" lagti hai aur students trust nahi karte. Is step ko chhodne se app kam bharosemand lagti hai.
  • Infrastructure — ₹15K se ₹50K/month recurring. AWS/GCP hosting, CDN for videos (BunnyCDN/Cloudflare), Razorpay account, SMS gateway, email service. Year-1 me total ₹1.8-6 lakh sirf infrastructure pe.

Yeh sab milkar kaafi bada upfront kharcha hai (apne quotes zaroor lijiye) — aur abhi tak app sirf ban rahi hai. Launch hone ke baad ka marketing budget alag hai — Meta ads, Google ads, influencer marketing, content creation — yeh saari layers educator ke own time + cash se chalengi. Solo educator ke liye yeh sustainable nahi hai. Aur jab app finally chalti hai, tab tak market me 5 naye competitors aa chuke hote hain.

Question Often Asked

Kya Flutter seekhke main khud apni coaching app bana sakta hu — bina developer hire kiye?

Theoretically yes — agar aapke pass 6-12 months full-time hain aur software engineering background hai. Practically no — aap educator hain, developer nahi. Flutter seekhna chhota nahi hai (40-60 hours just basics), aur production-grade coaching app ke liye state management, video streaming, push notifications, in-app payments, analytics — yeh sab ek-ek karke crack karne me 12+ months chahiye. Yeh 12 mahine teaching ke nahi hain. Educator ka opportunity cost — teaching ke 12 mahine — iss route ka asli hidden cost hai jo kabhi calculate nahi hota.

DIY ke liye sirf ek scenario justified hai — aap large coaching institute hain (5,000+ students), aapke pass dedicated tech team hai, aur aapko absolute customization chahiye. Solo educator ya 50-500 student institutes ke liye yeh route aam taur par bahut mehenga aur dheema padta hai. White-label coaching app development cost India guide me iss math ka line-by-line breakdown hai.

"Mai khud app banwa lunga" wala thought bahut se educators ka pehla sapna hota hai. Aur aksar wahi sabse mehenga mod bhi ban jaata hai.

  • White-label ki advertised price ko ignore kiya aur Year-1 real total dekha — phir step back kiya.
  • Marketing/discovery ko pehle din se plan kiya — woh actual primary problem hai, baad me bolt-on nahi.

Glossary

Key terms —
iss guide ke.

Term

Coaching App

Mobile + web platform jaha educator apne students ko courses, live classes, tests, aur study material deliver karta hai. Modern coaching apps me payment gateway, student CRM, live classes, aur discovery layer integrated hote hain — sirf "video upload" platform nahi.

Term

White-Label SaaS (Coaching)

SaaS platform jaha educator apne logo + brand name pe ek pre-built coaching app ka access leta hai. Headline price ke upar setup, domain, gateway, DRM, GST aur renewal jaise kharche ho sakte hain, isliye likhit me poochhiye. Classplus, Teachmint type platforms iss category me aate hain.

Term

Revenue Share Model

Educator paisa kamaata hai tabhi platform commission leta hai (typically 10–20%). AllCoaching par setup fee nahi; plan fee alag hai. AllCoaching me 10% rev-share on yearly Starter (0% on monthly plans) on paid earnings only — educator yearly Starter par 90% aur monthly plans par 100% rakhta hai. Yeh model educator aur platform ke incentives align karta hai.

Term

Video DRM (Anti-Piracy)

Digital Rights Management — encrypted video streaming (HLS+AES) + forensic watermarking + device-binding + screen-record blocking. Coaching apps me critical layer hai kyunki recorded lectures asset hain jo easily pirated ho jaate hain agar plain MP4 hosting kiya jaaye. AllCoaching ke har plan me DRM aur watermarking included hain.

Term

Payment Gateway (Coaching)

Software layer jo student se UPI/card/net-banking/EMI me payment accept karta hai aur educator ke bank account me transfer karta hai. India me Razorpay aur Cashfree dominant hain. Gateway ki apni transaction fee lagti hai; AllCoaching me checkout built-in hai (gateway charges alag).

Term

Student CRM (Coaching)

Tooling for tracking student attendance, fee payment status, course progress, communication history, aur engagement signals. 20+ active students par WhatsApp + Excel breakdown ho jaata hai — Excel formulas tut-te hain, WhatsApp groups overflow karte hain. AllCoaching me student records built-in hain.

Term

Educator Marketplace

Multi-educator platform jaha har educator ka apna branded studio hota hai aur courses ek shared marketplace app par bhi list ho sakte hain. AllCoaching ek self-serve LMS hai jisme educator apni branded academy khud banata hai, saath me AllCoaching marketplace app par listing bhi milti hai — combining the branding advantages of white-label apps with an extra discovery channel.

Marketplace Listing (AllCoaching)

Educator ka discoverable profile AllCoaching ke marketplace app me, jaha students exam, subject ya bhasha search karke educators dhoondh sakte hain. Ek extra channel, students ki guarantee nahi.
