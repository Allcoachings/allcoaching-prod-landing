    Aapke institute ke liye kaunsa Classplus alternative fit hai

    Solo tutor ya naya institute (saal ka ₹1 lakh se kam)

    Fixed kharcha kam rakhiye aur jaldi shuru kariye. Kam saal ke price aur 14 din ke trial wala self-serve platform aapko commit karne se pehle banane, test karne aur bechne deta hai. Apna coaching app kaise banaye wali guide pehle steps samjhati hai.

    Badhta hua institute (saal ka ₹1–5 lakh)

    Headline price nahi, apni asli sales par total compare kariye. Yahan apna domain aur apna app maayne rakhne lagte hain, kyunki woh platform ka nahi, aapka brand banate hain. Dekhiye apni coaching ki website kaise banaye.

    Bada institute (saal ka ₹5 lakh se kaafi upar)

    Flat-price plans kharche mein jeet sakte hain. Isko ownership, multi-teacher zaroorat aur khud kitni jaldi badlaav kar sakte ho, inke saath tauliye. Hamari best LMS for coaching institutes guide aur options compare karti hai.

    Zaroori shabd

    Platform fee
    Har sale ka woh percentage jo platform rakhta hai. AllCoaching ka plan ke hisaab se 10%, 7.5% ya 5% hai, sirf jab aap bechte ho.
    White-label app
    Android ya iOS app jis par aapke institute ka naam aur logo hota hai, kisi platform ki technology par bana.
    Custom domain
    Aapka apna web address, jaise youracademy.in, jo platform ke subdomain ki jagah aapki academy website se juda ho.
    Self-serve platform
    Platform jisme aap khud signup aur setup karte ho, bina demo ya sales call ke.
    Parallel run
    Switch ke dauraan purana aur naya platform ek saath chalana, taaki current students ka access bana rahe.
