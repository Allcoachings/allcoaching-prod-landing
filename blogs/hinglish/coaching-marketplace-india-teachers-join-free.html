Section 01

"Free coaching app kaise banayein?" —
sahi sawaal kya hai.

Bahut se teachers poochhte hain "free coaching app kaise banayein". Sahi sawaal thoda alag hai: students kahan se aayenge, aur jo aayenge woh kiske rahenge? App banana ab mushkil nahi — ready platforms par bina coding ban jaata hai. Mushkil hai students tak pahunchna.

Isliye is guide me hum do cheezein alag rakhenge: apni academy (website + app), jo aapka ghar hai; aur marketplace listing, jo ek extra darwaza hai jaha naye students aapko dhoondh sakte hain.

Strategic Definition

Coaching Marketplace

Ek app ya site jaha bahut se educators ke courses list hote hain aur students subject, exam ya bhasha search karke educators dhoondh sakte hain. Educator ke liye yeh ek extra discovery channel hai, students ki guarantee nahi.

· · ·

Section 02 · Reality Check

Apps bahut, students kam —
distribution ki problem.

Pichhle kuch saalon me bahut se educators ne apne apps banaye. Kai apps me students kam hain — content kharab hone ki wajah se nahi, balki isliye ki app khud students nahi laata. Student ko pehle aapke baare me pata hona chahiye: YouTube video, WhatsApp message, Google search, kisi purane student ki salah.

Isliye app banane se pehle distribution ka plan banaiye: kaun se 2–3 channel aap har hafte chalayenge. Marketplace listing un channels me ek aur jod sakti hai.

App banana aur audience banana do alag kaam hain. Pehla kuch clicks ka hai; doosra mahino ki consistent teaching ka.

· · ·

Section 03 · The Structural Distinction

Coaching app vs coaching marketplace —
do alag cheezein.

Coaching app aapka apna surface hai — aapka naam, aapka price, aapke students. Coaching marketplace ek shared jagah hai jaha bahut se educators list hote hain aur students compare karke chunte hain.

  • Apni app: brand aur data aapka; students laana aapka kaam.
  • Marketplace: students pehle se browse kar rahe hote hain; par compare bhi karte hain, aur listing akeli kaafi nahi hoti.
  • Dono saath: academy ghar, listing extra darwaza. AllCoaching par har plan me dono milte hain.

Strategic Definition

Own Academy

Educator ki apni website aur app, apne naam, logo aur domain par, jaha price, students aur data educator ke paas rehte hain.

Section 04 · The Six Pains

Reach vs revenue vs ownership —
teen trade-offs.

Har raasta teen cheezon me trade-off karta hai:

  1. Reach — kitne naye students aap tak pahunch sakte hain.
  2. Revenue — har sale ka kitna hissa aapka rehta hai (platform fee, gateway charges).
  3. Ownership — brand, price aur student data kiske paas hai.

Big EdTech par reach zyada ho sakti hai, ownership kam. Sirf apni app me ownership poori, reach aapke kaam par. Apni academy plus marketplace listing ownership rakhte hue ek extra reach channel jodti hai. AllCoaching par monthly plans par 0% commission (100% aapka) hai, yearly plans par 10%, 7.5% ya 5% fee per sale.

Strategic Definition

Platform Fee

Har sale ka woh percentage jo platform rakhta hai. AllCoaching par monthly plans par 0%, yearly Starter, Growth ya Pro par 10%, 7.5% ya 5%.

Section 05 · The Other Side

Student ki taraf se —
bahut saare apps.

Student ke phone me pehle se bahut saare apps hain. Har naye teacher ka alag app download karna unhe bhaari lagta hai. Isliye do cheezein madad karti hain: ek website jo bina download khul jaaye, aur ek shared marketplace app jaha woh alag-alag educators ko ek jagah dekh sakein. AllCoaching par aapki website, aapka Android app (iOS Pro monthly ya kisi bhi yearly plan par) aur marketplace listing — teeno milte hain.

· · ·

Section 06 · The Other Option

Big EdTech par padhana —
kya trade-off hai.

Bade EdTech platforms par teacher banne ka matlab hai unke brand ke neeche padhana. Isme reach aur ready audience mil sakti hai, aur marketing ka bojh kam hota hai. Trade-off: students platform ke rehte hain, price aur terms platform tay karta hai, aur aapka apna brand dheere banta hai.

Agar aap yeh raasta lete hain, agreement padhiye: kya aap saath me apni academy chala sakte ho, recordings kiske hain, aur revenue share kya hai. Kai educators dono karte hain — platform par padhate hain aur apni academy bhi banate hain.

· · ·

Section 07 · The Missing Layer

Network effects —
kya madad karta hai, kya nahi.

Ek shared marketplace me jitne zyada achhe educators hote hain, utne zyada students wahan browse karne aate hain — yeh network effect hai. Par isse har educator ko automatically students nahi milte. Jo madad karta hai: specific niche, saaf course page, honest reviews, aur aapka apna content (YouTube, notes) jo trust banata hai.

Section 10 · The Decision Matrix

Kis teacher ke liye kaunsa choice —
2026 ka honest verdict.

  • Naye teacher, audience nahi: apni academy trial me banaiye, marketplace listing on rakhiye, aur YouTube/WhatsApp par free content se trust banaiye.
