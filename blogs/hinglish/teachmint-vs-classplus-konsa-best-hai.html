    Teachmint aur Classplus asal mein kya hain

    Teachmint aur Classplus dono education technology companies hain, par 2026 mein unki website alag cheez bechti dikhti hai.

    Teachmint: classroom aur school ke liye

    Teachmint ki website ab Teachmint X se lead karti hai, jise woh "AI-Powered Connected Classroom Device" kehta hai: ek interactive board aur EduAI tools, schools, higher education aur coaching ke liye. Website par koi plan ya price nahi hai; "Book a Demo" se shuruaat hoti hai.[2]

    Achha kya hai: agar aapka institute physical classroom chalata hai aur smart board, teaching tools aur classroom management chahiye, to Teachmint ka focus wahi hai.

    Kya check kariye: jo plan offer ho, woh online courses bechne wale coaching institute ke liye hai ya schools ke liye, aur saal ka total kitna hai.

    Classplus: coaching business ke liye app aur website

    Classplus ka page kehta hai "Get your own brand's teaching app, create your own website & sell courses". Plan cards par Android app, website, admin portal, unlimited recorded courses, live classes, student enrolments, storage aur "A.I. Powered Leads" listed hain.[1]

    Achha kya hai: price published hai, aur coaching ke liye zaroori cheezein ek plan mein listed hain.

    Kya check kariye: pricing page par GST, sales par fee aur renewal price nahi likha, aur plan cards par iOS app listed nahi hai. Yeh sab likhit mein poochhiye.
