Section 02

Hidden costs —
signup se pehle kya poochhein.

Branded app vendors ka headline price aksar plan ka sirf ek hissa hota hai. Isme kuch galat nahi, par signup se pehle har line likhit me poochhiye. Yeh woh lines hain jo Year-1 total badalti hain:

  1. Plan price aur term. Monthly hai ya yearly, aur renewal par price kya hoga.
  2. Setup ya migration fee. Kuch vendors alag se lete hain. AllCoaching par setup fee nahi hai.
  3. Sales par fee. Har sale par percentage. AllCoaching par monthly plans par 0%, yearly par 10%, 7.5% ya 5%.
  4. Payment-gateway charges. Gateway ki apni rate hoti hai; yeh har platform par alag se lagti hai.
  5. App ka kharcha aur account. Branded app kis plan me hai, aur kis developer account par publish hota hai. AllCoaching par app aapke apne Google Play Console / App Store account par jaata hai (Google $25 ek baar, Apple $99 saal, educator deta hai). Store review Google aur Apple karte hain.
  6. Video hosting aur DRM. Kitni storage aur streaming milti hai. AllCoaching par uploaded video plan ke hisaab se (512 GB se 3 TB), YouTube video unlimited, DRM aur watermarking har plan me.
  7. GST. Price me included hai ya alag. AllCoaching ke prices GST se pehle hain (18% GST alag).
  8. Lock-in aur exit. Beech me band karne par kya hota hai, aur data kaise milta hai.

Marketing alag kharcha hai har route par — app students nahi laata. Apna YouTube, WhatsApp aur Instagram hi main raaste rehte hain.
