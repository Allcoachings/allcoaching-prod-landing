Step 03 / 14

Courses

Courses with modules, protected video and completion certificates, built in one studio.

Open the guideTutorial · 3 min

Details, price, and a page that builds itself.

Start with a title students would search for, a one-line short description and a clear description of who the course is for. Set your price, and the course page, its web address and checkout are created for you.

  • Paid or free, with an optional MRP shown struck through
  • Optional 18% GST added on top for the student
  • Banner image at 1280 × 720, or just pick a colour
  • Categories and suggested tags from your profile

Upload your videos. Keep them yours.

Upload a video to AllCoaching and it stays encrypted and watermarked, and plays on every device. You can also send videos to your own YouTube channel as unlisted, or add a link to one already there.

  • DRM-protected player with automatic quality
  • Each student’s name as a watermark while they watch
  • Your logo watermark on every upload
  • Choose whether students can comment or download

Module by module, then a certificate.

Students work through your course one module at a time, with videos, PDFs, tests and live classes inside. When they complete it, they get a certificate with your branding.

  • Certificates issued automatically on completion
  • Your logo and the student’s name, with a unique certificate ID
  • Students can download and share it
  • Modules can open a set number of days after a student enrols
studio.allcoaching.in
How to do it
  1. Create the courseIn Courses, press New course and fill in the title, descriptions, price and banner.
  2. Add modulesIn the Content tab, add modules for each chapter or week, with an opening day if you want one.
  3. Add lessonsAdd videos, PDFs, tests or live classes to each module from your library, or upload new.
  4. Upload videos securelyUpload to AllCoaching for a protected, watermarked player, or use your own YouTube.
  5. Schedule and publishSet release dates in the Schedule tab, then publish and share the course page.

Every document, in one place.

Documents shows how many you have, downloads in the last 30 days and average pages. Filter by status, category or file type, and see each document as a card or in a list.

  • Published, Draft and Hidden filters
  • Categories such as notes, formula sheet, PYQ, cheat sheet and workbook
  • Pages, downloads and file size on every card

What you preview is what the student sees.

Pick a file and the form opens with a live preview. Your logo is on every page and the student’s name runs across it as a watermark, so if a PDF travels, that student’s name goes with it.

  • Logo on every page, added before upload
  • Name watermark with the student’s name on every page
  • Block copy and print stops copying text and printing in the app
  • Let students download again or get a copy by email

Edit any time. Add it to any course.

Press Edit on any card to change the same settings, with pages, size and downloads at the top. Add the document to any module from a course’s Content tab, and see downloads over the last 14 days in a graph.

  • Status: Published, Draft or Hidden
  • In courses shows where the document is used
  • Category, language and tags for organising
  • Downloads graph for the last 14 days

Step 05 / 14

Test series

Exam-pattern tests from your Word or CSV paper, with rank and percentile.

Open the guideTutorial · 2 min

Start from the paper you already have.

Press Build test, give it a title, and drop in your question paper. A Word or CSV file written like the sample brings in the questions, options, answers and explanations on its own. You can also add questions one by one.

  • Word (.docx) or CSV import, with a sample file to copy the format
  • Add or edit any question by hand
  • Type: full-length mock, chapter test, sprint or olympiad
  • Level, language and tags for each test

Set the exam pattern. Marks, minus marks, time.

Choose the time limit, marks for each question and what a wrong answer costs, such as the minus one used in JEE Main and NEET. Decide how many times a student can attempt it, whether explanations show after the test, and whether the leaderboard is public.

  • Timer, marks each and negative marking per test
  • Set how many attempts, or unlimited for practice
  • Explanations shown after the test, on or off
  • Correct answer visible for each question, and the order easy to change

Every attempt scored. Every student ranked.

Results show attempts, the average score and the top score, with a graph of the last 14 days. Below, the leaderboard lists the top three and then every student with rank, score, accuracy, time and percentile, so a mock feels like the real exam.

  • All-India rank and percentile on the leaderboard
  • Filter by 24 hours, 7 days or all time, and export ranks
  • Full-screen anti-cheat lock while the test is running
  • Questions in random order for each student
studio.allcoaching.in
How to do it
  1. Build testOpen Content, then Test series, and press Build test.
  2. Add questionsUpload your Word or CSV question paper, or add questions one at a time.
  3. Set the rulesChoose the time, marks each, negative marking and number of attempts.
  4. Preview and publishSee the test as a student would, with the timer and answers, then publish it in a course.
  5. Read the leaderboardOpen Results to see attempts, scores and each student's rank and percentile.

Live classes

Live classes on YouTube, Zoom or your own private classroom, from one screen.

Open the guideTutorial · 2 min

Three places to teach. One studio screen.

YouTube streams to your own channel with no viewer limit, and the replay stays on your channel. The AllCoaching classroom is private to your students and records the class on its own. Zoom gives a two-way class where students can speak and raise a hand.

  • Unlimited live classes on YouTube Live or Zoom on every plan
  • Connect Zoom once in Integrations; each class gets its meeting made for you
  • AllCoaching classroom: private, recorded and saved with the class
  • Thumbnail made from your title, or upload your own

Your laptop, your phone, or your studio setup.

Into the AllCoaching classroom you can stream three ways: straight from this computer with camera and screen, from your phone by scanning a code, or from OBS or any encoder with a server address and stream key.

  • This computer: camera and screen, straight from the browser
  • My phone: scan a code and stream from its camera
  • OBS or RTMP: server address and stream key for any encoder
  • Recorded automatically, nothing to upload after

Go live now, or schedule it ahead.

Start now to go straight to the camera, or pick a date and time so students see the class listed and waiting. Open it to everyone, which suits filling a new batch, or only to the students of one course.

  • Start now, or Later with a date and time
  • Everyone, or one course only
  • YouTube: connect your channel or paste a stream link from YouTube Studio
  • Set the class length before you go live

Step 07 / 14

Students and batches

Morning, evening and weekend batches of one course, with timetable and attendance.

Open the guideTutorial · 3 min

Every batch belongs to one course.

When you create a batch, the Course field is what links it to a course, and only that course's students can be added to it. Once the batch is made, its course cannot be changed. One course can have many batches, such as Morning, Evening and Weekend.

  • Name, course, first and last class, class days and time
  • Online, in a classroom, or both; teacher and seats
  • The timetable is made for you when you press Create batch
  • Add students lists only those enrolled in the course and not yet in the batch

Take the register in one click.

On the day of the class, press Take attendance, mark everyone present in one click, change whoever was late or absent, and save the register. Attendance comes from the batch and progress comes from the course, and you see both side by side.

  • Present, late or absent for each student
  • Download the register, and see the last ten classes at a glance
  • Students tab: attendance beside lessons finished and last active
  • Timetable: add a topic, cancel a class or add an extra class

Remove from a batch. The course stays.

Remove a student from a batch, or delete the whole batch, and the student is not removed from the course. Their course carries on exactly as before; only that batch's attendance stops counting. The order is always the same: a student takes the course first, then you place them in a batch.

  • Removing a student keeps them enrolled in the course
  • Deleting a batch never touches course enrolments
  • Fixed batch dates in a course's Settings are only the course's own dates, not a batch
  • Timetable, students and attendance all live in Batches
studio.allcoaching.in
How to do it
  1. Create batchOpen Batches, press Create batch, give it a name and pick its course.
  2. Set the scheduleChoose the first class, class days, time, length, mode and seats.
  3. Add studentsTick the course's enrolled students who should study in this batch.
  4. Take attendanceOn class day, mark everyone present, change who was late or absent, and save the register.

  • Add as many options as you need

One question a day. The answer after the vote.

  • Mark the correct option with a tap
  • Add an explanation students see after voting
  • Followers list shows when each person joined, courses taken and amount spent

Step 09 / 14

Checkout and payments

Razorpay checkout by UPI or card, automatic access, and a clear line for every rupee.

Open the guideTutorial · 2 min

Two ways to get paid. Set it up once.

In Settings, Get paid shows two set-ups. Connect your own Razorpay and students pay straight into your Razorpay account, with no payout to wait for. No Razorpay of your own? Add your bank account: AllCoaching collects for you and sends each sale to your bank.

  • Your own Razorpay: you approve the connection on Razorpay’s own page, and AllCoaching never sees your password
  • Until Razorpay has activated your account, AllCoaching collects and pays you out
  • Bank account: add the account number, IFSC and the name on the account
  • Razorpay checks the account with a ₹1 test deposit, then payouts start

Click any order. See where each rupee went.

Transactions lists every order: the student, the course, how they paid and whether access was given. Open one and the full breakdown appears, from what the student paid down to Net to you, with a timeline, the payment ID and the invoice.

  • Subtotal, platform fee, GST on the fee and the payment gateway charge, line by line
  • Course access is given to the student automatically after payment
  • A timeline from order placed to payment received, access given and payout status
  • Payment ID and invoice number on every order

What is on the way, and what has arrived.

Payouts shows your balance and three steps: what students paid, what is on the way to your bank, and what has reached it. Payouts follow Razorpay settlement, usually T+2 days, and instant settlement is available. Withdraw your balance to your bank whenever you like.

  • Where your money went: sales, fee, GST on the fee and gateway charge
  • A list of every payout sent to you
  • Tax details for your PAN and GSTIN
  • Daily, weekly or monthly statements in one PDF for your CA
studio.allcoaching.in
How to do it
  1. Open Get paidIn Settings, choose Get paid and pick your own Razorpay or a bank account.
  2. Connect or verifyApprove the connection on Razorpay’s own page, or add your bank details and let Razorpay verify them with a ₹1 test deposit.
  3. Students payStudents pay by UPI or card at checkout, and the course opens for them on its own.
  4. Check every orderTransactions shows each order with the platform fee, GST and Net to you.
  5. Receive payoutsPayouts follow Razorpay settlement, usually T+2 days, with instant settlement available.

Every code, in one place.

The Discount page shows four numbers at the top: live codes, times used, revenue from codes and what students saved. Below, your codes are kept apart as Active, Scheduled and Expired, with a search.

  • Live codes and times used
  • Revenue from codes and students saved
  • Active, Scheduled and Expired filters
  • Create a code now, or save it as a draft
studio.allcoaching.in
How to do it
